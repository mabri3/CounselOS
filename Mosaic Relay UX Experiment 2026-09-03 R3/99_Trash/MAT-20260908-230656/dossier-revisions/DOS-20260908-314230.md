# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Mosaic Relay is acquiring Harbor Pay, with close targeted for Nov 15 (~10 weeks out). The plan is to migrate 240,000 consumer users and 12,000 business accounts into Mosaic Relay's app on day 1 post-close, with no re-verification of KYC. Harbor holds money transmitter licenses in 38 states; Mosaic Relay holds 41; the combined footprint is 44 states, opening four new markets: California, Iowa, New York, and Florida. Diligence flagged ~40 open alerts and a handful of SARs at Harbor that have not yet been reviewed.

## Decision question

Does any part of the Harbor Pay acquisition and day-1 migration need legal review before the Nov 15 close, or is it "really just a data migration"?

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay is acquiring Harbor Pay; close targeted Nov 15 (~10 weeks out).
- Day-1 post-close migration moves 240k consumer users and 12k business accounts into Mosaic Relay's app with one unified account and one balance.
- Plan is no re-verification; Harbor users were already KYC'd by Harbor.
- Harbor holds money transmitter licenses (MTLs) in 38 states; Mosaic Relay holds 41; combined 44, opening four new markets.
- Harbor's KYC ran through a vendor that shut down in 2024; Mosaic Relay has pass/fail results for everyone but not underlying documents for users onboarded before 2023.
- Harbor's transaction monitoring is a third-party SaaS whose contract terminates at close; plan is to repoint Harbor's transaction feed at Mosaic Relay's engine on day 1.
- Marketing wants a 'Welcome' campaign with a $50 bonus on first transfer, launching close+1.
- Diligence flagged ~40 open alerts and 'a handful' of SARs from the last year at Harbor; none have been read yet.
- Have the ~40 open alerts and the SARs from the last year been reviewed yet, and what do they show? — The ~40 open alerts and the SARs from the last year have not been reviewed yet.
- The four new markets opened by the combined MTL footprint are CA, IA, NY, and FL.

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay is a money transmitter subject to state MTL requirements and BSA/AML obligations in the states where it operates.
- The acquired Harbor accounts and balances transfer to Mosaic Relay as the licensed entity.

## Issues and workstreams

- BSA/AML exposure from unreviewed ~40 open alerts and SARs
- KYC recordkeeping gap for pre-2023 onboarded users
- MTL footprint and state change-of-control notice/approval in CA, IA, NY, FL
- Day-1 transaction monitoring continuity after Harbor SaaS contract terminates
- $50 Welcome bonus characterization and consumer-protection disclosures
- Consumer-protection disclosures for unified-account migration

## Open questions

- Whether the pre-2023 underlying KYC documents can be reconstructed
- Whether the $50 bonus is offered to all migrated users and tied to any condition
- Whether Harbor's and Mosaic Relay's MTL footprints overlap in any state
