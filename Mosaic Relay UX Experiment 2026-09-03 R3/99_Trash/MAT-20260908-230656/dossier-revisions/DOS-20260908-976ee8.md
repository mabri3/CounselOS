# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Acquisition of Harbor Pay by Mosaic Relay with day-1 migration of 240k consumer and 12k business accounts; focus on BSA/AML continuity, MTL licensing, monitoring/SAR review, consumer protection, and marketing bonus compliance.

## Decision question

Does any part of the Harbor Pay acquisition and day-1 migration need legal review before the Nov 15 close, or is it "really just a data migration"?

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay is acquiring Harbor Pay; close targeted Nov 15 (~10 weeks out).
- Day-1 post-close migration moves 240k consumer users and 12k business accounts into Mosaic Relay's app with one unified account and one balance.
- Plan is no re-verification; Harbor users were already KYC'd by Harbor.
- Harbor holds money transmitter licenses (MTLs) in 38 states; Mosaic Relay holds 41; combined 44, opening four new markets.
- Harbor's KYC ran through a vendor that shut down in 2024; Mosaic Relay has pass/fail results for everyone but not underlying documents for users onboarded before 2023.
- Harbor's transaction monitoring is a third-party SaaS whose contract terminates at close; plan is to repoint Harbor's transaction feed at Mosaic Relay's engine on day 1.
- Marketing wants a 'Welcome' campaign with a $50 bonus on first transfer, launching close+1.
- Diligence flagged ~40 open alerts and 'a handful' of SARs from the last year at Harbor; none have been read yet.

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay is a money transmitter subject to state MTL requirements and BSA/AML obligations in the states where it operates.
- The acquired Harbor accounts and balances transfer to Mosaic Relay as the licensed entity.

## Issues and workstreams

- BSA/AML and KYC continuity across the migration (no re-verification, missing underlying KYC documents)
- Money transmitter licensing coverage and state notice/approval for the combined footprint and new markets
- Transaction monitoring continuity and SAR/alerts review before close
- Consumer protection and funds-flow disclosures for the unified account migration
- Marketing 'Welcome' bonus campaign compliance (state money transmission and consumer protection)
- Privacy/data protection for the data migration

## Open questions

- Whether the ~40 open alerts and SARs have been reviewed and what they reveal
- Whether Harbor's MTLs and Mosaic Relay's MTLs overlap or are in different states, and which four new markets open
- Whether state regulators require notice/approval for the acquisition or license transfer
- Whether the $50 bonus is a prohibited inducement under any state money transmission or consumer protection rules
- Whether the missing pre-2023 KYC documents create a recordkeeping/compliance gap that regulators require to be remediated
- Which states are the four new markets, and do Harbor's and Mosaic Relay's MTL footprints overlap?
