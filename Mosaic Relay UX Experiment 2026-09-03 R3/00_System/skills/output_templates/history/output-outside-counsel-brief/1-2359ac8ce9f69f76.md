# Outside Counsel Brief

Prepare one lawyer-owned packet: a focused ask, business goal, essential facts, working conclusion, available support, material uncertainty, and requested scope. Add a short cover email and a selected attachment list only when the lawyer selected those files. Internal context selection is not an outgoing attachment list. Do not send, bundle, or present this as a collaboration workflow.
