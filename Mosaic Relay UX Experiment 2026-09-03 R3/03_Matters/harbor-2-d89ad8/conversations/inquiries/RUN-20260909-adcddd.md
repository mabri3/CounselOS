Now let me give the lawyer the useful first answer.
**Short answer: Yes — this is not "just a data migration."** Several items carry real regulatory and legal exposure that should be triaged before the Nov 15 close. I'd rank them by risk:
**1. The unread open alerts and SARs (highest risk).** ~40 open alerts and "a handful" of SARs from the last year that nobody has read is the single most decision-changing item. If any of those SARs reflect unresolved suspicious activity, you're inheriting BSA/AML exposure at close. This needs review before close, not after — it can affect the deal itself, not just the migration. This is the first thing I'd want answered.
**2. KYC records gap.** You have pass/fail results for everyone but not the underlying documents for pre-2023 users. Under BSA/AML customer identification and recordkeeping rules, relying on a prior acquirer's KYC without the underlying documents is a real question — whether it's a compliance gap depends on how you plan to satisfy your own CIP/recordkeeping obligations for those users. "No re-verification" may not be a safe default for the pre-2023 cohort.
**3. Money transmission licensing for the four new markets.** The combined footprint "opens four new markets" — but that only works if Harbor's MTLs transfer to Mosaic at close without new state approval. State MTL transfer/approval requirements vary, and this is a gating question for whether you can actually serve those markets on day 1.
**4. Monitoring repoint.** Repointing Harbor's transaction feed at your engine on day 1, with the SaaS contract terminating at close, creates a monitoring-continuity risk. If the repoint isn't validated before close, you could have a gap in suspicious-activity monitoring.
**5. The $50 welcome bonus.** Depending on structure (cash incentive vs. referral vs. promotional credit), this can trigger consumer disclosure, funds-flow, or state promotional requirements. Lower risk, but needs a quick check.
**6. Data migration/privacy.** Moving 252k customers' personal data is a data-protection and privacy matter, plus consumer funds-flow disclosures for migrated accounts.
I've recorded the reported facts and flagged these as issues. To prioritize the work, the most decision-changing questions are:
1. **Have the ~40 open alerts and SARs been reviewed, and what did they reveal?** (highest risk — could affect the deal)
2. **For pre-2023 users with no underlying KYC documents, what's the plan to satisfy Mosaic's own BSA/AML obligations?**
3. **For the four new markets, do Harbor's MTLs transfer at close or is new state approval needed?**
4. **How is the $50 welcome bonus structured?**
The first one is the one I'd most want answered — it's the item that could change the deal itself rather than just the migration plan.
