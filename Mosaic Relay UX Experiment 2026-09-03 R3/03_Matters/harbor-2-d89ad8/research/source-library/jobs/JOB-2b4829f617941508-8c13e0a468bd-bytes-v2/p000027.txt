Section 7.03 Authorized Delegate Reporting. 
(a) 
Each licensee shall submit a report of authorized delegates within 45 days 
of the end of the calendar quarter.20 The [Commissioner] is authorized and encouraged to utilize 
NMLS for the submission of the report required by this [Section 7.03(a)] provided that such 
functionality is consistent with the requirements of this [Section 7.03]. 
(b) 
The authorized delegate report shall include, at a minimum, each authorized 
delegate's: 
(1) 
Company legal name; 
(2) 
Taxpayer employer identification number; 
(3) 
Principal provider identifier; 
(4) 
Physical address; 
(5) 
Mailing address; 
(6) 
Any business conducted in other states; 
(7) 
Any fictitious or trade name; 
(8) 
Contact person name, phone number, and email 
(9) 
Start date as licensee’s authorized delegate; 
(10) 
End date acting as licensee’s authorized delegate, if applicable;  
(11) 
[Court orders pursuant to [Section 8.03];] and 
(12) 
Any other information the [Commissioner] reasonably requires with 
respect to the authorized delegate.
