(B)

virtual-currency business activity on behalf of another person; or 
(C) 
provides only to a person otherwise exempt from this [Act] 
virtual currency as one or more enterprise solutions used solely among each other 
and has no agreement or relationship with a person that is an end-user of virtual 
currency;
