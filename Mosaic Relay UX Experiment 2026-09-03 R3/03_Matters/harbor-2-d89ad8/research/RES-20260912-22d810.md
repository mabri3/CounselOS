          ### Money transmission licensing: whether Mosaic Relay can operate under
          the combined 44-state footprint and whether Harbor''s MTLs transfer/survive

          **Current answer**


          Mosaic should not treat Harbor’s money transmitter licenses as assets that
          automatically move to Mosaic through the reported asset purchase. Mosaic
          should not start regulated services in a state unless it has documented
          authority for its complete post-close activity.


          The acquisition close and the service launch can be managed as separate
          milestones. If authority is not established, the present fallback is to
          hold the affected state launch or restrict regulated functions.

          Saved research concludes that the answer must be determined state by state.
          The review must cover Mosaic’s authority, transaction notices or approvals,
          interim operations, outstanding customer obligations, and Harbor’s license
          surrender.
