      The team should not limit its review to provisions called “license transfer.”

      - merger or corporate reorganization;

      - change in legal entity or licensed name;

      - change in responsible individuals;

      - change in locations or agents;

      - assignment of customer obligations;

      - surrender, suspension, or termination of a license; or

      - acquisition of outstanding payment obligations.


      A pure asset sale may not meet a state’s technical definition of “change of
      control.” That does **not** mean no filing is required. The seller’s disposition
      of the business and the buyer’s start of regulated activity can create separate
      requirements.

      approval before the transaction, before the buyer begins business, or before
      the seller surrenders its license. The applicable timing must be verified state
