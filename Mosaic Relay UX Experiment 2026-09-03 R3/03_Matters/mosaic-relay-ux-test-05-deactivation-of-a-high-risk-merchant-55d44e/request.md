# Original Request

Mosaic Relay’s monitoring team wants a standard process to deactivate merchants whose activity creates sanctions, fraud, chargeback, consumer-harm, or money-laundering concerns. The proposed flow would place a temporary restriction on new payments or payouts, preserve relevant records, contact the merchant when legally and operationally appropriate, and either restore access or terminate the relationship. The business goal is to act quickly while reducing wrongful closures and avoiding inconsistent treatment across customers.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, the merchant, the merchant’s sellers and buyers, acquiring partners, processors, identity and fraud vendors, compliance staff, legal staff, and customer support. A restriction may need to occur within minutes of an alert, while investigation and final disposition may take several days. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay may process payments and payouts for customers but is not a bank. Missing facts include the contractual suspension rights, funds currently pending, consumer orders in progress, law-enforcement requests, sanctions matches, notice rules, appeal rights, and procedures for returning or holding funds. Please advise on the legal basis and process for temporary restrictions and termination, required notices, handling of funds and refunds, suspicious-activity confidentiality, sanctions obligations, and records needed to support the decision.
