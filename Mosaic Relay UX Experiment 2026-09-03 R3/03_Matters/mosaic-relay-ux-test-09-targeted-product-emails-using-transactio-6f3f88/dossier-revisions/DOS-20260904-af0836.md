# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Privacy review of targeted product emails using transaction and identity data. Key workstreams: (1) purpose limitation and legal basis for using service data for marketing; (2) consent/unsubscribe and message classification across jurisdictions; (3) profiling and sensitive-data restrictions; (4) customer-vs-Mosaic responsibility and contract terms; (5) retention and suppression/preference controls.

## Decision question

Advise on whether Mosaic Relay may use service data (transaction volume, dispute rates, payout behavior, onboarding status, fraud-risk indicators) to send targeted product emails to merchants and platform administrators, and on the required consent, unsubscribe, profiling/sensitive-data, message-classification, customer-vs-Mosaic responsibility, retention, and suppression/preference controls.

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay wants to send targeted emails to merchants and platform administrators based on transaction volume, dispute rates, payout behavior, onboarding status, and fraud-risk indicators.
- Example use cases: recommending dispute tools to merchants with rising chargebacks; promoting faster onboarding to businesses that have not completed verification.
- Business goal is to increase feature adoption and reduce avoidable operational problems without sending irrelevant messages.
- Messages would be sent weekly, with event-triggered emails after a transaction or account review.
- Mosaic Relay collects payment, onboarding, identity, risk, and operations data to provide its services.

## Assumptions

- Recipients are business contacts (merchants/platform admins), not consumers
- Mosaic Relay is the data controller for its own marketing emails
- Recipients are merchants and platform administrators (business contacts), not consumers.

## Issues and workstreams

- Use of service data for marketing vs. the purpose for which it was collected (purpose limitation)
- Consent vs. legitimate-interest legal basis across jurisdictions
- Profiling and sensitive-data restrictions (GDPR Art. 22, special categories)
- Message classification: transactional/service vs. marketing (CAN-SPAM, PECR, GDPR)
- Allocation of responsibility between Mosaic Relay and its platform customers
- Suppression, unsubscribe, and preference-management controls

## Open questions

- Applicable jurisdictions for recipients (US, UK, EU, other)
- Whether recipients are consumers or business contacts
- Consent status and legal basis for using service data for marketing
- Whether targeting model uses sensitive-data fields (e.g., identity, risk, sanctions data)
- Existing opt-out/unsubscribe and preference controls
- Customer instructions/contract terms governing use of their data for Mosaic marketing
- Retention period for targeting data and suppression lists
