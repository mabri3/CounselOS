# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to send targeted emails to merchants and platform administrators based on transaction volume, dispute rates, payout behavior, onboarding status, and fraud-risk indicators. Examples include recommending dispute tools to merchants with rising chargebacks and promoting faster onboarding to businesses that have not completed verification. The business goal is to increase feature adoption and reduce avoidable operational problems without sending irrelevant messages.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, merchant and platform users, customer administrators, marketing staff, product teams, support teams, email providers, and customers’ end users whose activity may influence targeting. Messages would be sent weekly, with event-triggered emails after a transaction or account review. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay collects payment, onboarding, identity, risk, and operations data to provide its services. Missing facts include consent status, user roles, opt-out controls, applicable jurisdictions, sensitive-data fields in the targeting model, customer instructions, and whether recipients are consumers or business contacts. Please advise on permitted use of service data for marketing, consent and unsubscribe requirements, profiling and sensitive-data restrictions, message classification, customer versus Mosaic Relay responsibility, retention, and required controls for suppression and preference management.
