    questions that differ sharply by jurisdiction and by whether recipients are consumers
    or business contacts.

    / not sure)

    profiling restrictions (Art. 22), and special-category limits; US CAN-SPAM is
    far lighter (opt-out only, no consent). The whole advice structure changes.

    **2. Are the recipients consumers or business contacts?** (business only / consumers
    / mixed / not sure)

    Business-to-business email is treated very differently from consumer marketing
    under PECR/UK GDPR and many EU laws, and under US state laws.

    **3. What''s the current consent / legal-basis position?** (no consent, rely on
    legitimate interest / consent already collected / covered by contract terms /
    not sure)

    Determines whether you can proceed at all on the current data, or need to add
    consent collection.

    **4. Does the targeting model use sensitive-data fields?** (no sensitive fields
    / risk/fraud scores / identity-verification data / not sure)

    If the model keys off sanctions screening, identity documents, or fraud-risk scores,
    that can trigger special-category treatment and profiling restrictions.

    **5. What opt-out/unsubscribe and preference controls exist today?** (none / unsubscribe
    link only / preference center / not sure)

    Gaps here are a near-certain compliance issue regardless of jurisdiction.

    Answer these (step by step or together) and I''ll refine the analysis.'
