# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 01 — Risk-Based Merchant Onboarding for a Marketplace

## Chosen path

Approve a controlled, jurisdiction-gated pilot for general retail/services sellers: allow capped card acceptance after clear automated KYB/KYC, identity, sanctions, and fraud checks; require enhanced review before full activity and before payouts.

## Rationale

The working recommendation is accepted with a stricter payout gate and explicit hard stops. Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold deposits, but cross-border scope and up to $3M monthly volume require careful partner, licensing, and sanctions analysis.

## Recommendation disposition

Modified: Modified to keep payouts off during the limited window and to require explicit country, partner, and vendor validation before launch.

## Conditions

- Confirm country-level coverage and partner licensing allocation before launch
- Verify seller identity, beneficial owners/control persons, and payout destination before payouts
- Block acceptance and payouts for unresolved sanctions potential matches or identity/liveness failures
- Apply risk score >=70/100, document flags, data mismatch, unexpected velocity, or manual concern as enhanced-review triggers
- Implement initial plus weekly and event-driven sanctions rescreening
- Give full terms, funds-flow, privacy, retention, hold, and appeal disclosures
- Retain records for at least six years only with documented minimization and local-law review

## Not decided

- Whether Mosaic Relay is directly subject to BSA/AML or state money-transmission duties
- UK/EU beneficial-owner thresholds and register-check details
- Exact pilot caps, payout timing, vendor locations, and escalation SLAs
- Whether any seller category or marketplace contract adds partner restrictions
