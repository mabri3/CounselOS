# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Regulatory analysis of a risk-based merchant onboarding flow for a marketplace customer, covering KYB/KYC, sanctions, beneficial-owner verification, disclosures, recordkeeping, and activation criteria.

## Decision question

Advise Mosaic Relay on the legal and regulatory requirements for a risk-based merchant onboarding flow for a new marketplace customer: required onboarding data, permissible risk-based shortcuts, beneficial-owner verification standards, sanctions-screening controls, disclosures, recordkeeping, and the legal criteria for activating card acceptance and seller payouts.

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold or take deposits.
- The proposed flow provides a limited approval or review status within minutes, with enhanced review completed within one to three business days when needed.
- The customer would collect legal name, business name, address, tax details, ownership information, government identification, bank/payment destination details, and expected transaction volume.
- Mosaic Relay would run KYB/KYC checks, sanctions screening, identity verification, and fraud-risk scoring before enabling card acceptance and seller payouts.
- The business goal is to reduce onboarding time while allowing low-risk sellers to begin limited activity quickly.

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay acts as a payment facilitator/infrastructure provider rather than holding funds, consistent with the company profile

## Issues and workstreams

- Money transmission licensing exposure depends on jurisdiction and whether Mosaic Relay or the marketplace holds funds
- Beneficial-owner verification scope depends on ownership thresholds and entity types
- Risk-based shortcut (minutes-level approval) must be reconciled with KYB/KYC and sanctions obligations
- Sanctions screening scope and frequency depend on geographic coverage and customer base

## Open questions

- Geographic coverage / jurisdictions where sellers and the marketplace operate
- Seller categories and business types to be onboarded
- Ownership thresholds for beneficial-owner verification
- Expected transaction patterns and volume
- Record retention periods
- Escalation procedures for enhanced review
- Target launch date
