# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans to let a new marketplace customer onboard sellers through an API and hosted onboarding page. The customer would collect legal name, business name, address, tax details, ownership information, government identification, bank or payment destination details, and expected transaction volume. Mosaic Relay would run KYB/KYC checks, sanctions screening, identity verification, and fraud-risk scoring before enabling card acceptance and seller payouts. The business goal is to reduce onboarding time while allowing low-risk sellers to begin limited activity quickly.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, the marketplace customer, individual sellers, business owners, identity-verification vendors, acquiring partners, and payout recipients. The proposed flow would begin when a seller applies, provide a limited approval or review status within minutes, and complete enhanced review within one to three business days when needed. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold deposits. Missing facts include the customer’s seller categories, geographic coverage, ownership thresholds, expected transaction patterns, retention periods, and escalation procedures. Please advise on the required onboarding data, permissible risk-based shortcuts, beneficial-owner verification standards, sanctions-screening controls, disclosures, recordkeeping, and the legal criteria for activating acceptance or payouts.
