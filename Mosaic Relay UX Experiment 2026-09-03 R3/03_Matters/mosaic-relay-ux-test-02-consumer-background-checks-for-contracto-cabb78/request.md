# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to offer a customer a feature that screens contractors before they can receive payouts. The proposed experience would ask contractors for consent, collect identifying information, obtain a background report from a vendor, and return either “eligible,” “manual review,” or “not eligible” to the customer. The business goal is to help platforms reduce fraud and meet internal trust requirements without exposing detailed reports to platform staff who do not need them.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, the platform customer, contractors, background-screening vendors, Mosaic Relay operations staff, and payout recipients. Screening would occur during onboarding and again annually, with urgent rechecks after certain fraud or dispute events. Known facts are that the feature may use criminal-record, identity, sanctions, and address information, but the exact report contents and decision rules are unsettled. Missing facts include the jurisdictions involved, whether the customer or Mosaic Relay is the decision-maker, the source and accuracy process for records, adverse-action procedures, dispute handling, and retention periods. Please advise whether Mosaic Relay may provide this service, which consumer notices and consents are required, who bears compliance duties, how adverse decisions must be handled, and whether any information may be used for payout restrictions.
