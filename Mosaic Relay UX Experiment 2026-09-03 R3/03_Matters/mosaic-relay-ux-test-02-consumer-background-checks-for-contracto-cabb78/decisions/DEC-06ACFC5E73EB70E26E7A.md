# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 02 — Consumer Background Checks for Contractor Payouts

## Chosen path

Proceed with a limited US design phase for contractor screening; do not launch or restrict payouts until the FCRA workflow, state review, data-flow allocation, and dispute controls are verified.

## Rationale

The service appears viable as a controlled design effort, but the record lacks verified authority and key facts. The product must make the platform customer’s FCRA duties operational and must not use screening to withhold completed payouts until state and consumer-protection review is complete.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Confirm contractor states and localities and complete state/fair-chance review
- Confirm CRA status, report contents, and accuracy/reinvestigation controls
- Implement standalone disclosure, written authorization, pre-adverse and final adverse-action notices
- Document data flow and contractually allocate FCRA duties
- Keep sanctions/identity screening separate unless FCRA treatment is accepted
- Do not restrict, delay, or condition payouts for completed work until reviewed
- Set retention, disposal, least-privilege access, and dispute escalation controls

## Not decided

- Whether one authorization covers annual and event-triggered rechecks
- Exact contractor state distribution and local overlays
- Whether Mosaic Relay receives full reports or only status codes
- Exact report fields and decision rules
- Retention period and current dispute procedure
- Whether any payout restriction would affect completed work
