# Research packet — privacy controls for payment operations dashboard

RESEARCH PACKET (unverified legal leads + generated analysis; no external sources supplied or verified).

JURISDICTION MAP (unknown — drives all downstream analysis):
- If US only: no GDPR/UK GDPR; apply state privacy laws (e.g., CCPA/CPRA for California residents), GLBA/FCRA-adjacent considerations for financial data, and card-network/ACH rules. No Art. 22 automated-decision regime; rely on FCRA adverse-action and state AI laws (e.g., Colorado AI Act) as leads.
- If UK/EU: GDPR/UK GDPR apply; Art. 22 automated decision-making, cross-border transfer rules (SCCs, adequacy, TIA), and lawful-basis documentation required.
- If global: layered analysis; highest-common-denominator controls recommended.

CONTROLLER/PROCESSOR (lead): Customer platform is controller for its end-user data where it determines purposes/means; Mosaic Relay is processor for customer-directed dashboard processing (DPA + documented instructions). Mosaic Relay may be independent controller for its own legal/security/fraud duties. Verify per data flow and vendor.

NOTICES (lead): Update customer and individual-facing notices for collection, sources, purposes, categories, recipients, vendors, retention, transfers, profiling, rights, complaint routes.

LAWFUL BASES (lead): Contract for requested services; legal obligation for AML/sanctions/accounting/disputes; legitimate interests for security/fraud after necessity+balancing. Consent not default for core service. Separate sensitive KYC/identity data.

MINIMIZATION/ACCESS/EXPORT (lead): Purpose- and role-limited views; mask identity/KYC/fraud/dispute/support/payout by default; reason-coded time-limited investigation access; restrict exports (fields, date range, approval for bulk, logging, alerts, retention on copies).

AUTOMATED DECISIONS (lead): Inventory fraud scores/profiling; where legal or similarly significant effects, provide notice, meaningful information, human review, challenge, escalation. GDPR Art. 22; FCRA adverse action as US lead.

RETENTION/DELETION (lead): Purpose-specific schedules; separate legal/fraud/accounting/support holds; suspend deletion only for documented exception; deletion/correction/access workflows across Mosaic Relay and vendors.

CROSS-BORDER TRANSFERS (lead): Confirm countries, hosting, sub-processors, transfer mechanisms (SCCs/adequacy), supplementary safeguards before EU/UK data moves.

SECONDARY USE (lead): No customer marketing reuse without separate purpose, notice, lawful basis, and permission.
