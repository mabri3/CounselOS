# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to create a unified operations dashboard that combines transaction details, identity-verification results, KYB/KYC data, fraud scores, dispute records, support notes, and payout information. Authorized customer users would search by person, business, payment, or recipient and export selected records for reconciliation and investigations. The business goal is to reduce fragmented investigations and improve response time, but the proposed design may expose more personal information than each user needs.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, customer administrators, customer operations users, merchants, sellers, payers, recipients, vendors, and Mosaic Relay support and compliance staff. Data would be collected during onboarding and payment activity, displayed throughout the customer relationship, and retained for legal, fraud, accounting, and support purposes. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay works with identity, fraud, acquiring, processing, cloud, and other vendors. Missing facts include the countries of users and data subjects, role-based access rules, export controls, vendor contracts, retention schedules, deletion exceptions, profiling logic, and whether customers may use the dashboard for their own marketing. Please advise on controller and processor roles, privacy notices, lawful bases, data minimization, access and export permissions, automated decision requirements, retention and deletion, cross-border transfers, and restrictions on secondary use.
