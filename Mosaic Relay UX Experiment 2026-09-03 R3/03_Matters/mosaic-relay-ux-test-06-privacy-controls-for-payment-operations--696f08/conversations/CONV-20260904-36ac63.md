    # Mosaic Relay — Privacy Controls for Payment Operations Data

    ## Recommendation (working recommendation, not a recorded decision)

# Mosaic Relay — Privacy Controls for Payment Operations Data
## Recommendation (working recommendation, not a recorded decision)
Do not launch the unified dashboard until Mosaic Relay documents a **data map**, a **role-based access matrix**, **export controls**, and a **controller/processor allocation per data flow and vendor**. Default to the least data needed for reconciliation or investigation; mask identity, KYC/KYB, fraud, dispute, support, and payout fields unless the role and purpose require them. Log searches, views, exports, and administrative changes. Require a reason, narrow date range, and approval for bulk exports, with rate limits and alerts.
## Controller and processor roles
Treat the **customer platform as controller** for its own end-user data where it determines the purposes and means of processing. Treat **Mosaic Relay as processor** for customer-directed dashboard processing, subject to a data processing agreement and documented instructions. Mosaic Relay may be an **independent controller** for its own fraud prevention, security, legal, and compliance duties only to the extent required by applicable law. Confirm the allocation per data flow and per vendor (identity, fraud, acquiring, processing, cloud).
## Privacy notices
Update customer-facing and individual-facing notices to cover: what is collected, sources, purposes, data categories, recipients and vendors, retention, transfers, profiling, individual rights, and complaint routes. Do not use dashboard data for customer marketing unless a separate purpose, lawful basis, notice, and permission support it.
## Lawful bases
Select and document a lawful basis per purpose and jurisdiction:
- **Contract** — requested payment services.
- **Legal obligation** — AML, sanctions, accounting, and dispute duties.
- **Legitimate interests** — security and fraud prevention, after a necessity and balancing review.
- **Consent** should not be the default for core service processing.
Separate sensitive identity/KYC data from general operations views, define field-level access, and prohibit unrestricted person-wide search.
## Data minimization, access, and export permissions
Use purpose- and role-limited views. Mask sensitive fields by default. Require reason-coded, time-limited access for investigations. Restrict exports to selected fields and date ranges, require approval for bulk export, log every search/view/export, alert on unusual volume, and apply retention and deletion to exported copies.
## Automated decision requirements
Inventory fraud scores and risk rules. Where output has **legal or similarly significant effects**, provide required notice and meaningful information, human review, challenge routes, and safeguards. Do not let a score alone deny service or freeze funds without documented review and escalation.
## Retention and deletion
Set purpose-specific retention schedules by record type. Keep legal, fraud, accounting, and support holds separate; suspend deletion only for a documented exception and release it when the reason ends. Define deletion, correction, and access workflows across Mosaic Relay and its vendors.
## Cross-border transfers
Confirm countries, hosting locations, sub-processors, transfer mechanisms, and supplementary safeguards before EU/UK data moves cross-border.
## Restrictions on secondary use
Do not permit customer marketing reuse of dashboard data without a separate purpose, notice, and lawful basis.
