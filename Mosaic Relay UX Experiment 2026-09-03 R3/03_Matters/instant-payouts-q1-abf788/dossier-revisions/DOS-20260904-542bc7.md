# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Can Mosaic Relay ship Instant Payouts for marketplace sellers in Q1, given the proposed onboarding (email + bank account only, full identity deferred until $3,000 cumulative payouts), ACH payouts initiated via bank partner, optional USDC payouts to international sellers via Circuit, nightly batch OFAC screening, W-9-only business onboarding without owner collection, and a public verification progress bar?

## Decision question

Can Mosaic Relay ship Instant Payouts for marketplace sellers in Q1, given the proposed onboarding (email + bank account only, full identity deferred until $3,000 cumulative payouts), ACH payouts initiated via bank partner, optional USDC payouts to international sellers via Circuit, nightly batch OFAC screening, W-9-only business onboarding without owner collection, and a public verification progress bar?

## Material facts

- Sellers onboard with email and bank account only; full identity info is collected only after a seller crosses $3,000 in cumulative payouts.
- Engineering selected the $3,000 cumulative payout threshold because they believe it is 'the BSA threshold.'
- Payouts go out via the bank partner's ACH rails, and Mosaic Relay is the party initiating them.
- International sellers can elect payout in USDC through Circuit (a licensed exchange Mosaic Relay contracts with); the seller provides a wallet address that may be an exchange account or their own wallet.
- OFAC screening runs nightly in batch against the day's payouts; real-time screening adds ~400ms and would undermine the 'instant' promise.
- Business sellers upload a W-9 and Mosaic Relay takes the signer's name; Mosaic Relay is not asking for owners because the UI is already 11 screens.
- Sellers are shown a progress bar reading '$1,240 of $3,000 until full verification.'
- The bank partner has seen the deck.
- What is the legal basis for the $3,000 cumulative payout threshold before collecting full identity? This is the foundation of the onboarding design and appears to rest on a misconception about 'the BSA threshold.' — It was purely an engineering/product choice, no legal basis confirmed

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold deposits; the payout flow routes funds to sellers via the bank partner's ACH rails.
- The bank partner's review of the deck does not constitute a legal determination of Mosaic Relay's own compliance obligations.
- The $3,000 figure in the request is being treated by the requester as a compliance threshold, but its legal basis is unverified.

## Issues and workstreams

- The $3,000 cumulative payout threshold is not a recognized BSA customer-identification threshold; there is no $3,000 BSA CIP trigger. Under BSA/AML, a money transmitter must collect CIP before establishing the account relationship, not after a payout threshold. If Mosaic Relay is a money transmitter, deferring identity collection until $3,000 is likely non-compliant.
- OFAC screening in nightly batch against the day's payouts means payouts are released before screening completes, which risks releasing funds to sanctioned parties. Screening must generally occur before funds are released/blocked.
- Initiating ACH payouts may make Mosaic Relay a money transmitter requiring state money transmission licenses; the bank partner's comfort does not resolve Mosaic Relay's own licensing posture.
- USDC payouts to international sellers, including to self-hosted wallets, raise sanctions, AML, and money transmission licensing questions that differ from fiat ACH payouts.
- Not collecting beneficial owners for business sellers is a KYB/AML gap and may conflict with beneficial-ownership reporting obligations.
- Publicly displaying the $3,000 verification threshold via a progress bar may facilitate evasion and raises funds-flow/consumer disclosure concerns.

## Open questions

- Whether Mosaic Relay holds a money transmission license or relies on the bank partner's license/agent status for initiating ACH payouts.
- Whether the $3,000 deferred-verification threshold has any legal basis or was purely an engineering choice.
- Which jurisdictions the international sellers are located in (affects sanctions, AML, and licensing analysis).
- Whether Circuit's license covers Mosaic Relay's USDC payout activity or whether Mosaic Relay needs its own authorization.
- Whether the bank partner's ACH agreement permits Mosaic Relay to onboard sellers with only email + bank account and no identity verification.
