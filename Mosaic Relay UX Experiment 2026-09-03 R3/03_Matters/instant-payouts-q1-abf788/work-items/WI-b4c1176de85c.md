# Move OFAC screening before payout release

Nightly batch screening against the day's payouts releases funds before screening completes, risking release to sanctioned parties. Design screening to occur before funds are released/blocked, addressing the ~400ms latency concern as a product tradeoff rather than a compliance justification.
