# Redesign identity collection: collect CIP at onboarding, not at $3,000 payout threshold

The $3,000 cumulative payout threshold has no legal basis (confirmed as an engineering/product choice). Under BSA/AML, a money transmitter must collect CIP before establishing the account relationship. Design a compliant onboarding that collects full identity at onboarding rather than deferring to a payout threshold.
