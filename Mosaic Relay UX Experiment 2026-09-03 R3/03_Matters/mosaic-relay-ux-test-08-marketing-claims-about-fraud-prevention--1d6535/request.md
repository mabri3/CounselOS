# Original Request

Mosaic Relay’s marketing team wants to advertise its onboarding, fraud monitoring, sanctions screening, and transaction-risk products as helping customers “stop fraud,” “ensure compliant payments,” and “prevent money laundering.” Sales also wants customer-specific case studies stating that Mosaic Relay reduced losses by a particular percentage. The business goal is to increase adoption among marketplaces and platforms while giving prospects confidence in Mosaic Relay’s controls.

The actors are Mosaic Relay marketing, sales, product, legal, customer references, prospective customers, and vendors whose services support the features. Campaigns would run on the website, in sales presentations, email, webinars, and partner materials beginning next quarter. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay provides infrastructure and hosted tools, relies on acquiring banks, processors, identity and fraud vendors, card networks, and cloud providers, and cannot guarantee perfect results. Missing facts include the evidence for performance claims, approval ownership, customer permissions, required substantiation, limitations by geography or product, and the intended audience. Please advise which claims are misleading or impermissible, what substantiation and qualifiers are required, how to describe third-party dependencies, how to approve case studies, and whether any compliance-related claim creates contractual or regulatory risk.
