    **The single biggest fork in this matter:** Mosaic Relay is a B2B payment-infrastructure
    provider whose direct counterparty is the merchant/platform — not the end consumer.
    Whether this is a merchant-contract/regulatory matter or a consumer-protection
    matter turns entirely on **whose funds are being held**. If the held funds belong
    to underlying sellers or consumers paid through the merchant, the consumer-protection
    and funds-handling analysis is far heavier than if they''re the merchant''s own
    funds.

    To frame the legal work, I need a few facts. Here are the questions in priority
    order:

    3. **Contractual authority** — Does the current merchant agreement already grant

    notice, timing, or appeal terms? (Yes, fully / Partially / No / Need to check)
