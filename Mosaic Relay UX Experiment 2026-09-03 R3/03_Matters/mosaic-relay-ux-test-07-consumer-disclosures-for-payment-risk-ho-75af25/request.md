# Original Request

Mosaic Relay proposes a risk-control feature that temporarily delays a merchant payout when transaction-risk scoring identifies unusual activity, a high dispute risk, or incomplete verification. The merchant would see a status such as “review required,” receive a general explanation, and submit documents through a secure portal. The business goal is to prevent losses while giving legitimate merchants a clear path to resume payouts.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, merchants, sellers, payers, fraud and compliance analysts, risk-scoring vendors, processors, and acquiring partners. Holds could begin automatically within minutes of a transaction and last from several hours to multiple weeks depending on review results. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay provides payout controls and risk scoring but does not hold deposits or operate as a bank. Missing facts include contractual authority, whether funds belong to the merchant or sellers, notice timing, available appeal rights, review standards, interest or fee treatment, and what happens if a merchant cannot provide documents. Please advise on consumer and merchant disclosure language, timing and duration limits, fairness and transparency for automated risk decisions, handling of funds, escalation duties, and records needed to justify a hold.
