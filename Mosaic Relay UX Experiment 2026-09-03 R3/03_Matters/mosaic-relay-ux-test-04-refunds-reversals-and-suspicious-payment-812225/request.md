# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to give merchants a self-service refund tool for card, ACH, and local payment methods. The tool would allow full or partial refunds, route funds back to the original payer where possible, and support manual operations overrides when a merchant account is closed. The business goal is to shorten refund processing time and reduce support requests, but operations has identified cases where a merchant may use refunds to move funds between unrelated cards or recipients.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, merchants, payers, card issuers, acquiring partners, processors, payment networks, fraud teams, and operations staff. Refund requests may arrive immediately after payment or months later, and some payment methods may require separate reversal or return procedures. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay supports payment routing and merchant operations but does not hold customer deposits. Missing facts include refund windows by method, permitted destination rules, limits on manual overrides, transaction-monitoring alerts, suspicious-activity escalation, and treatment of refunds after a payout or account closure. Please advise on refund and reversal obligations, anti-money-laundering indicators, customer disclosures, recordkeeping, controls against misuse, and when Mosaic Relay must delay, reject, or investigate a refund.
