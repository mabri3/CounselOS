# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Mosaic Relay, a payment infrastructure provider, is developing a self-service refund tool for merchants that would process full or partial refunds across card, ACH, and local payment methods. The tool would route funds back to original payers where possible and include manual operations overrides for closed merchant accounts. Counsel is involved because operations has identified potential misuse cases where merchants could exploit refunds to move funds between unrelated cards or recipients, creating AML and regulatory exposure. The tool lacks a defined launch date, and critical operational parameters—including refund windows by payment method and transaction-monitoring coverage—remain undefined.

## Decision question

Should Mosaic Relay launch a merchant self-service refund tool that processes full and partial refunds across card, ACH, and local payment methods with manual operations overrides for closed accounts, and if so, what specific controls, monitoring rules, and disclosure requirements must be implemented to prevent misuse for money laundering while complying with varying refund windows and network rules across jurisdictions?

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay supports payment routing and merchant operations but does not hold customer deposits.
- The proposed tool would allow full or partial refunds across card, ACH, and local payment methods.
- The tool would route refund funds back to the original payer where possible.
- The tool would support manual operations overrides when a merchant account is closed.
- Business goal is to shorten refund processing time and reduce support requests.
- Operations has identified cases where a merchant may use refunds to move funds between unrelated cards or recipients.
- Refund requests may arrive immediately after payment or months later.
- Some payment methods may require separate reversal or return procedures.

- What limits and approvals apply to manual operations overrides, especially for closed accounts? — Overrides require two-person approval and are logged/audited
- Does Mosaic Relay's existing transaction-monitoring cover refunds and reversals, or is this a new monitoring surface? — Not sure
- What is the target launch timing for the tool? — Not yet defined
- Launch jurisdictions — Not yet defined; launch jurisdictions are pending product and regulatory sign-off.
- Refund windows by payment method — Not yet defined; confirm card, ACH, and local-method refund and return windows with each acquiring partner and processor before launch.
- Target launch timing — Not yet defined; no firm launch date has been set.

## Assumptions

- Refund destination is restricted to the original payer's funding source unless an override applies
- Mosaic Relay's refund obligations vary by payment method and network rules

## Issues and workstreams

- AML/financial-crime risk: refunds used to move funds between unrelated cards or recipients (structuring/layering)
- Card network rules on refunds vs. reversals and refund windows
- ACH and local payment return/reversal procedures
- Consumer protection and funds-flow disclosure obligations
- Recordkeeping requirements for refunds and reversals
- Controls against misuse and when to delay, reject, or investigate a refund
- Governance of manual operations overrides, especially for closed accounts
- Whether existing transaction-monitoring covers refunds/reversals (unconfirmed)

## Open questions

- Which specific jurisdictions will the tool launch in, given that refund windows, consumer protection rules, and AML obligations vary significantly across North America, the UK, and EU markets?
- What are the exact refund and return windows for each payment method (card networks, ACH, local payment schemes) per acquiring partner and processor contracts?
- Does Mosaic Relay's existing transaction-monitoring system currently cover refunds and reversals, or does this represent a new monitoring surface requiring separate rules and alerts?
- What specific limits, approval workflows, and audit trails apply to manual operations overrides, particularly for closed merchant accounts?
- How should refunds be treated when requested after a payout has occurred or after a merchant account has been closed?

## Options or working recommendation

Launch the self-service refund tool in phases — card refunds first (original-source-only, no destination-changing overrides), then ACH, then local methods — with launch gates requiring: (1) confirmation that transaction-monitoring covers refunds/reversals (or building that coverage), (2) review of acquiring-partner/processor agreements, (3) confirmation of Mosaic Relay's legal entity/licensing status and funds-flow model, (4) confirmation of per-partner refund windows, and (5) drafting merchant and payer disclosures. Treat any refund to a destination other than the original funding source as a high-risk exception requiring enhanced due diligence, monitoring-alert generation, and post-transaction review — or prohibit it outright. This is a working recommendation, not a durable decision.
