# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans to support merchants that accept cards, ACH, and selected local payment methods from customers in several new countries. The proposed experience would use one global onboarding flow, local payment options at checkout, automated currency conversion, and cross-border payouts to sellers and contractors. The business goal is to help existing customers expand without building separate payment operations for each country.

The actors are Mosaic Relay, merchants, sellers, payers, recipients, acquiring and processing partners, local payment providers, identity and fraud vendors, cloud providers, and local regulators or financial institutions. Product development would begin this quarter, with a pilot in six months and broader release after operational testing. Known facts are that Mosaic Relay is not a bank, does not hold deposits, and works through external payment and infrastructure providers. Missing facts include the target countries, local licensing requirements, funds-flow diagrams, contracting entities, data locations, sanctions rules, consumer cancellation rights, tax obligations, local disclosures, and payout restrictions. Please advise on the required regulatory analysis before launch, licensing or agent issues, cross-border AML/KYC controls, consumer and privacy requirements, marketing limits, contracting structure, and the minimum facts and documentation needed for country approval.
