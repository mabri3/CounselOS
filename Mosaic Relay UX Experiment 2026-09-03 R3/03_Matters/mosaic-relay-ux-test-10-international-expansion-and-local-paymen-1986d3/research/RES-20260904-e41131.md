# What cross-border AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring oblig

## Question

What cross-border AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring obligations apply to cross-border payouts to sellers and contractors?

## Working Analysis

**No external authority retrieved**

## Matter summary
Mosaic Relay, a payment infrastructure provider, is expanding its services to support merchants in several new countries, involving cross-border payouts to sellers and contractors. This expansion requires navigating complex regulatory landscapes, including AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring obligations. Counsel is involved to ensure compliance with these regulations before the product development begins this quarter, with a pilot in six months. The expansion's success hinges on understanding and adhering to the regulatory requirements of each target country.
## Decision question
What specific cross-border AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring obligations must Mosaic Relay comply with for payouts to sellers and contractors in the new target countries, and how will these obligations impact the planned six-month pilot launch?
## Open questions
- Which specific countries are targeted for expansion, and what are their respective AML/KYC, sanctions, and transaction monitoring regulations?
- How will Mosaic Relay's decision to not hold deposits affect its AML/KYC obligations in each target country?
- What are the specific requirements for cross-border payouts to sellers and contractors in each target country, including any restrictions or reporting obligations?
- How will Mosaic Relay ensure compliance with sanctions screening across different jurisdictions, especially given the dynamic nature of sanctions lists?
- What transaction monitoring systems and processes need to be in place to comply with local regulations and detect suspicious activities effectively?
## Likely rules/issues
- **AML/KYC Obligations**: Each target country will have its own set of AML/KYC regulations that Mosaic Relay must comply with. These may include customer due diligence (CDD), enhanced due diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers, and ongoing monitoring requirements.
- **Sanctions Screening**: Mosaic Relay must screen payouts against relevant sanctions lists, which can vary by country and are subject to change. This requires a robust system to ensure compliance and avoid penalties.
- **Transaction Monitoring**: Effective transaction monitoring systems must be in place to detect and report suspicious activities as per local regulations. This includes setting thresholds for transaction amounts and monitoring for unusual patterns.
## Viable paths
- **Partner with Local Entities**: Collaborate with local financial institutions or payment providers that are already compliant with local regulations. This can help navigate the regulatory landscape more effectively.
- **Implement Robust Compliance Systems**: Invest in advanced compliance software that can handle AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring across multiple jurisdictions.
- **Seek Legal Counsel in Each Country**: Engage local legal experts to ensure full compliance with each country's specific regulations and to stay updated on any changes.
## Facts that could change the answer
- The specific countries targeted for expansion, as regulatory requirements vary significantly by jurisdiction.
- Any changes in Mosaic Relay's business model, such as deciding to hold deposits, which would significantly alter its regulatory obligations.
- Updates to international sanctions lists or AML/KYC regulations that could impose new requirements or restrictions.
## Recommended last-mile verification
- Conduct a detailed regulatory analysis for each target country, focusing on AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and transaction monitoring requirements.
- Consult with local legal experts to validate the compliance strategy and ensure all regulatory obligations are met.
- Test the compliance systems and processes in a pilot phase to identify and address any gaps before the broader launch.
