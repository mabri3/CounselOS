# Approved Wording Proposal — "Bank-Level Security" / "Institution-Grade Protection"

Matter: Mosaic Relay UX Test — 07 — Bank-Level Security Marketing Claim
Status: **Proposal for legal review — NOT yet approved.** This is a recommendation for the lawyer's decision, not a recorded decision. Final approval requires the substantiation evidence file, geographic confirmation, and the lawyer's sign-off.

## Recommended position

**Do not approve "institution-grade protection" as a standalone claim.** It reads as fund protection and institution status, which Mosaic Relay cannot support. **"Bank-level security" may be approved only as a qualified security-strength comparison** with the qualifiers and limitations below, or replaced with descriptive claims (preferred).

## Approved wording (conditional on substantiation)

### Option 1 — Qualified "bank-level security" (Path A)
> "Mosaic Relay provides bank-level security for your payment data — AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit, 24/7 monitoring, and [certification]. Mosaic Relay is a payments-infrastructure provider, not a bank, and does not hold deposits or customer funds."

**Required qualifiers (all must appear with the claim):**
- "Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold deposits or customer funds."
- A specific description of what the claim covers (encryption, monitoring, certification).
- A limitations-of-coverage statement (what "bank-level" does and does not protect).

### Option 2 — Descriptive claims (Path B, preferred)
> "AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit. 24/7 security monitoring with [SLA]. [SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 / PCI DSS] certified."

## Prohibited language (never approve)

- **"Institution-grade protection"** — implies fund protection and regulated-institution status; not supportable.
- **"Bank-level protection"** (of funds) — implies fund custody.
- **"Your funds are protected like a bank deposit"** or any FDIC/NCUA-insurance implication.
- **"We are a bank"** or any implication of bank status or regulation.
- **"FDIC insured"** or any deposit-insurance claim.
- Standalone "bank-level" or "institution-grade" without the required qualifier.
- Any claim of a certification not actually held and current.
- Any comparison to a named competitor without comparative-advertising substantiation.
- Any customer logo or testimonial without written endorsement consent.

## Review controls before launch

1. **Substantiation evidence file** completed and verified by Security/Compliance (see workspace).
2. **Audience-perception check** passed (no reader interprets the claim as bank status or fund protection).
3. **Geographic scope** confirmed; if EU/UK reached, local counsel review completed.
4. **Customer logo / competitor comparison** use confirmed and cleared.
5. **Acquiring-bank / processor contracts** reviewed for marketing restrictions on "bank" language.
6. **Approved wording recorded** in the workspace and distributed to Marketing, Sales, and partners; sales enablement trained on prohibited language.
7. **Re-review trigger** — the claim must be re-verified whenever a security control, certification, or monitoring program changes, and at least annually.

## Decision needed from the lawyer

- **Approve Option 1 (qualified "bank-level security")** after substantiation, or
- **Adopt Option 2 (descriptive claims)** as the lower-risk default, or
- **Delay launch** pending the substantiation audit.

This is a recommendation. It becomes a recorded decision only when the lawyer approves it.
