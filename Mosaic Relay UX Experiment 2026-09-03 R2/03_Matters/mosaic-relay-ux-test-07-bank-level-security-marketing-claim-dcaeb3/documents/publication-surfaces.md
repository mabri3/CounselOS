# Publication Surface Inventory

Matter: Mosaic Relay UX Test — 07 — Bank-Level Security Marketing Claim
Purpose: Identify every surface where the "bank-level security" / "institution-grade protection" claim will appear, so legal can scope review, qualifiers, and controls per surface. Each surface has different substantiation, disclosure, and review requirements.

## Confirmed surfaces (from the request)

| # | Surface | Audience | Key considerations |
|---|---------|----------|--------------------|
| 1 | Website | Prospective customers, general public | Highest-visibility surface; persistent claim; must carry full qualifier and limitations disclosure; subject to FTC/state UDAP and, if geo-reached, EU/UK rules |
| 2 | Sales materials | Prospective customers via sales team | Sales scripts and decks must use approved wording; sales team must not ad-lib stronger claims; training/control needed |
| 3 | Partner presentations | Partners, resellers, platforms | B2B audience; partner may republish claims — need contractual control over how partners restate the claim |
| 4 | Paid search ads | Prospective customers (search) | Character-limited; qualifier may not fit in ad copy — may need to rely on landing-page disclosure; verify geo-targeting |

## Potential additional surfaces (to confirm)

| # | Surface | Why it matters |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 5 | Social media / organic posts | If the campaign extends here, short-form claims need the same qualifier discipline |
| 6 | Email / nurture campaigns | Same claim discipline; retention of substantiation |
| 7 | Case studies / customer testimonials | If customer logos or quotes are used, FTC endorsement-guide consent and substantiation apply |
| 8 | Product dashboards / in-product messaging | Internal-facing but may be captured/screenshotted by customers |
| 9 | Press releases / PR | Third-party republication risk; claim may be quoted out of context |

## Controls per surface

- **Website:** Full qualifier + limitations disclosure adjacent to the claim; link to substantiation.
- **Sales materials:** Approved wording only; no ad-libbed escalation; sales enablement training.
- **Partner presentations:** Approved wording; contractual restriction on partner republication.
- **Paid search:** Verify geo-targeting; if qualifier cannot fit in ad copy, ensure landing page carries the disclosure immediately and prominently; consider whether the phrase is safe in short-form at all.

## Open items

- Confirm whether customer logos or testimonials will be used (endorsement consent).
- Confirm whether the campaign compares Mosaic Relay to named competitors (comparative-advertising substantiation).
- Confirm whether the campaign extends beyond the four confirmed surfaces.
- Confirm geographic reach per surface (US-only vs. LatAm/Europe).
