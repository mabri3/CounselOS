## What "bank-level" must mean to be supportable

For the claim to be truthful and non-misleading, "bank-level security" must be substantiated as a security-strength comparison to the baseline practices of regulated financial institutions. That requires evidence that Mosaic Relay's controls **meet or exceed** the security practices of actual banks — not merely that it has "good" security. The documented encryption and monitoring program is a start but is not by itself proof of a bank-comparable standard.

## Material limitation

Mosaic Relay is a payments-infrastructure provider, **not a bank**, and does not hold deposits or operate as a bank. "Bank-level security" describes data/security controls only. It must not be read as, and must not imply, that Mosaic Relay is a bank, is regulated like a bank, or protects customer funds in deposit accounts. Any approved wording must carry a qualifier making this boundary explicit.
