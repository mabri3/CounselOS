  Matter: Mosaic Relay UX Test — 07 — Bank-Level Security Marketing Claim

  Status: Recommendation for the lawyer''s decision. Not a recorded decision.


  ## Recommendation


  **Do not approve "institution-grade protection" as a standalone claim.** It reads
  as fund protection and regulated-institution status, which Mosaic Relay — a payments-infrastructure
  provider that does not hold deposits — cannot support.


  **Approve "bank-level security" only as a qualified security-strength comparison**,
  contingent on:

  1. **Substantiation** — a completed evidence file showing Mosaic Relay''s controls
  meet or exceed the security baseline of actual banks (encryption, monitoring, incident
  response, and any certification), verified current.

  2. **Mandatory qualifier** — "Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold deposits
  or customer funds," plus a specific description of what the claim covers and a limitations-of-coverage
  statement.

  3. **Prohibited language** — no "institution-grade protection," no fund-protection
  or deposit-insurance implication, no standalone "bank-level" without the qualifier,
  no unheld certification, no competitor comparison or customer logo without consent/substantiation.

  4. **Review controls** — audience-perception check, geographic-scope confirmation
  (local review if EU/UK reached), acquiring-bank/processor contract review, approved
  wording recorded and distributed, and a re-verification trigger on any security
  change or annually.


  **Preferred alternative:** Replace the phrases with specific, verifiable descriptive
  claims (e.g., "AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit, 24/7 monitoring, [certification]"),
  which eliminates the implication and substantiation gaps.


  ## Decision point for the lawyer


  Choose among:

  - **Path A** — Approve qualified "bank-level security" after substantiation (medium
  risk).

  - **Path B** — Adopt descriptive claims (low risk; recommended default).

  - **Path C** — Delay launch pending the substantiation audit (low legal risk, high
  business cost).


  This is a recommendation only. It becomes a recorded decision when the lawyer approves

# Lawyer Response — Security Marketing Claims

## 1. Executive answer

**Mosaic Relay will not launch with standalone "bank-level security" or "institution-grade protection" claims.** The recorded decision adopts descriptive, verifiable security claims as the lower-risk launch default. This is a decision, not a recommendation.

Even then it carries net-impression risk.

Launch is **conditional** on completing and verifying the substantiation evidence file, geographic scope review, audience-perception check, partner/processor contract review, and surface-by-surface approval. Nothing in this draft authorizes sending or publishing anything.
