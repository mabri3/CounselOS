# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Determine what exact claims, qualifiers, evidence, review controls, and prohibited language legal should approve for Mosaic Relay's "bank-level security" and "institution-grade protection" marketing campaign launching next month (target 2026-10-03) across website, sales materials, partner presentations, and paid search ads.

## Decision question

Determine what exact claims, qualifiers, evidence, review controls, and prohibited language legal should approve for Mosaic Relay's "bank-level security" and "institution-grade protection" marketing campaign launching next month (target 2026-10-03) across website, sales materials, partner presentations, and paid search ads.

## Material facts

- Marketing wants to advertise Mosaic Relay as providing 'bank-level security' and 'institution-grade protection' for online businesses.
- The campaign would appear on the website, sales materials, partner presentations, and paid search ads.
- The campaign is planned for launch next month (target date 2026-10-03).
- Mosaic Relay is a payments-infrastructure provider, not a bank, and does not hold deposits or operate as a bank.
- Known facts include current encryption, monitoring, incident response, and certification claims.
- Actors include Marketing, Sales, Product, Security, Compliance, customers, and prospective customers.
- Does the campaign intend to convey that Mosaic Relay itself is a bank or that it holds/protects customer funds, or is the 'bank-level security' language meant purely as a security-strength comparison? — Purely a security-strength comparison; no implication of bank status or fund custody

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay's existing encryption, monitoring, incident response, and certification claims are accurate and current as represented
- The campaign is primarily targeted at US audiences unless geographic scope is expanded
- No prior legal approval or approved wording exists for these phrases

## Issues and workstreams

- Whether 'bank-level security' / 'institution-grade protection' implies Mosaic Relay is a bank or protects/holds customer funds, which could be affirmatively misleading and implicate money-transmission characterization
- Whether each phrase is substantiated by competent and reliable evidence (FTC Act s.5 / state UDAP) for the specific audience
- Whether the claim's permissibility varies by geographic audience (US vs. selected LatAm and European markets)
- Whether use of customer logos and comparisons with competitors raise endorsement and comparative-advertising issues
- Whether limitations of coverage (what 'bank-level' does and does not cover) must be disclosed

## Open questions

- Specific substantiating evidence for each phrase (encryption standards, monitoring scope, incident-response metrics, certifications held)
- Geographic audience for the campaign
- Whether customer logos will be used and with what consent
- Whether the campaign compares Mosaic Relay to named competitors
- What limitations of coverage exist that would need disclosure
- What specific certifications and security controls does Mosaic Relay currently hold that would substantiate 'bank-level security'?
