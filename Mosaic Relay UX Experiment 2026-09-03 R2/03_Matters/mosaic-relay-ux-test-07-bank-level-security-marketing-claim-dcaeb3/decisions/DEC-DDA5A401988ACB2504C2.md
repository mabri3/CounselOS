# Adopt descriptive security claims as launch default; no standalone "bank-level security" or "institution-grade protection"

## Chosen path

Option 2 — descriptive, verifiable security claims as the lower-risk launch default. Do not approve standalone "bank-level security" or "institution-grade protection."

## Rationale

Mosaic Relay is a payments-infrastructure provider, not a bank, and does not hold deposits or customer funds. "Institution-grade protection" reads as fund protection and regulated-institution status and cannot be supported. "Bank-level security" is an objective, measurable claim that would require substantiation showing controls meet or exceed the security baseline of actual banks, plus a mandatory non-bank qualifier and limitations-of-coverage disclosure; even then it carries net-impression risk. Descriptive, verifiable claims (encryption, monitoring, certifications actually held) eliminate the implication and substantiation gaps and are the lower-risk default for launch.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Completion and verification of the substantiation evidence file (documented encryption standards, monitoring and incident-response program, and any certification actually held, verified current)
- Geographic scope review confirming the campaign audience and applicable rules (local review if EU/UK reached)
- Audience-perception check confirming the wording reads as a security claim, not bank status or fund custody
- Partner/processor (acquiring-bank) contract review confirming no restriction on the security language
- Surface-by-surface approval of the approved wording across every publication surface
