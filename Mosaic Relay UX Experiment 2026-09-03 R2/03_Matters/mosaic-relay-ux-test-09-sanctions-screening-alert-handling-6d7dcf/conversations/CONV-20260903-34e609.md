    Legal must approve the exact screening events, matching rules, manual-review steps,

    I''m working under (Mosaic Relay is a payments-infrastructure provider coordinating
    regulated partners, not a bank).

    moves the recommendation:

    up to a dollar cap, hold for a fixed window then auto-release, or undecided.)

    cross-border privacy/transfer obligations apply.
