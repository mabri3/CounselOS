# Conditional approval of sanctions screening design; hard gate on weekend cutover

## Chosen path

APPROVE the sanctions screening design conditionally, but DO NOT approve or authorize the weekend cutover until all migration-readiness gates are complete.

## Rationale

The proposed design is sound in principle — hold funds on low-confidence matches until two-person (maker/checker) review clears, auto-block exact matches, fall back to manual screening on vendor outage, and daily vendor-managed list refresh. However, the offshore transfer of review data, unverified vendor list coverage against the global/multi-jurisdiction aspiration, undefined evidence/retention standards, and the aggressive two-month weekend timeline create material exposure that must be resolved before any cutover. Approval is therefore conditional on completing every migration-readiness gate; the cutover date itself is not approved.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Exact screening-event scope defined (onboarding of each actor, defined 'selected payouts', re-screening on daily list refresh)
- Documented matching thresholds with no low-confidence auto-release (all low-confidence matches held until maker/checker clears)
- Maker/checker evidence standard, documentation, and defined escalation path with no auto-release on reviewer unavailability
- Offshore transfer mechanism finalized (adequacy decision, SCCs, or derogation) and vendor DPA/sub-processor terms reviewed
- Least-privilege access restrictions and audit trail for screening and review data
- Customer and partner notices defined for blocked and held payouts
- Record retention schedule for screening and review records
- Independent verification of daily vendor-managed list refresh
- Tested manual fallback with defined capacity and SLA, including re-screening on vendor return
- Pilot/parallel validation of the vendor flow against current manual screening
