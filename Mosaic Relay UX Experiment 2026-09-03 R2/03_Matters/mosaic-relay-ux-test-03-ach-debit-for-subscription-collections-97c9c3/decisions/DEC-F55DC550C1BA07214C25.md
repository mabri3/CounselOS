# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 03 — ACH Debit for Subscription Collections

## Chosen path

Conditional approval for the US ACH subscription-collections pilot; do not launch until the launch gate is met.

## Rationale

The recommendation is followed because NACHA controls and conditional Reg E protections are addressed, but two material facts remain open. A gated approval preserves a 30-day pilot path without approving an unverified operational flow.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Confirm consumer versus business account mix
- Confirm fixed versus variable debit amount
- Prove authorization, notice, cancellation, return, retry, and audit-log controls end to end
- Retain an electronic reproducible authorization record for at least two years

## Not decided

- Final consumer versus business account mix
- Final fixed versus variable debit model
- Exact notice timing and unauthorized-return operational runbook
