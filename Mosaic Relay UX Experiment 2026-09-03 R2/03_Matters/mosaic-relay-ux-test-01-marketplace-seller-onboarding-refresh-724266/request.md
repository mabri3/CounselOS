# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to reduce seller onboarding time for a marketplace customer. The proposed flow collects legal name, business type, tax ID, ownership details, government ID, bank-account information for payouts, and an explanation of the seller’s business. The marketplace and its sellers are the primary actors. Product wants to launch a mobile-first flow in six weeks, with automatic approval for low-risk sellers and manual review for exceptions. Known facts include current KYC/KYB fields, vendor capabilities, and an expected 25% reduction in abandonment. Missing facts include customer geography, seller types, beneficial-owner thresholds, data retention periods, and which party will provide required notices.

Please use the Counsel OS briefing page to identify the main legal issues, assumptions, and decision points. Then use the matter workspace to organize the missing facts, assign operational owners, and record the proposed launch conditions. What exact onboarding data, disclosures, review steps, approval rules, and responsibility split should legal approve before launch?
