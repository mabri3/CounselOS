# Original Request

Risk Operations wants a standard process for suspending a seller and holding pending payouts when fraud indicators, sanctions alerts, identity discrepancies, or unusual transaction activity appear. The seller may receive an automated notice, submit documents, and request review. Risk Operations, Compliance, Customer Support, the marketplace customer, the seller, and payout partners are the actors. Product wants consistent behavior across all customers before the next holiday sales period. Known facts include existing risk scores, manual-review queues, and a proposed seven-day review target. Missing facts include contractual rights, applicable notice limits, funds-flow timing, escalation criteria, false-positive rates, and how to handle legally required reporting.
