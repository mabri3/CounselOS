EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Approve a reason-coded, temporary payout hold and proportionate account restriction for marketplace sellers only. Use immediate automated notice, except where Compliance documents a sanctions or law-enforcement constraint. Treat seven days as a review target, not an automatic termination date. Any extension requires joint Risk Operations and Compliance approval, a reason, evidence, and a next review date.

1. LEGAL DISTINCTIONS
Temporary hold pauses pending payout while facts are checked. Account restriction limits selling or withdrawals during review. Termination/deactivation ends the seller relationship or access and requires stronger contractual and process support. Do not treat a hold or restriction as a termination substitute.

2. TRIGGER AND OWNER MATRIX
Fraud or unusual activity: Risk Operations; preserve risk score, transaction pattern, case notes, and queue history. Sanctions alert: Compliance; preserve screening hit, match disposition, and reporting analysis. Identity discrepancy: Risk Operations with Compliance escalation; preserve KYC records, verification logs, mismatch explanation, and seller response. Law-enforcement request: Compliance; preserve request, legal-hold basis, notice decision, and report. Review beyond seven days: Risk Operations and Compliance jointly; preserve timestamps, delay reason, next review date, and approval. Cleared appeal: Customer Support and Risk Operations; preserve documents, outcome, restoration approval, payout instruction, and notice.

3. NOTICE CONTENT
Tell the seller that a temporary hold or restriction is active; give the non-prejudicial reason category, effective time, affected payouts, document list, secure submission route, review contact, target response time, extension rule, and appeal/review steps. Explain that release may be partial for documented chargebacks or fees. Do not disclose restricted sanctions or law-enforcement details.

4. REVIEW AND DURATION
Open a case at hold creation. Customer Support receives documents; Risk Operations investigates; Compliance handles sanctions, reporting, and extensions. Target completion within seven days. If unresolved, joint approval is required for each extension, with a specific reason and next review date. Set a maximum total duration after jurisdiction and partner rules are confirmed; never allow indefinite holds by default.

5. PAYOUT TREATMENT
Funds sit with a payout/acquiring partner. Confirm partner controls, timing, and instructions. On clearance, release promptly. Partial release or deductions require contractual basis, itemized calculation, notice, and records. Do not net funds without confirming partner and jurisdiction rules.

6. DEACTIVATION AUTHORITY
Only a named authority under approved policy may deactivate. Compliance review is mandatory for sanctions, reporting, or law-enforcement matters. Require contractual basis, documented evidence, written reason that can be disclosed, final payout treatment, appeal route, and audit log.

7. EVIDENCE AND DOCUMENTATION
Store trigger evidence, model/risk score inputs, reviewer identity, timestamps, seller submissions, notices, decisions, escalation reasons, reporting analysis, payout instructions, and restoration/deactivation approvals. Measure false-positive rates, review age, extension count, and outcomes.

8. RECOMMENDATION AND OPEN GAPS
Proceed only after reviewing both agreement sets and a US/LatAm/EU notice-and-hold matrix. Confirm exact launch date, maximum total hold duration, partner funds-flow timing, reporting duties, false-positive rates, seller notice exceptions, and restoration approver.
