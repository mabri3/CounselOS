# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 04 — Payout Hold and Account Deactivation

## Chosen path

Approve a controlled temporary hold and proportionate account restriction for marketplace sellers only; do not approve termination or launch until the listed conditions are met.

## Rationale

The process can reduce fraud risk while preserving review and appeal rights, but contractual authority, jurisdiction limits, reporting duties, partner controls, and total-duration limits remain unresolved. A hold is distinct from restriction and termination.

## Recommendation disposition

Modified: Modified to add launch conditions and separate termination controls.

## Conditions

- Review marketplace customer agreements and Mosaic Relay seller terms
- Complete US/LatAm/EU notice, hold, and reporting matrix
- Confirm payout-partner funds flow, timing, and deduction authority
- Set maximum total hold duration and extension approvals
- Name deactivation authority and restoration approver
- Publish notice, evidence, audit, and appeal workflow

## Not decided

- Exact holiday launch date
- Whether any notice exception beyond sanctions/law-enforcement exists
- False-positive rate baseline
