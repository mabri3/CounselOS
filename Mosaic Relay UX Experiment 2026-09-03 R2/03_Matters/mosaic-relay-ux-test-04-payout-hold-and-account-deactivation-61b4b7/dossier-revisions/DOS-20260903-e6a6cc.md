# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Risk Operations wants a standard process for suspending a marketplace seller and holding pending payouts when fraud indicators, sanctions alerts, identity discrepancies, or unusual transaction activity appear. Seller receives automated notice, may submit documents and request review. Consistent behavior across all customers before the next holiday sales period (no exact launch date). Seven-day review target, extendable on documented escalation. Holds sit with a payout/acquiring partner; release can be partial/with deductions. Applies to marketplace sellers only. Legal must build a jurisdiction notice matrix (US + LatAm + EU) and review both agreement sets for suspension/hold rights.
