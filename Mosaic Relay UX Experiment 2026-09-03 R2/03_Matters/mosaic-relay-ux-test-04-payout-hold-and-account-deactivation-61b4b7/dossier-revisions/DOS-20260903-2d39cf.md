# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Designing a standard seller-suspension and payout-hold process; contractual rights to suspend/hold are unverified and need agreement review.

## Decision question

Design a standard, legally sound process for suspending a seller and holding pending payouts when fraud indicators, sanctions alerts, identity discrepancies, or unusual transaction activity appear, with consistent behavior across all customers before the next holiday sales period.

## Material facts

- Risk Operations wants a standard process for suspending a seller and holding pending payouts when fraud indicators, sanctions alerts, identity discrepancies, or unusual transaction activity appear.
- The seller may receive an automated notice, submit documents, and request review.
- Actors include Risk Operations, Compliance, Customer Support, the marketplace customer, the seller, and payout partners.
- Product wants consistent behavior across all customers before the next holiday sales period.
- Existing risk scores and manual-review queues are in place.
- A proposed seven-day review target is under consideration.
- Do the marketplace customer agreements (and Mosaic Relay's terms with sellers) currently grant the right to suspend a seller and hold pending payouts, or does this process require new contractual rights? — Not sure — need to check the agreements

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay does not hold deposits or operate as a bank; held funds flow through regulated partners.
- The process applies to marketplace sellers across US plus selected LatAm and European markets.

## Issues and workstreams

- Money-transmission characterization of holding pending payouts
- Contractual suspension/hold rights vs. notice obligations
- Consumer-protection and funds-disclosure obligations
- Sanctions/AML reporting interplay with holds

## Open questions

- Whether the marketplace customer agreements and Mosaic Relay's seller terms grant suspension and payout-hold rights (needs agreement review)
- Applicable notice limits and timing for suspension/hold notifications by jurisdiction
- Where held funds sit (Mosaic Relay, payout/acquiring partner, or marketplace customer) and funds-flow timing
- Escalation criteria for when a hold must be escalated
- False-positive rates for the triggering indicators
- How legally required reporting (e.g., SARs) interacts with the hold process
- Which markets must this cover for the holiday launch: US only, US + LatAm, US + LatAm + EU, or all markets?
- Is seven days a firm maximum hold, or a target that can extend on escalation?
- Who should own the decision to escalate or release a hold when the seven-day target is exceeded: Risk Operations, Compliance, or jointly?
