# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Standard process for seller suspension and payout holds triggered by fraud, sanctions, identity, or unusual-activity indicators; scope US + LatAm + EU; seven-day target extendable on escalation; funds held with payout/acquiring partner; legal answer needed before planned launch.

## Decision question

Risk Operations wants a standard process for suspending a seller and holding pending payouts when fraud indicators, sanctions alerts, identity discrepancies, or unusual transaction activity appear. The seller may receive an automated notice, submit documents, and request review. Actors include Risk Operations, Compliance, Customer Support, the marketplace customer, the seller, and payout partners. Product wants consistent behavior across all customers before the next holiday sales period. Legal answer needed before a planned launch. Scope: US + LatAm + EU. Seven-day review is a target, extendable on escalation. Held funds sit with a payout/acquiring partner.
