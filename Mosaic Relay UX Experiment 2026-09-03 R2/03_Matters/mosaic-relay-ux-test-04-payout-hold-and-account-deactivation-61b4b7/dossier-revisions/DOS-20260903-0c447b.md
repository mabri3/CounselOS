# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Standard process for seller suspension and payout holds across US/LatAm/EU before the next holiday sales period; legal must confirm contractual rights, map notice limits, and identify reporting obligations.

## Decision question

Risk Operations wants a standard process for suspending a seller and holding pending payouts when fraud indicators, sanctions alerts, identity discrepancies, or unusual transaction activity appear. Product wants consistent behavior across all customers before the next holiday sales period. Legal must confirm contractual suspension/hold rights, map jurisdiction-specific notice limits across US/LatAm/EU, and identify any reporting obligations the process should trigger.
