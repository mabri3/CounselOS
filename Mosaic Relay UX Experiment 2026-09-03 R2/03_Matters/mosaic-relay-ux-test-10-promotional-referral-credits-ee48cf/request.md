# Original Request

Growth wants to offer customers a referral program. A business customer would receive a $100 platform credit when a referred merchant completes onboarding and processes its first eligible payment. Marketing may promote the offer through email, social media, and partner channels. Referrers, referred merchants, Mosaic Relay, marketing partners, and sales teams are the actors. Product wants to run a three-month test in the United States and Canada.

Known facts include the reward amount, eligibility trigger, draft terms, and fraud controls. Missing facts include tax treatment, regional exclusions, email-consent status, partner compensation, disclosure placement, credit expiration, customer eligibility, and treatment of self-referrals. Please use the Counsel OS briefing page to identify advertising, promotion, privacy, tax, and platform-risk questions. In the matter workspace, organize the terms, campaign variants, approval evidence, and monitoring plan. What exact eligibility rules, disclosures, consent basis, partner terms, reward restrictions, expiration language, fraud responses, and campaign records should legal approve?
