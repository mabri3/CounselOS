# Approve Referral Credit Program (US/Canada three-month test)

## Chosen path

Approve the $100 non-cash platform-fee referral credit program for the US/Canada three-month test (2026-10-03 launch), subject to the three launch blockers: (1) email-consent opt-in records attached before any promotional email; (2) tax counsel confirmation of credit characterization and partner-fee reporting; (3) Product upload of exact terms/fraud-control language for legal approval. Quebec excluded unless a Quebec-specific variant is separately approved.

## Rationale

Program is well-scoped (US/Canada only, non-cash platform-fee credit, written partner terms, documented fraud controls). Approval is conditional on resolving the email-consent basis, tax characterization, and exact-language review before the 2026-10-03 launch.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- No promotional email may launch until consent evidence is attached (CAN-SPAM/CASL)
- Tax counsel confirms the $100 credit is a fee discount (not income) and the $25 partner-fee reporting path
- Product uploads draft terms and fraud-control documents for legal approval of exact language
- Quebec excluded unless Product and Legal approve a Quebec-specific variant
- US and Canada only; no other regions

- None recorded
