# Draft seller term amendments for refunds, fee reversal, and notice

Seller agreements are silent on refunds, fee reversal, and notice. The tool's behavior (prorated fee reversal, negative-balance payout holds, refund timing within 5-7 business days, and seller notices) is not contractually supported. Draft/amend seller terms to cover: (1) seller obligation to fund refunds, (2) prorated platform fee reversal, (3) negative-balance recovery via held payouts, (4) refund timing and notice to sellers, and (5) interaction with disputes/chargebacks referencing the immutable order ledger.
