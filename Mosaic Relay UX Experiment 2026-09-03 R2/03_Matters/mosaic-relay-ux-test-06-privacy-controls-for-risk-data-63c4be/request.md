# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to combine onboarding information, transaction history, device signals, sanctions results, dispute records, and payout behavior to improve fraud and risk scoring. The score would support onboarding, transaction review, payout controls, and account deactivation. Mosaic Relay, its business customers, individual payers, sellers, contractors, risk vendors, and support teams are the actors. Engineering proposes a shared data model and a launch in ten weeks. Known facts include the intended data fields, current vendor contracts, and a plan to retain risk events for seven years. Missing facts include the jurisdictions involved, data-source permissions, automated-decision effects, access roles, deletion exceptions, model explainability, and customer instructions.

Please use the Counsel OS briefing page to distinguish permitted operational use from secondary use and to identify high-impact privacy questions. In the matter workspace, map data flows to product features, record retention assumptions, and track required contract and engineering changes. What exact purposes, notices, consent or opt-out steps, retention limits, access controls, rights workflows, and model-governance controls should legal approve?
