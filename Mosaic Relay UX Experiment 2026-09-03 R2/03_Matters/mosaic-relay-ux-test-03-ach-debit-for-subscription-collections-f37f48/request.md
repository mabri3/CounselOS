# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans to support recurring ACH debits for a software platform that collects monthly subscription fees. A user would enter a bank account, agree to recurring debits, receive confirmation, and be able to cancel through the platform. The platform, its users, Mosaic Relay, the originating bank, and the ACH operator are the actors. Product wants a pilot in 30 days. Known facts include the proposed authorization screen, debit schedule, return handling, and expected transaction volume. Missing facts include the customer’s cancellation process, authorization record format, notice timing, retry logic, consumer versus business account mix, and treatment of unauthorized returns.

Please use the Counsel OS briefing page to summarize the legal issues and the facts that could change the answer. Use the matter workspace to compare the proposed user journey with the actual operations process and to record a launch decision. What exact authorization, recurring-debit, cancellation, notice, return, and recordkeeping requirements should legal approve?
