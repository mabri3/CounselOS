# Recommendation

HOLD the proposed 30-day ACH debit pilot until Product confirms the consumer/business account mix, platform role, fixed or variable amount, cancellation propagation, notice timing, authorization record format and retention, retry policy, and unauthorized-return handling.

If Product confirms a US-only pilot with consumer accounts, approve only after: clear affirmative recurring authorization; amount/frequency/start date and revocation terms; 10-day first and 7-day subsequent advance notice for variable debits; a simple cancellation and stop-payment path with propagation before the next file; no retry after revocation or R07/R10/R29 unauthorized returns; documented Reg E error handling; and a reproducible authorization and event record retained at least 2 years.

This is a working recommendation, not a formal decision. Reassess when Operations provides evidence for each control and Legal verifies current NACHA authority.
