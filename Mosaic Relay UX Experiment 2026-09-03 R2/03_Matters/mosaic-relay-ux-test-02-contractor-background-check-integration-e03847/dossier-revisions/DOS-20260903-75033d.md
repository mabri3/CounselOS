# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

A software platform wants to use Mosaic Relay to pay delivery contractors and proposes adding a background-check vendor to screen contractors for identity, criminal history, sanctions, and work eligibility before the first payout. The platform would receive a “clear,” “review,” or “fail” result, while Mosaic Relay would process identity data and payout instructions. Counsel is involved because the screening workflow raises consumer-reporting, privacy, discrimination, and payments risks, and the target launch is eight weeks away on 2026-10-29. The key unresolved decision is who owns the final screening decision and the adverse-action workflow.

## Decision question

Before the 2026-10-29 launch, should Mosaic Relay permit the platform to use a background-check vendor to screen delivery contractors before first payout, and if so, what exact product behavior, consent language, notices, dispute rights, access controls, and customer contract terms must be required so the platform—not Mosaic Relay—can lawfully make and communicate the final decision?
