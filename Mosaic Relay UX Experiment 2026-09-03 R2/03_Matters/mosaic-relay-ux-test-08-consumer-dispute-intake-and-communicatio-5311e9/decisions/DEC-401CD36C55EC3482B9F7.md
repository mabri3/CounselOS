# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 08 — Consumer Dispute Intake and Communications

## Chosen path

Approve the recommended consumer dispute portal operating model for the six-week initial release, with Tier 3 auto-submit and no silent lapse.

## Rationale

This separates fixed legal controls from marketplace policy choices and gives buyers a complete, traceable path across non-receipt, unauthorized use, duplicate charges, and defective goods.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Confirm merchant-of-record and payment-facilitator roles from contracts
- Verify current Visa and Mastercard rules, reason codes, evidence requirements, and clock calculations
- Complete state-law consumer-protection scope
- Complete accessibility review against WCAG 2.1 AA
- Complete native-speaker English and Spanish notice review
- Verify evidence sharing, redaction, retention, and privacy requirements

## Not decided

- Final merchant-of-record and customer-of-record status
- Supported networks and regions for launch
- Seller-term allocation of dispute, refund, and chargeback responsibility
- Marketplace policy for refund or credit timing after Tier 3 auto-submit
