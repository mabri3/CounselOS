# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

A consumer-facing marketplace asks Mosaic Relay to provide a dispute portal for card purchases. Buyers can report non-receipt, unauthorized use, duplicate charges, or defective goods. The portal collects statements and documents, sends automated messages, and routes cases to the marketplace or seller. Actors: buyers, sellers, marketplace, Mosaic Relay, card networks, issuers, support agents. Product wants an initial release in six weeks (target 2026-10-15). Legal must specify the intake questions, notices, deadlines, evidence rules, status labels, communications, and escalation paths the portal should require, separating legal requirements from customer policy choices, assigning owners, and recording the approved operating model. Merchant-of-record status and network/region support are still being decided.

## Material facts

- A consumer-facing marketplace asks Mosaic Relay to provide a dispute portal for card purchases.
- Proposed dispute categories: non-receipt, unauthorized use, duplicate charges, defective goods.
- The portal would collect statements and documents, send automated messages, and route cases to the marketplace or seller.
- Actors include buyers, sellers, the marketplace, Mosaic Relay, card networks, issuers, and support agents.
- Product wants an initial release in six weeks (target date 2026-10-15).
- Known facts include the proposed categories, evidence upload flow, and response-time dashboard.
- Missing facts include network rules, customer-of-record status, required notices, language access, accessibility, evidence sharing, and escalation when the marketplace does not respond.
- Who is the merchant of record / customer of record for the card transactions the dispute portal will cover? — Not yet determined — this is being decided
- Which card networks and regions must the initial release support? — Not yet determined
- Are the buyers who will use the portal consumers, business purchasers, or both? — Consumers only

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay is not the merchant of record for the card transactions unless stated otherwise; the marketplace likely is, with Mosaic Relay as a payments-infrastructure service provider
- Card transactions are consumer card purchases subject to Visa/Mastercard chargeback rules and U.S. consumer-protection law
- The dispute portal is U.S.-focused for the initial release unless stated otherwise

## Issues and workstreams

- Card network chargeback rules (Visa/Mastercard) and who bears them — depends on merchant-of-record status
- Consumer-protection obligations (e.g., FTC Mail/Telephone Order rules, state consumer laws, EFTA/Reg E for unauthorized use) and required notices
- Whether Mosaic Relay is a service provider to the marketplace or the merchant of record, which determines who must respond to disputes
- Evidence handling, sharing, and retention rules for dispute documentation
- Escalation path when the marketplace or seller does not respond within network deadlines
- Language access and accessibility obligations for consumer-facing dispute communications
- Separating legal requirements from customer policy choices, and assigning owners

## Open questions

- Whether marketplace seller terms and marketplace terms allocate dispute/refund/chargeback responsibility
- Escalation path when the marketplace or seller does not respond
- Languages the portal and notices must support
