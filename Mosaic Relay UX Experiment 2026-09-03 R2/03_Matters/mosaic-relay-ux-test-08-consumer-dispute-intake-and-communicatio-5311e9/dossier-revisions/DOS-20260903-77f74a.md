# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Consumer dispute portal for card purchases; consumers only; initial release in six weeks (2026-10-15). Merchant-of-record, network/region, and seller-terms allocation still being decided.

## Decision question

A consumer-facing marketplace asks Mosaic Relay to provide a dispute portal for card purchases. Buyers (consumers only) could report non-receipt, unauthorized use, duplicate charges, or defective goods. The portal would collect statements and documents, send automated messages, and route cases to the marketplace or seller. Product wants an initial release in six weeks (target 2026-10-15). Legal must specify the exact intake questions, notices, deadlines, evidence rules, status labels, communications, and escalation paths, separating legal requirements from customer policy choices, assigning owners, and recording the approved operating model. Merchant-of-record status, network/region support, and seller-terms allocation are still being decided.

## Assumptions

- The dispute portal is U.S.-focused for the initial release unless stated otherwise
