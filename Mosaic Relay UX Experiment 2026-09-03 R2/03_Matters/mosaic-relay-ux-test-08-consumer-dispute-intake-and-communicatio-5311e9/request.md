# Original Request

A consumer-facing marketplace asks Mosaic Relay to provide a dispute portal for card purchases. Buyers could report non-receipt, unauthorized use, duplicate charges, or defective goods. The portal would collect statements and documents, send automated messages, and route cases to the marketplace or seller. Buyers, sellers, the marketplace, Mosaic Relay, card networks, issuers, and support agents are the actors. Product wants an initial release in six weeks. Known facts include the proposed categories, evidence upload flow, and response-time dashboard. Missing facts include network rules, customer-of-record status, required notices, language access, accessibility, evidence sharing, and escalation when the marketplace does not respond. Please use the Counsel OS briefing page to identify the consumer-protection and payment-network questions. In the matter workspace, separate legal requirements from customer policy choices, assign owners, and record the approved operating model. What exact intake questions, notices, deadlines, evidence rules, status labels, communications, and escalation paths should legal require?
