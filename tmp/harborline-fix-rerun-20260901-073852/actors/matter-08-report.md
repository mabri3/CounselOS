## Findings

- Working well: Matter creation accepted the full request, title, type, priority, and target date with visible controls. Intake clearly surfaced five high-value questions and preserved unknowns without blocking the first pass. The research flow produced a broad, usable packet with a practical fact checklist and ten-merchant guardrail plan even though public retrieval failed. The editor showed saved state. The workflow kept recommendation and durable decision distinct when the decision was entered through the visible register. Approval, local delivery recording, closure, reload persistence, and the decision register all gave visible confirmation. These strengths reduced cognitive load and supported end-to-end completion.
- Product friction: (1) The default intake “Guided” mode required a one-at-a-time interaction, while “Answer a set” was a separate mode that still required five radio selections and four Next clicks. The question panel did not offer a clear “Proceed with assumptions” control after all answers were unknown; using Stop questions was the available path. (2) “Save as work product” for the recommendation visibly said “Current draft saved,” but Overview still said “No working recommendation is saved yet.” This created uncertainty about whether the recommendation artifact or only the canonical draft was saved. (3) Research and drafting progress required long waits with limited phase detail; status remained “Still working” for extended periods. (4) The matter contents showed categories and document counts, but assignment and work-item completion were not exposed as a clear list. (5) The user-facing matter stage remained Ready to send after local delivery recording until closure; the delivery record did not change the banner to a distinct Delivered state.
- Broken behavior: (1) The chat response to the explicit request to record a durable decision said “The durable decision was not recorded in this response” while also stating “The decision is now part of the matter’s record.” Overview immediately afterward correctly showed “No durable decision is recorded for this matter” and exposed the Record decision control. The decision was only reliably recorded through the visible dialog. This is contradictory record-state messaging. (2) The research artifact visibly added “Lawyer” to headings and paragraphs, producing malformed text such as “Cohort SuppressionLawyer,” and exposed “Public research failed” as internal wording in the evidence list. The artifact was saved, but the output was not clean enough for direct work-product use. (3) The Overview repeatedly showed “No working recommendation is saved yet” even though the recommendation content was saved into the current draft and visible in the editor. This is misleading state and weakens record integrity, though the content remained recoverable.
- Browser-control errors: One strict-mode locator error occurred when `getByRole("button", {name: "Next"})` matched both the intake Next button and the Next.js developer-tools button. Recovery used the exact visible label. No work was lost. The visibility capability was unsupported in this run. A long wait reset the browser control session; reconnecting to the same visible matter tab recovered the run.
- Environment failures: Public research retrieval failed visibly; the app saved partial research and reported that no public source was retrieved. In-app browser visibility control was unsupported. The browser control session reset during a long wait and required reconnect. These were kept separate from product findings.
- Blockers: No final product blocker. The recommendation save-state mismatch and malformed research markup caused material uncertainty and reduced polish, but visible workarounds allowed completion. No real external contact or delivery occurred. The matter was visibly Closed and persisted after reload.
