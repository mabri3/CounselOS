# Focused follow-up research plan

The prior answer reached a conditional conclusion because the states, entity types, account structure, and migration mechanics are unknown. The following missing propositions can change what the parties may do at closing, migration, expansion, or promotion.

1. **Whether accounts transferred in an acquisition are “new accounts” under the federal bank CIP rule.** The supplied rule states what a bank must do when a customer opens an account and when formal reliance is allowed, but it does not answer whether a successor bank must treat every acquired account as newly opened. Primary agency guidance could permit continuity for some acquired accounts. That would materially reduce required customer reverification at migration.

2. **Whether a nonbank money transmitter has a federal CIP duty or a different AML framework.** The supplied CIP authority applies to banks. Primary FinCEN authority is needed to avoid applying the bank reliance clause to a nonbank buyer and to identify the correct minimum federal standard. This changes who can accept seller checks and on what basis.

3. **The privacy limits after customer data is disclosed under the Regulation P sale exception.** The supplied text permits a disclosure connected with a sale, but it does not resolve the buyer’s later reuse, its initial or revised notice duties, or promotion. The answer changes if the buyer may use the data only for the sale or inherited relationship and must give notice before a new disclosure practice.

4. **Operative state-law support for license transfer, acquisition, control, and unlicensed promotion.** The supplied evidence is a secondary fifty-state overview. At least one primary state regime can test the stated working rule and show which transaction facts create a pre-closing filing, a new-license need, or a bar on advertising. Because no target states are known, the research will not claim that one state controls the deal. It will identify the exact state-level questions that must be answered.

5. **Whether a licensed seller may continue to serve transferred customers during a delayed migration.** The prior answer treated a transition arrangement as conditional. Primary authority or regulator guidance may show whether the licensee must remain the real provider and retain control of regulated functions. This changes whether closing before account migration is a practical fallback.

Research limits: two focused search batches, no more than four queries per batch, followed by targeted reads of primary sources and directly linked definitions or guidance. Failed searches and unresolved gaps will be recorded.
