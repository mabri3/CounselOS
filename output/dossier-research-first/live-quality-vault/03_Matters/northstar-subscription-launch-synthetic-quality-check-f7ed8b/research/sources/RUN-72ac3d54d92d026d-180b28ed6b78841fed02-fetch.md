    Cal. Code Regs. Tit. 11, § 7051 - Contract Requirements for Service Providers
    and Contractors State Regulations Compare (a) The contract\n\t\t\t\t  required
    by the CCPA for service providers and contractors shall: (1) Prohibit the service
    provider or\n\t\t\t\t  contractor from selling or sharing personal information
    it collects pursuant to\n\t\t\t\t  the written contract with the business. (2)
    Identify the specific business purpose(s)\n\t\t\t\t  for which the service provider
    or contractor is processing personal information\n\t\t\t\t  pursuant to the written
    contract with the business, and specify that the\n\t\t\t\t  business is disclosing
    the personal information to the service provider or\n\t\t\t\t  contractor only
    for the limited and specified business purpose(s) set forth\n\t\t\t\t  within
    the contract. The business purpose(s) shall not be described in generic\n\t\t\t\t
    \ terms, such as referencing the entire contract generally. The description shall\n\t\t\t\t
    \ be specific. (3) Prohibit the\n\t\t\t\t  service provider or contractor from
    retaining, using, or disclosing the\n\t\t\t\t  personal information that it collected
    pursuant to the written contract with\n\t\t\t\t  the business for any purpose
    other than the business purpose(s) specified in\n\t\t\t\t  the contract or as
    otherwise permitted by the CCPA and these\n\t\t\t\t  regulations. (4) Prohibit
    the\n\t\t\t\t  service provider or contractor from retaining, using, or disclosing
    the\n\t\t\t\t  personal information that it collected pursuant to the written
    contract with\n\t\t\t\t  the business for any commercial purpose other than the
    business purpose(s)\n\t\t\t\t  specified in the contract, unless expressly permitted
    by the CCPA or these\n\t\t\t\t  regulations. (5) Prohibit the\n\t\t\t\t  service
    provider or contractor from retaining, using, or disclosing the\n\t\t\t\t  personal
    information that it collected pursuant to the written contract with\n\t\t\t\t
    \ the business outside the direct business relationship between the service\n\t\t\t\t
    \ provider or contractor and the business, unless expressly permitted by the CCPA\n\t\t\t\t
    \ or these regulations. For example, a service provider or contractor shall be\n\t\t\t\t
    \ prohibited from combining or updating personal information that it collected\n\t\t\t\t
    \ pursuant to the written contract with the business with personal information\n\t\t\t\t
    \ that it received from another source or collected from its own interaction with\n\t\t\t\t
    \ the consumer, unless expressly permitted by the CCPA or these\n\t\t\t\t  regulations.
    (6) Require the\n\t\t\t\t  service provider or contractor to comply with all applicable
    sections of the\n\t\t\t\t  CCPA and these regulations, including--with respect
    to the personal information\n\t\t\t\t  that it collected pursuant to the written
    contract with the business--providing\n\t\t\t\t  the same level of privacy protection
    as required of businesses by the CCPA and\n\t\t\t\t  these regulations. For example,
    the contract may require the service provider\n\t\t\t\t  or contractor to cooperate
    with the business in responding to and complying\n\t\t\t\t  with consumers' requests
    made pursuant to the CCPA, and to implement reasonable\n\t\t\t\t  security procedures
    and practices appropriate to the nature of the personal\n\t\t\t\t  information
    to protect the personal information from unauthorized or illegal\n\t\t\t\t  access,
    destruction, use, modification, or disclosure in accordance with Civil\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Code section 1798.81.5 . (7) Grant the business the right to take\n\t\t\t\t
    \ reasonable and appropriate steps to ensure that the service provider or\n\t\t\t\t
    \ contractor uses the personal information that it collected pursuant to the\n\t\t\t\t
    \ written contract with the business in a manner consistent with the business's\n\t\t\t\t
    \ obligations under the CCPA and these regulations. Reasonable and appropriate\n\t\t\t\t
    \ steps may include ongoing manual reviews and automated scans of the service\n\t\t\t\t
    \ provider's system and regular internal or third-party assessments, audits, or\n\t\t\t\t
    \ other technical and operational testing at least once every 12\n\t\t\t\t  months.
    (8) Require the service\n\t\t\t\t  provider or contractor to notify the business
    after it makes a determination\n\t\t\t\t  that it can no longer meet its obligations
    under the CCPA and these\n\t\t\t\t  regulations. (9) Grant the business\n\t\t\t\t
    \ the right, upon notice, to take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and\n\t\t\t\t
    \ remediate the service provider or contractor's unauthorized use of personal\n\t\t\t\t
    \ information. For example, the business may require the service provider or\n\t\t\t\t
    \ contractor to provide documentation that verifies that they no longer retain
    or\n\t\t\t\t  use the personal information of consumers that have made a valid
    request to\n\t\t\t\t  delete with the business. (10) Require the service provider
    or contractor to enable the business to comply\n\t\t\t\t  with consumer requests
    made pursuant to the CCPA or require the business to\n\t\t\t\t  inform the service
    provider or contractor of any consumer request made pursuant\n\t\t\t\t  to the
    CCPA that they must comply with and provide the information necessary\n\t\t\t\t
    \ for the service provider or contractor to comply with the request. (b) A service
    provider or\n\t\t\t\t  contractor that subcontracts with another person in providing
    services to the\n\t\t\t\t  business for whom it is a service provider or contractor
    shall have a contract\n\t\t\t\t  with the subcontractor that complies with the
    CCPA and these regulations,\n\t\t\t\t  including subsection (a). (c) Whether a
    business conducts due diligence of its service providers and\n\t\t\t\t  contractors
    factors into whether the business has reason to believe that a\n\t\t\t\t  service
    provider or contractor is using personal information in violation of\n\t\t\t\t
    \ the CCPA and these regulations. For example, depending on the circumstances,
    a\n\t\t\t\t  business that never enforces the terms of the contract nor exercises
    its rights\n\t\t\t\t  to audit or test the service provider's or contractor's
    systems might not be\n\t\t\t\t  able to rely on the defense that it did not have
    reason to believe that the\n\t\t\t\t  service provider or contractor intends to
    use the personal information in\n\t\t\t\t  violation of the CCPA and these regulations
    at the time the business disclosed\n\t\t\t\t  the personal information to the
    service provider or contractor. Notes Cal. Code\n\t\t\t\t\t Regs. Tit. 11 , §
