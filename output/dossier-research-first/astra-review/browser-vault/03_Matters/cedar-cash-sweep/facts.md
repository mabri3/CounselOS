# Facts

## Verified launch facts

- Customer funds remain owned by the business customer under the approved account terms.
- Deposit-account records identify the custodial or agency relationship.
- The company ledger identifies each customer, each receiving bank, and the customer's allocated amount.
- The sweep administrator sends a bank-level allocation file each business day.
- Customers do not select receiving banks, but they can view the current allocation by bank in the app.
- The disclosed maximum assumes available capacity across eight network banks and can be lower when a customer holds other deposits at the same bank.
- The annual percentage yield is variable and can change after account opening.
- Three prelaunch reconciliation exceptions were resolved the next morning without customer loss.

## Post-launch facts to monitor

- [ ] Reconciliation exception count and age during the first 90 days.
- [ ] Time required to produce customer-level ownership records during a simulated bank failure.
- [ ] Complaints that show confusion about the role of the company, sponsor bank, or network bank.
