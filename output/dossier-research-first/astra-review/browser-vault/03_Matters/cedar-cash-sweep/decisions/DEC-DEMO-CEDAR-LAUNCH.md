# Approve Cedar Cash-Sweep Launch with Disclosure and Reconciliation Conditions

## Chosen path

Launch the eight-bank sweep with qualified insurance language, daily reconciliation, customer-visible allocations, and tested failure operations.

## Rationale

The approved records, account structure, disclosures, and operating controls support a useful customer product without making an unconditional insurance promise.

## Conditions

- Reconcile customer and administrator allocation records each business day.
- Stop new placement into an affected bank for a material unresolved exception.
- Recheck the disclosure before changing the network or maximum advertised coverage.
