# Issue Map

1. **Pass-through requirements.** Do ownership, account-title, disclosure, and recordkeeping facts support deposit insurance passing through to each customer if a network bank fails?
2. **Coverage amount.** Does the advertised maximum account for customer deposits held outside Cedar at the same network banks and for current network capacity?
3. **Entity clarity.** Does the experience clearly state which entities are banks, which entity provides the technology, and when funds are eligible for deposit insurance?
4. **Yield advertising.** Is the variable annual percentage yield current, clear, and shown with material conditions and timing?
5. **Reconciliation.** Can the company produce accurate customer ownership and allocation records each day, investigate differences, and restrict movement when records are uncertain?
6. **Failure operations.** Can the company respond to a bank failure, administrator outage, allocation-file failure, or customer withdrawal during an exception?
7. **Third-party oversight.** Do contracts, testing rights, service levels, business continuity, and data-export duties support the company's customer promise?
