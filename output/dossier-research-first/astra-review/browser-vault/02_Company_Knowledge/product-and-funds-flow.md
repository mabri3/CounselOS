# Product and Funds Flow

DemoCo offers business accounts through a sponsor bank. Customer funds are held by the bank. DemoCo provides the software interface, sub-ledgering, transaction instructions, controls, and customer support. Domestic ACH, domestic wires, and debit-card transactions are supported in the demo profile.
