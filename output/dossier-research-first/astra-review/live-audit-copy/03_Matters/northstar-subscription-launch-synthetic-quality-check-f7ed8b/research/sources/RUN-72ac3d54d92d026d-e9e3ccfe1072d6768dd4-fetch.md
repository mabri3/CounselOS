    State Regulations California Code of Regulations Title 11 - Law Division 6 - California
    Privacy Protection Agency Chapter 1 - California Consumer Privacy Act Regulations
    Article 2 - Required Disclosures to Consumers Cal. Code Regs. Tit. 11, § 7012
    - Notice at Collection of Personal Information Cal. Code Regs. Tit. 11, § 7012
    - Notice at Collection of Personal Information State Regulations Compare (a) The
    purpose of the Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t  is to provide consumers with timely
    notice, at or before the point of\n\t\t\t\t  collection, about the categories
    of personal information to be collected from\n\t\t\t\t  them, the purposes for
    which the personal information is collected or used, and\n\t\t\t\t  whether that
    information is sold or shared, so that consumers have a tool to\n\t\t\t\t  exercise
    meaningful control over the business's use of their personal\n\t\t\t\t  information.
    For example, upon receiving the Notice at Collection, the consumer\n\t\t\t\t  can
    use the information in the notice as a tool to choose whether to engage\n\t\t\t\t
    \ with the business, or to direct the business not to sell or share their\n\t\t\t\t
    \ personal information and to limit the use and disclosure of their sensitive\n\t\t\t\t
    \ personal information. (b) The\n\t\t\t\t  Notice at Collection shall comply with
    section 7003 , subsections (a) and\n\t\t\t\t  (b). (c) The Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t
    \ shall be made readily available where consumers will encounter it at or before\n\t\t\t\t
    \ the point of collection of any personal information. Illustrative examples\n\t\t\t\t
    \ follow. (1) When a business collects\n\t\t\t\t  consumers' personal information
    online, it may post a conspicuous link to the\n\t\t\t\t  notice on the introductory
    page of the business's website and on all webpages\n\t\t\t\t  where personal information
    is collected. (2) When a business collects consumers'\n\t\t\t\t  personal information
    through a webform, it may post a conspicuous link to the\n\t\t\t\t  notice in
    close proximity to the fields in which the consumer inputs their\n\t\t\t\t  personal
    information, or in close proximity to the button by which the consumer\n\t\t\t\t
    \ submits their personal information to the business. (3) When a business collects
    personal\n\t\t\t\t  information through a mobile application, it may provide a
    link to the notice\n\t\t\t\t  on the mobile application's download page and within
    the application, such as\n\t\t\t\t  through the application's settings menu. (4)
    When a business collects consumers'\n\t\t\t\t  personal information offline, it
    may include the notice on printed forms that\n\t\t\t\t  collect personal information,
    provide the consumer with a paper version of the\n\t\t\t\t  notice, or post prominent
    signage directing consumers to where the notice can\n\t\t\t\t  be found online.
    (5) When a\n\t\t\t\t  business collects personal information over the telephone
    or in person, it may\n\t\t\t\t  provide the notice orally. (d) If a business does
    not give the Notice at\n\t\t\t\t  Collection to the consumer at or before the
    point of collection of their\n\t\t\t\t  personal information, the business shall
    not collect personal information from\n\t\t\t\t  the consumer. (e) A business
    shall\n\t\t\t\t  include the following in its Notice at Collection: (1) A list
    of the categories of personal\n\t\t\t\t  information about consumers, including
    categories of sensitive personal\n\t\t\t\t  information, to be collected. Each
    category of personal information shall be\n\t\t\t\t  written in a manner that
    provides consumers a meaningful understanding of the\n\t\t\t\t  information being
    collected. (2) The purpose(s) for which the categories of personal information,
    including\n\t\t\t\t  categories of sensitive personal information, are collected
    and used. (3) Whether each category of personal\n\t\t\t\t  information identified
    in subsection (e)(1) is sold or shared. (4) The length of time the business intends\n\t\t\t\t
    \ to retain each category of personal information identified in subsection\n\t\t\t\t
    \ (e)(1), or if that is not possible, the criteria used to determine the period\n\t\t\t\t
    \ of time it will be retained. (5) If\n\t\t\t\t  the business sells or shares
    personal information, the link to the Notice of\n\t\t\t\t  Right to Opt-out of
    Sale/Sharing, or in the case of offline notices, where the\n\t\t\t\t  webpage
    can be found online. (6) A\n\t\t\t\t  link to the business's privacy policy, or
    in the case of offline notices, where\n\t\t\t\t  the privacy policy can be found
    online. (f) If a business collects personal\n\t\t\t\t  information from a consumer
    online, the Notice at Collection may be given to\n\t\t\t\t  the consumer by providing
    a link that takes the consumer directly to the\n\t\t\t\t  specific section of
    the business's privacy policy that contains the information\n\t\t\t\t  required
    in subsection (e)(1) through (6). Directing the consumer to the\n\t\t\t\t  beginning
    of the privacy policy, or to another section of the privacy policy\n\t\t\t\t  that
    does not contain the required information, so that the consumer is\n\t\t\t\t  required
    to scroll through other information in order to determine the\n\t\t\t\t  categories
    of personal information to be collected and/or whether the business\n\t\t\t\t
    \ sells or shares the personal information collected, does not satisfy this\n\t\t\t\t
    \ standard. (g) Third Parties that\n\t\t\t\t  Control the Collection of Personal
    Information. This subsection shall not\n\t\t\t\t  affect the first party's obligations
    under the CCPA to comply with a consumer's\n\t\t\t\t  request to opt-out of sale/sharing.
    (1) For\n\t\t\t\t  purposes of giving Notice at Collection, more than one business
    may control the\n\t\t\t\t  collection of a consumer's personal information, and
    thus, have an obligation\n\t\t\t\t  to provide a Notice at Collection in accordance
    with the CCPA and these\n\t\t\t\t  regulations. For example, a first party may
    allow another business, acting as a\n\t\t\t\t  third party, to control the collection
    of personal information from consumers\n\t\t\t\t  browsing the first party's website.
    Both the first party that allows the third\n\t\t\t\t  parties to collect personal
    information via its website, as well as the third\n\t\t\t\t  party controlling
    the collection of personal information, shall provide a\n\t\t\t\t  Notice at Collection.
    The first party and third parties may provide a single\n\t\t\t\t  Notice at Collection
    that includes the required information about their\n\t\t\t\t  collective information
    practices. (2) A business that, acting as a third party,\n\t\t\t\t  controls the
    collection of personal information on another business's physical\n\t\t\t\t  premises,
    such as in a retail store or in a vehicle, shall provide a Notice at\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Collection in a conspicuous manner at the physical location(s) where it is\n\t\t\t\t
    \ collecting the personal information. (3) Illustrative examples follow. (A) Business
    F allows Business G, a third\n\t\t\t\t  party ad network, to collect consumers'
    personal information through Business\n\t\t\t\t  F's website. Business F may post
    a conspicuous link to its Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t  on its homepage(s).
    Business G shall provide a Notice at Collection on its\n\t\t\t\t  homepage(s)
    or include the required information about its information practices\n\t\t\t\t
    \ in Business F's Notice at Collection. (B) Business H, a coffee shop, allows\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Business I, a business providing Wi-Fi services, to collect personal\n\t\t\t\t
    \ information from consumers using Business I's services on Business H's\n\t\t\t\t
    \ premises. Business H may post conspicuous signage at the entrance of the store\n\t\t\t\t
    \ or at the point-of-sale directing consumers to where the Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t
    \ for Business H can be found online. In addition, Business I shall post its own\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Notice at Collection on the first webpage or other interface consumers see\n\t\t\t\t
    \ before connecting to the Wi-Fi services offered. (C) Business J, a car rental
    business, allows\n\t\t\t\t  Business K to collect personal information from consumers
    within the vehicles\n\t\t\t\t  Business J rents to consumers. Business J may give
    its Notice at Collection to\n\t\t\t\t  the consumer at the point of sale ( i.e.
    , at the rental\n\t\t\t\t  counter) either in writing or orally. Business K may
    provide its own Notice at\n\t\t\t\t  Collection within the vehicle, such as through
    signage on the vehicle's\n\t\t\t\t  dashboard directing consumers to where the
    notice can be found\n\t\t\t\t  online. (h) A\n\t\t\t\t  business that neither
    collects nor controls the collection of personal\n\t\t\t\t  information directly
    from the consumer does not need to provide a Notice at\n\t\t\t\t  Collection to
    the consumer if it neither sells nor shares the consumer's\n\t\t\t\t  personal
    information. (i) A data\n\t\t\t\t  broker registered with the Attorney General
    pursuant to Civil Code section 1798.99.80 et seq. that collects personal information
    from a source other\n\t\t\t\t  than directly from the consumer does not need to
    provide a Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t  to the consumer if it has included in
    its registration submission a link to its\n\t\t\t\t  online privacy policy that
    includes instructions on how a consumer can submit a\n\t\t\t\t  request to opt-out

    Article 2 - Required Disclosures to Consumers Cal. Code Regs. Tit. 11, § 7012
    - Notice at Collection of Personal Information Cal. Code Regs. Tit. 11, § 7012
    - Notice at Collection of Personal Information State Regulations Compare (a) The
    purpose of the Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t  is to provide consumers with timely
    notice, at or before the point of\n\t\t\t\t  collection, about the categories
    of personal information to be collected from\n\t\t\t\t  them, the purposes for
    which the personal information is collected or used, and\n\t\t\t\t  whether that
    information is sold or shared, so that consumers have a tool to\n\t\t\t\t  exercise
    meaningful control over the business's use of their personal\n\t\t\t\t  information.
    For example, upon receiving the Notice at Collection, the consumer\n\t\t\t\t  can
    use the information in the notice as a tool to choose whether to engage\n\t\t\t\t
    \ with the business, or to direct the business not to sell or share their\n\t\t\t\t
    \ personal information and to limit the use and disclosure of their sensitive\n\t\t\t\t
    \ personal information. (b) The\n\t\t\t\t  Notice at Collection shall comply with
    section 7003 , subsections (a) and\n\t\t\t\t  (b). (c) The Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t
    \ shall be made readily available where consumers will encounter it at or before\n\t\t\t\t
    \ the point of collection of any personal information. Illustrative examples\n\t\t\t\t
    \ follow. (1) When a business collects\n\t\t\t\t  consumers' personal information
    online, it may post a conspicuous link to the\n\t\t\t\t  notice on the introductory
    page of the business's website and on all webpages\n\t\t\t\t  where personal information
    is collected. (2) When a business collects consumers'\n\t\t\t\t  personal information
    through a webform, it may post a conspicuous link to the\n\t\t\t\t  notice in
    close proximity to the fields in which the consumer inputs their\n\t\t\t\t  personal
    information, or in close proximity to the button by which the consumer\n\t\t\t\t
    \ submits their personal information to the business. (3) When a business collects
    personal\n\t\t\t\t  information through a mobile application, it may provide a
    link to the notice\n\t\t\t\t  on the mobile application's download page and within
    the application, such as\n\t\t\t\t  through the application's settings menu. (4)
    When a business collects consumers'\n\t\t\t\t  personal information offline, it
    may include the notice on printed forms that\n\t\t\t\t  collect personal information,
    provide the consumer with a paper version of the\n\t\t\t\t  notice, or post prominent
    signage directing consumers to where the notice can\n\t\t\t\t  be found online.
    (5) When a\n\t\t\t\t  business collects personal information over the telephone
    or in person, it may\n\t\t\t\t  provide the notice orally. (d) If a business does
    not give the Notice at\n\t\t\t\t  Collection to the consumer at or before the
    point of collection of their\n\t\t\t\t  personal information, the business shall
    not collect personal information from\n\t\t\t\t  the consumer. (e) A business
    shall\n\t\t\t\t  include the following in its Notice at Collection: (1) A list
    of the categories of personal\n\t\t\t\t  information about consumers, including
    categories of sensitive personal\n\t\t\t\t  information, to be collected. Each
    category of personal information shall be\n\t\t\t\t  written in a manner that
    provides consumers a meaningful understanding of the\n\t\t\t\t  information being
    collected. (2) The purpose(s) for which the categories of personal information,
    including\n\t\t\t\t  categories of sensitive personal information, are collected
    and used. (3) Whether each category of personal\n\t\t\t\t  information identified
    in subsection (e)(1) is sold or shared. (4) The length of time the business intends\n\t\t\t\t
    \ to retain each category of personal information identified in subsection\n\t\t\t\t
    \ (e)(1), or if that is not possible, the criteria used to determine the period\n\t\t\t\t
    \ of time it will be retained. (5) If\n\t\t\t\t  the business sells or shares
    personal information, the link to the Notice of\n\t\t\t\t  Right to Opt-out of
    Sale/Sharing, or in the case of offline notices, where the\n\t\t\t\t  webpage
    can be found online. (6) A\n\t\t\t\t  link to the business's privacy policy, or
    in the case of offline notices, where\n\t\t\t\t  the privacy policy can be found
    online. (f) If a business collects personal\n\t\t\t\t  information from a consumer
    online, the Notice at Collection may be given to\n\t\t\t\t  the consumer by providing
    a link that takes the consumer directly to the\n\t\t\t\t  specific section of
    the business's privacy policy that contains the information\n\t\t\t\t  required
    in subsection (e)(1) through (6). Directing the consumer to the\n\t\t\t\t  beginning
    of the privacy policy, or to another section of the privacy policy\n\t\t\t\t  that
    does not contain the required information, so that the consumer is\n\t\t\t\t  required
    to scroll through other information in order to determine the\n\t\t\t\t  categories
    of personal information to be collected and/or whether the business\n\t\t\t\t
    \ sells or shares the personal information collected, does not satisfy this\n\t\t\t\t
    \ standard. (g) Third Parties that\n\t\t\t\t  Control the Collection of Personal
    Information. This subsection shall not\n\t\t\t\t  affect the first party's obligations
    under the CCPA to comply with a consumer's\n\t\t\t\t  request to opt-out of sale/sharing.
    (1) For\n\t\t\t\t  purposes of giving Notice at Collection, more than one business
    may control the\n\t\t\t\t  collection of a consumer's personal information, and
    thus, have an obligation\n\t\t\t\t  to provide a Notice at Collection in accordance
    with the CCPA and these\n\t\t\t\t  regulations. For example, a first party may
    allow another business, acting as a\n\t\t\t\t  third party, to control the collection
    of personal information from consumers\n\t\t\t\t  browsing the first party's website.
    Both the first party that allows the third\n\t\t\t\t  parties to collect personal
    information via its website, as well as the third\n\t\t\t\t  party controlling
    the collection of personal information, shall provide a\n\t\t\t\t  Notice at Collection.
    The first party and third parties may provide a single\n\t\t\t\t  Notice at Collection
    that includes the required information about their\n\t\t\t\t  collective information
    practices. (2) A business that, acting as a third party,\n\t\t\t\t  controls the
    collection of personal information on another business's physical\n\t\t\t\t  premises,
    such as in a retail store or in a vehicle, shall provide a Notice at\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Collection in a conspicuous manner at the physical location(s) where it is\n\t\t\t\t
    \ collecting the personal information. (3) Illustrative examples follow. (A) Business
    F allows Business G, a third\n\t\t\t\t  party ad network, to collect consumers'
    personal information through Business\n\t\t\t\t  F's website. Business F may post
    a conspicuous link to its Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t  on its homepage(s).
    Business G shall provide a Notice at Collection on its\n\t\t\t\t  homepage(s)
    or include the required information about its information practices\n\t\t\t\t
    \ in Business F's Notice at Collection. (B) Business H, a coffee shop, allows\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Business I, a business providing Wi-Fi services, to collect personal\n\t\t\t\t
    \ information from consumers using Business I's services on Business H's\n\t\t\t\t
    \ premises. Business H may post conspicuous signage at the entrance of the store\n\t\t\t\t
    \ or at the point-of-sale directing consumers to where the Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t
    \ for Business H can be found online. In addition, Business I shall post its own\n\t\t\t\t
    \ Notice at Collection on the first webpage or other interface consumers see\n\t\t\t\t
    \ before connecting to the Wi-Fi services offered. (C) Business J, a car rental
    business, allows\n\t\t\t\t  Business K to collect personal information from consumers
    within the vehicles\n\t\t\t\t  Business J rents to consumers. Business J may give
    its Notice at Collection to\n\t\t\t\t  the consumer at the point of sale ( i.e.
    , at the rental\n\t\t\t\t  counter) either in writing or orally. Business K may
    provide its own Notice at\n\t\t\t\t  Collection within the vehicle, such as through
    signage on the vehicle's\n\t\t\t\t  dashboard directing consumers to where the
    notice can be found\n\t\t\t\t  online. (h) A\n\t\t\t\t  business that neither
    collects nor controls the collection of personal\n\t\t\t\t  information directly
    from the consumer does not need to provide a Notice at\n\t\t\t\t  Collection to
    the consumer if it neither sells nor shares the consumer's\n\t\t\t\t  personal
    information. (i) A data\n\t\t\t\t  broker registered with the Attorney General
    pursuant to Civil Code section 1798.99.80 et seq. that collects personal information
    from a source other\n\t\t\t\t  than directly from the consumer does not need to
    provide a Notice at Collection\n\t\t\t\t  to the consumer if it has included in
    its registration submission a link to its\n\t\t\t\t  online privacy policy that
    includes instructions on how a consumer can submit a\n\t\t\t\t  request to opt-out
