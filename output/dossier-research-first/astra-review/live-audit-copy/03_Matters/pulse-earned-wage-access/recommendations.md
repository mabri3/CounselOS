# Recommendations

No launch recommendation is ready. The funder and repayment flow are material facts, not implementation details.

## Immediate direction

- Keep the October date as a planning target only.
- Do not finalize “not a loan” or “no cost” copy during intake.
- Change the prototype so no tip is preselected. This is reversible and reduces design risk while the legal analysis continues.
- Separate free and instant delivery clearly. Show timing and price before the user selects either option.
- Start the licensing review after Treasury confirms the legal funder and funds flow.

## Decision counsel will later make

Counsel must choose whether to approve a three-state pilot, narrow the pilot to a lower-risk state and fee model, or delay launch until required permissions and controls are in place.
