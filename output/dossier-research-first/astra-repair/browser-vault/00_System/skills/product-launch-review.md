# Product Launch Review

1. Identify the key components of the launch request from the provided files (e.g., product description, target market, timeline, budget, risks).
2. Evaluate each component against common success criteria: market need, feasibility, resource availability, and alignment with strategic goals.
3. Summarize strengths and weaknesses in plain language.
4. Provide a clear recommendation: 'Go', 'No-go', or 'Go with conditions' (list the conditions).
5. Keep the response concise and structured, using bullet points for clarity.
