# Final Recommendation

Launch Cedar with the revised disclosure hierarchy and reconciliation controls approved by the launch committee.

## Conditions satisfied before launch

- Replace the unqualified “FDIC insurance up to $2 million” headline with a statement that funds are placed at network banks and may be eligible for pass-through insurance, subject to conditions and aggregation with the customer's deposits at the same banks.
- Show current bank allocations in the customer account view.
- Reconcile the company ledger to the administrator file each business day.
- Stop new sweeps into an affected bank while a material allocation difference remains unresolved.
- Keep a tested export that identifies each customer, receiving bank, ownership capacity, and amount.
- Maintain a bank-failure and administrator-outage runbook with named owners.

## Post-launch review

Compliance and Finance will review the first 90 days of exceptions. The decision must return to Legal if a difference remains unresolved for more than one business day, the network changes materially, or Marketing proposes a higher insurance maximum.
