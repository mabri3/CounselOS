# Working Recommendations

## Direction that can be given now

- Do not approve automated notices based only on a generic twelve-code lookup table.
- Require a decision-level record that connects the factors actually used, the principal factors for the outcome, and the reasons sent to the customer.
- Keep manual review for unmapped features, close feature weights, missing inputs, and account-management actions until testing shows the automated path is reliable.
- Pause use of device-derived underwriting features until Privacy confirms the collection notice and vendor terms support this purpose.
- Run comparative outcomes and less-discriminatory-alternative testing before the approval meeting, not after launch.

## Recommendation still pending

Final approval depends on the completed notice back-test, fair-lending analysis, device-data review, and the bank's written model-risk conditions.
