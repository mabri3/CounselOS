Harbor Relay pilot review memo
Version 1 working baseline — for review against version 2
Matter: the internal record · US pilot launch 21 October 2026
Status note: This memo is a version 1 working baseline built from the supplied specification v1 and the original saved advice. It reflects a 30-day report retention, customer-only access, and support access unconfirmed.) It must be reviewed against specification version 2 (which changes retention to 180 days and specifies ticket-only, logged support access) before any conclusion is relied on. No legal decision is recorded and no final draft exists.
Summary
Harbor Relay is planned for a US pilot launching 21 October 2026. The product sends an account activity report to the customer. Under the version 1 baseline, reports are retained for 30 days and only the customer can open them; whether support staff can open reports is unconfirmed. Because the report contains customer transaction/account data, the retention period and the access model are privacy and data-access controls that should be confirmed and documented before launch. This memo is a baseline only and needs review against version 2.
Retention
Baseline (v2): Reports are retained for 180 days.
The 180-day window should be confirmed as deliberate and consistent with any data-minimization or deletion commitments.
Review point: Version 2 changes retention to 180 days. That is a materially longer window and must be reviewed on its own — the business rationale, consistency with deletion/data-minimization commitments, and whether the longer window is disclosed.
Support access
Baseline (v1): The supplied specification states only the customer can open reports; support access is unconfirmed.
Reported (not independently verified): Sam Patel reports that support can open a report only for a customer support ticket, and every access is logged. This is a reported fact, not a verified one.
Review point: Version 2 specifies that customers and support staff can open reports, with support access requiring a customer support ticket and being logged. If that is the intended model, the earlier "only the customer can open" language is superseded, and any customer-facing disclosure should reflect the actual access model.
Open items
Confirm the intended retention period (30 vs 180 days) and the rationale for any change.
Confirm the intended support-access model: ticket-only and logged (per Sam Patel's report and v2) versus customer-only (per v1).
Confirm whether the "only the customer can open" access statement needs reconciling with any support access.
Confirm whether the ticket-gating and logging controls are sufficient and implemented as described.
Confirm whether the 30/180-day retention is disclosed to customers.

Preserve this exact qualification: Sam Patel reported ticket-only logged support access; implementation and the retention rationale remain unverified.
