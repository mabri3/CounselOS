# Facts

## Confirmed facts

- Relay has about 280,000 customers with an active linked bank account.
- Existing connections include credential-based and API-based methods.
- Current uses include ACH verification, balance display, categorization, fraud detection, and overdraft-buffer eligibility.
- The current consent uses the broad phrase “provide and improve Relay services.”
- The product does not give a clear retention period after disconnection.
- Growth proposes using two years of transaction history to create segments for card and lending offers.
- The aggregator contract renews on September 15, 2026.

## Resolved research fact

- As of the research check on August 28, 2026, the CFPB states that a court stayed the compliance dates for the 2024 Personal Financial Data Rights Rule. The underlying statutory, privacy, contract, and consumer-protection analysis still applies.
