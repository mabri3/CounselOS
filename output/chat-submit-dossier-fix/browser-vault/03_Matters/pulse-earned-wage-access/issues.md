# Issue Map

1. **Product classification.** Is the advance credit under federal or state law, or does a state earned-wage-access law create a separate treatment?
2. **State permissions.** Which licenses, registrations, examinations, fee limits, and reporting duties apply in California, Nevada, and New York?
3. **Tips and expedited-delivery fees.** Are the charges truly voluntary, and can they become finance charges or evidence of an unfair design when the paid choice is favored?
4. **Repayment authorization.** Does the ACH authorization satisfy Regulation E and network rules, and can access be conditioned on preauthorized electronic repayment?
5. **Advertising and disclosures.** Can the company use “no interest,” “not a loan,” and “access your wages” in the same flow without qualification?
6. **Data use.** Do the payroll and bank-data consents cover eligibility, fraud prevention, servicing, and model improvement?
7. **Operational controls.** What complaint handling, error resolution, failed-payment, insolvency, and reconciliation controls must exist before launch?
8. **Partner allocation.** Which duties belong to the company, sponsor bank, payroll-data provider, and payment processor, and which duties remain non-delegable?
