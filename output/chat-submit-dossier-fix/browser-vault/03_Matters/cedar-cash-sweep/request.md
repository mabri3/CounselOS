# Original Request

Banking is ready to launch Cedar, which automatically moves business-customer cash above a target operating balance from our sponsor-bank account into deposit accounts at up to eight network banks. The product page currently says customers can “earn 3.75% APY with FDIC insurance up to $2 million.” Customers see one balance in our app, can withdraw on demand, and do not choose the receiving banks. Our ledger records each customer's allocation, and the sweep administrator sends a daily bank-level file.

Operations found three cases in testing where our customer ledger and the administrator file differed until the next morning. No money was missing, but Compliance wants to know whether we can launch with next-day exception handling. The sponsor bank also asked us to explain that insurance depends on account titling, records, customer ownership, and the customer's deposits at the same bank outside Cedar. Marketing does not want the first screen to become a wall of qualifications.

Please approve the disclosure approach, confirm which records and controls support pass-through treatment, and say whether daily reconciliation must be complete before we advertise the insurance amount. We also need a plan for a bank failure, administrator outage, and customer withdrawal while an allocation file is unresolved. The launch committee meets August 22.
