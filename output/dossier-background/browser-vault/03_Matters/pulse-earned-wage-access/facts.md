# Facts

## Reported facts

- Pulse would be offered directly to consumers, not through an employer.
- The proposed maximum advance is $150 per pay cycle.
- Standard delivery is free and takes two to three business days.
- Instant delivery costs $3.99.
- The proposed tip screen offers $0, $2, $4, or a custom amount, with $2 preselected.
- Users may change the tip to zero, but the current design requires two taps.
- Eligibility uses payroll-login data and linked bank-account data.
- A failed repayment blocks later advances. The company does not use collections or credit reporting.
- Product proposes California, Nevada, and New York for the first launch.
