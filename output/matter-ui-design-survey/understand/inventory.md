## A-style compact design recommendations

1. Keep the top reading order: matter title/state, one business question, one short working answer, one next action. Keep `Understand / Discuss / Draft` as a compact native radio group.
2. Keep the six-issue navigator near the first viewport. Use a compact row with issue text, explicit state word (`OPEN` / `No disposition recorded`), and owner. Open issue detail inline or in a right rail.
3. Put agent content in the dashed iris treatment with an explicit `Agent work · No cited sources` label. Keep recorded facts and decisions visually separate.
4. Group lower controls into four collapsible sections: `Evidence and history`, `Explore`, `Prior work and watches`, and `Research and work`. Preserve every existing action inside these sections.
5. Put empty/disabled reasons beside the disabled control, for example `Choose a question before preparing a request` or `No proposed facts are ready`. Keep disabled actions visually quiet and retain the state word.
6. Move the long research queue, work-item assignments, participants, and maintenance history into a sticky right rail or secondary drawer. The main column should keep the current question, answer, issue list, and next action readable.
7. Keep “record” actions explicit and separate from recommendations: `Record disposition`, `Record formal decision`, and `Complete` should remain direct lawyer controls with no implied conversion.
8. Keep the existing semantic colors: amber for `Needs attention`, purple/iris for agent work, green for complete, and a visible state word beside every color. Use light washes for rows and stronger tints only for small controls.
