## What the grade means

A working draft must answer the business question, explain the operative
conditions, apply the known facts, identify material source limits, and give a
usable work plan. It must preserve the distinction between reported facts,
analysis, hypothetical alternatives, proposed work, and recorded decisions.
Useful existing research must stay accessible.

## General changes

- The writer now receives saved source passages, locators, source versions, and
  support limits. Raw fetched pages do not crowd out these passages.
- A source used by several issues retains each selected passage. Different
  source versions stay separate.
- Known saved external-source links use the saved source label. Model-written
  captions cannot rename the linked record or claim a higher support state.
- The current direction's working note reaches the writer as fallible analysis.
  It does not become a reported fact. Source restrictions still apply.
- Writing guidance requires conditions, exceptions, factual application,
  dependencies, and a practical fallback. A refresh must reconsider the earlier
  draft rather than copy its conclusions.
- Work plans distinguish existing assignments and source-based deadlines from
  proposed roles and working targets.
- The application owns the preserved research disclosures. The model supplies
  the current synthesis. This removed duplicate research folds in the test output.
- Internal markers remain in Markdown but are hidden in the reader. Code
  examples remain unchanged.
- Long document titles now occupy one scrollable tab row. The browser check
  reproduced obstructed source/return clicks before this layout change and
  completed those clicks after it.

These changes use focused input, prompt, reference, and display fixes. They add
no legal verifier, approval gate, queue, or extra generation agent.
