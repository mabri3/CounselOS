# Original Request

RelayPay,” is a U.S. fintech that lets customers send international remittances. It uses a licensed money-transmitter partner, but RelayPay performs customer onboarding, transaction monitoring, and first-line investigations.
Over 12 days, one customer opened four accounts using different email addresses and phone numbers. The accounts used the same device, IP range, and debit card. Each account sent transfers of $1,800–$1,950 to different recipients in the same foreign city. Total outbound transfers were $28,400.
The customer told support that the payments are “family support.” The recipient names do not match the customer’s disclosed relatives. One recipient is linked in adverse media to an unlicensed currency-exchange business. No sanctions match has been confirmed.
Product asks: “Can we keep processing these transfers? What must we do now?”
