# Original Request

Northstar Pay plans to redesign its merchant checkout experience for pay-in-4 purchases. The business goal is to increase completion rates while giving customers a clear view of the payment schedule before they accept the plan. The proposed experience will show the purchase amount, four scheduled payments, payment dates, any applicable fees, the amount due today, and a link to the loan agreement. The customer will complete identity and eligibility checks in the checkout flow. The merchant, Northstar Pay, the customer, and the payment processor will participate in the transaction. Product wants to launch the redesign during the next quarter.

Known facts are that pay-in-4 may be offered with no interest, but some transactions may include late fees or other charges depending on the product and applicable law. The experience may differ between a merchant’s website, a mobile web flow, and the Northstar Pay app. Open questions include which disclosures must appear before approval, which disclosures must appear before acceptance, how payment timing must be displayed, and whether the same presentation may be used across all states. Please advise on the required federal and state disclosures, agreement delivery, electronic-consent language, and any changes needed to the proposed user experience before launch.
