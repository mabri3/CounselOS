# Issues

## 1. Disclosure timing and content
- Which disclosures must appear before approval vs. before acceptance (Reg Z closed-end credit vs. state BNPL rules; ECOA/Reg B adverse action notice).
- Payment schedule presentation: amount due today, four payment amounts, dates, fees.

## 2. State-by-state variation
- Whether one presentation may be used in all states; fee limits, disclosure format, and licensing-driven differences.
- Partner/responsible-entity differences by state may change the disclosure owner and agreement terms.

## 3. Agreement delivery and e-consent
- E-SIGN/UETA consent flow for electronic delivery of the loan agreement; link placement and retention of delivered agreements.
- Electronic-consent language for the checkout flow.

## 4. UX changes needed before launch
- Disclosure prominence and timing relative to the accept action.
- Cross-channel consistency (merchant web, mobile web, app).
- Fee disclosure (late fees) prominence where applicable.

## 5. Partner and processor touchpoints
- Lending partner responsibilities for disclosures and servicing; payment processor data flows (privacy notice alignment).
