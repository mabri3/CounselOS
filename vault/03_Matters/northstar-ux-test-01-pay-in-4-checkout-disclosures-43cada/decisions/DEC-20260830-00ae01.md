# Pay-in-4 checkout redesign — conditional approval for next-quarter launch

## Chosen path

Conditional approval for a next-quarter launch; not approval to launch today. Launch is authorized only after all six launch conditions are satisfied and verified: (1) pre-acceptance disclosure set completed (four payment amounts and dates, amount due today, total of payments, all fees with trigger conditions, creditor identity) consistent across merchant web, mobile web, and the Northstar Pay app; (2) E-SIGN consent step added before electronic agreement delivery, with consent events, delivered agreements, and disclosure versions retained; (3) CFPB BNPL interpretive-rule status verified against the live product configuration; (4) state matrix confirmed (creditor/partner, licensing, fee caps, required text) for each launch state; (5) adverse-action process defined for declined applicants in the checkout flow; (6) legal test-matrix review completed covering no-fee and fee-bearing transactions, decline paths, and agreement-delivery failures across all three channels.

## Rationale

The schedule-first UX supports the completion-rate goal only if all pre-acceptance terms, E-SIGN consent, creditor identity, state fee rules, and decline handling are implemented and tested across merchant web, mobile web, and the Northstar Pay app. Absent those, the current design does not satisfy the disclosure and consent requirements and should not launch. This is conditional approval for a next-quarter launch, not approval to launch today. Separate recommendation recorded: adopt a CA-baseline disclosure set with state overlays, while preserving that California SB 975 and other state-specific details are unverified leads until confirmed.

## Conditions

- Pre-acceptance disclosure set completed and consistent across merchant web, mobile web, and the Northstar Pay app
- E-SIGN consent step added before electronic agreement delivery, with consent events, delivered agreements, and disclosure versions retained
- CFPB BNPL interpretive-rule status verified against the live product configuration
- State matrix confirmed: creditor/partner, licensing, fee caps, and required text for each launch state
- Adverse-action process defined for declined applicants in the checkout flow
- Legal test-matrix review completed: no-fee and fee-bearing transactions, decline paths, and agreement-delivery failures across all three channels
