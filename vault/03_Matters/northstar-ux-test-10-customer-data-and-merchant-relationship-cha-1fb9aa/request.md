# Original Request

Northstar Pay plans to expand its use of customer and transaction data for product analytics, fraud prevention, personalized offers, and merchant performance reporting. The business goal is to improve underwriting and show merchants which checkout experiences lead to completed purchases. The proposed experience may use purchase categories, repayment history, device information, approximate location, and app activity. Customers may receive personalized offers for new financing products or merchant promotions. Merchants may receive aggregated dashboards, while selected strategic merchants may receive more detailed reports. The actors include customers, Northstar Pay, merchants, lending partners, analytics vendors, advertising partners, and service providers. Product wants to begin the data program this quarter.

Known facts are that Northstar Pay has existing privacy notices and contracts that were written for a narrower product. Some merchants want to use Northstar Pay transaction data for their own marketing. The company may also change a merchant’s underwriting rules, pricing, settlement timing, or access to customer data as part of a new commercial program. Open questions include permitted data uses, consent and opt-out requirements, sensitive-data restrictions, retention periods, sharing agreements, customer notice changes, and legal limits on changing merchant or customer-facing product terms. Please advise on the data-use model, required notices and agreements, and the legal review needed for the proposed merchant relationship and product changes.
