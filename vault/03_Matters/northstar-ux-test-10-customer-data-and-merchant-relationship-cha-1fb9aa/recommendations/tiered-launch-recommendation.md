# Recommendation: Tiered Launch of the Expanded Data Program

Matter: Northstar UX Test — 10 — Customer Data and Merchant Relationship Changes

Status: Draft recommendation — not a decision. No durable decision has been recorded; approval rests with the legal owner (Brian Harris) and, for customer-facing term changes, Alex Morgan per company policy.

Decision question

Which of the proposed data uses can Northstar Pay launch this quarter under existing notices and contracts, and which require new customer notice/consent, merchant agreement amendments, or partner/vendor agreements first?

Recommendation in one line

Launch Tier 1 (internal product analytics, fraud prevention, aggregated merchant dashboards) this quarter, subject to the verification gates below. Hold Tier 2 (personalized offers, merchant promotions, detailed strategic-merchant reporting) and Tier 3 (merchant-owned marketing, advertising-partner sharing) pending notice updates, consent/opt-out flows, and amended agreements. Reject merchant-owned marketing use of identifiable transaction data absent a documented legal basis and customer choice.

Tier structure and per-use treatment

1. Product analytics (internal) — Tier 1, launchable

Use: Internal analysis of purchase categories, repayment history, device info, approximate location, and app activity to improve underwriting and product.

Basis: Likely within a service-provider/internal-operations purpose; lowest new-purpose risk.

Gaps/controls: Confirm the current privacy notice covers analytics on these data types; minimize sensitive fields and precise location; role-based access; documented retention schedule (not indefinite); deletion/suppression workflow on account closure.

Gate: Notice text review + data-map confirmation of fields actually collected.

2. Fraud prevention — Tier 1, launchable

Use: Transaction and device signals for fraud detection and prevention.

Basis: Fraud prevention is a widely recognized compatible purpose and typically does not require opt-in, but state law and notice language still matter.

Gaps/controls: Confirm vendor agreements put the fraud vendor under written instructions; restrict secondary use of fraud data; retention tied to dispute/litigation needs.

Gate: Vendor agreement check.

3. Underwriting improvements — Tier 1, launchable with care

Use: Expanded data inputs for underwriting models.

Basis: Core account-servicing/underwriting purpose; but use of location, device, and app-activity data in credit decisions raises fair-lending, accuracy, and adverse-action considerations.

Gaps/controls: Model-input documentation; confirm no state in the live footprint (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA) restricts the data types used; adverse-action/notice alignment if decisions change.

Gate: Compliance sign-off on model inputs and adverse-action flow.

4. Personalized offers (new financing products) — Tier 2, gated

Use: Customer-tailored offers using repayment history, purchase categories, and app activity.

Why gated: New purpose not covered by the narrower existing notice; company policy: do not assume consent covers a new purpose. Cross-context behavioral advertising and sensitive-data rules (especially CA/CO) may require opt-in or opt-out.

Required before launch: Updated privacy notice; in-app consent/opt-out flow; suppression workflow honoring choices; state-law mapping for the live footprint.

Gate: Notice update + consent flow live + state-law review complete.

5. Merchant promotions (offers delivered via merchants) — Tier 2, gated

Use: Merchant-funded promotions surfaced to customers based on Northstar Pay data.

Why gated: Combines new-purpose use with merchant-facing sharing; requires merchant agreement terms defining permitted use, plus customer notice and choice.

Required before launch: Merchant agreement amendment (permitted-use clause), customer notice/consent, opt-out honored across channels.

Gate: Amendment + notice + consent flow.

6. Merchant performance reporting — split

Aggregated/de-identified dashboards (default): Tier 1, launchable. Confirm aggregation standards (no re-identification, minimum cohort sizes) and that the notice covers merchant reporting at this level.

Detailed reports for strategic merchants: Tier 2, gated. Per company policy, requires separate legal review of purpose, data fields, contract, customer notice, access controls, and state limits. Identifiable repayment history should not be included absent a documented legal basis and customer choice.

Gate (detailed tier): Field-level data map + contract + notice + access controls approved.

7. Merchant-owned marketing using Northstar Pay transaction data — Tier 3, reject for now

Use: Merchants using identifiable transaction/repayment data for their own marketing.

Why rejected: Company policy prohibits merchants or advertising partners using identifiable repayment history or sensitive customer data for unrelated marketing without a documented legal basis and required customer choice. No such basis or choice mechanism exists today.

Path if business insists: Separate legal review establishing a lawful basis, explicit customer opt-in, contract restrictions, and state-law clearance — treat as a new matter, not a launch feature.

8. Advertising partners — Tier 3, gated/reject for now

Use: Sharing data with advertising partners for targeting or measurement.

Why gated: Cross-context behavioral advertising restrictions (CA/CO opt-out or opt-in regimes) and the same new-purpose problem as personalized offers; no current agreement or customer choice flow supports it.

Required before any launch: Written agreement with use restrictions, customer opt-out honored, notice update, state-law mapping.

9. Merchant commercial-term changes (underwriting rules, pricing, settlement timing, data access) — separate workstream, not a data gate

Issue: The new commercial program would change merchant underwriting rules, pricing, settlement timing, and data access.

Analysis: Whether Northstar Pay can make these changes unilaterally depends on the amendment/change-of-terms provisions in each merchant agreement. Unilateral changes risk breach and state unfair-practices exposure.

Required: Contract-by-contract review of amendment rights; notice periods; renegotiation for merchants on older templates.

Gate: Contract review complete before the commercial program is announced to merchants.

10. Customer-facing term changes — separate approval track

Any material change to customer-facing terms, sharing, or retention requires Alex Morgan's approval per company policy, plus updated notice delivery and, where the change is material to existing customers, advance notice and possibly re-consent. This is a distinct approval from the tiered launch decision.
