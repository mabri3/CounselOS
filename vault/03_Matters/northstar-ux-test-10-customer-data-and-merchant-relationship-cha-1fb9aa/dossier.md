## Summary

Northstar Pay (Product requester; legal owner Brian Harris) wants to expand use of customer and transaction data this quarter for product analytics, fraud prevention, personalized offers, and merchant performance reporting, using purchase categories, repayment history, device info, approximate location, and app activity. Existing privacy notices and contracts were written for a narrower product. Some merchants want Northstar Pay transaction data for their own marketing, and a new commercial program may change merchant underwriting rules, pricing, settlement timing, and data access. Counsel must define the permitted data-use model, required notices and agreements, and legal limits on changing merchant and customer-facing terms before launch.

## Decision question

Which data uses in the proposed program can Northstar Pay launch this quarter under its existing notices and contracts, and which require new customer notice/consent, merchant agreement amendments, or partner/vendor agreements first — given that merchants want transaction data for their own marketing and the commercial program would change merchant underwriting, pricing, settlement, and data-access terms?

## Open questions

- What do the current privacy notice and customer terms actually permit for analytics, personalized offers, location/device data, and merchant promotions — and where are the gaps?
- Which strategic merchants would receive detailed (non-aggregated) reports, and what data fields, purpose, and access controls would that involve?
- What do current merchant and lending-partner contracts say about data ownership, sharing, and unilateral changes to underwriting, pricing, settlement timing, or data access?
- What retention periods apply to the new analytics and reporting datasets, and do any uses involve sensitive data or precise location?
- Which states in the live footprint (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA) impose consent, opt-out, or sensitive-data limits that constrain the program?
