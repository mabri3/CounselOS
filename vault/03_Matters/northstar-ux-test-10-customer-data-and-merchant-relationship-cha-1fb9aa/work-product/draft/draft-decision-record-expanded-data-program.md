# Draft Decision Record — Expanded Data Program (Tiered Launch)

**Status: DRAFT — reviewed and edited, not recorded. This decision record has not been confirmed by Alex Morgan and is not a durable decision. It remains separate from the tiered-launch recommendation and the lawyer response.**

## Decision question

Should Northstar Pay approve the proposed expansion of customer and transaction data use (product analytics, fraud prevention, underwriting, personalized offers, merchant performance reporting, merchant-owned marketing, and advertising-partner sharing) on a tiered-launch basis, with separate tracks for merchant commercial-term changes and customer-facing term changes?

## Decision maker

Alex Morgan, Legal — Northstar Pay (fictional company; fictional decision record for review).

## Proposed decision

Approve a tiered launch of the expanded data program.

### Approved — Tier 1 (launchable this quarter, subject to conditions)

1. **Product analytics** using purchase categories, repayment history, device information, approximate location, and app activity for internal measurement of checkout completion and product improvement — subject to the Tier 1 conditions below, including notice-text confirmation that the existing privacy notice covers analytics on these data types.
2. **Fraud prevention** using transaction, device, approximate-location, and app-activity signals — subject to confirmation that the fraud vendor operates under written instructions with no secondary use, and that retention is tied to dispute and litigation needs.
3. **Underwriting use of new data inputs** — approved in principle only. Underwriting is not automatically launchable: each proposed input, and any changed credit term resulting from it, requires model-input documentation, accuracy and fairness testing, adverse-action notice alignment, lending-partner review, and compliance sign-off before use. No new underwriting input goes live under this decision without that sign-off.
4. **Merchant performance reporting** limited to aggregated or de-identified dashboards — approved as the default only, pending confirmation of (a) aggregation and de-identification standards (minimum cohort sizes, small-cohort suppression, no re-identification), (b) notice coverage of merchant reporting at this level, and (c) merchant contract terms consistent with the default. Detailed reporting for strategic merchants is not included in Tier 1.

### Held — Tier 2 (not launched until gates are met)

1. **Personalized offers** for new Northstar Pay financing products — pending updated privacy notice, in-app consent/opt-out flow, preference suppression honored across channels, and state-law review for the live footprint.
2. **Merchant promotions** using Northstar Pay data — pending the same Tier 2 gates plus a merchant-agreement permitted-use amendment.
3. **Detailed strategic-merchant reports** — pending field-level data-map approval, contract limits on use and onward sharing, customer notice, access controls, and state-law review. Identifiable repayment history is not included absent a documented legal basis and customer choice.

Tier 2 uses launch only after their gates are met and this decision is revisited; opening a Tier 2 gate is itself a review trigger.

### Rejected — Tier 3 (not approved in this launch)

1. **Merchant-owned marketing** using identifiable Northstar Pay transaction or repayment data, or audiences derived from repayment history.
2. **Advertising-partner sharing** of raw events, identifiers, device data, location data, repayment history, or matched audiences.

If Product wants either Tier 3 use, it requires a new scoped review with a documented legal basis, explicit customer-choice design, a data-flow map, and contracts — treated as a new matter, not a feature of this launch.

### Separate tracks (not decided by this record)

1. **Merchant commercial-term changes** (underwriting rules, pricing, settlement timing, access to customer data) — require a contract-by-contract amendment-rights review before the commercial program is announced to merchants. Unilateral changes risk breach and state unfair-practices exposure; merchants on older templates may require renegotiation.
2. **Customer-facing term changes** (pricing, underwriting, sharing, retention, or other material terms) — require Alex Morgan review and approval of the final customer notice and terms, plus advance notice and, where material, re-consent for existing customers. This approval track is distinct from the tiered-launch decision.

## Reasons

- The Tier 1 uses fit the narrower existing privacy notices and contracts better than the full program and support the business goal of improving underwriting and merchant checkout insights this quarter.
- Underwriting expansion carries fair-lending, accuracy, and adverse-action risk that cannot be cleared by a blanket approval; it is approved in principle with per-input sign-off.
- Personalized offers, merchant promotions, and detailed reporting are new purposes under the existing notice and require customer choice, contract amendments, and state-law verification before launch.
- Merchant-owned marketing with identifiable repayment data and advertising-partner sharing lack a documented legal basis and required customer choice, and risk sensitive-data and cross-context advertising violations.
- A staged launch preserves the quarter for the core uses while keeping higher-risk uses behind proper gates.

## Conditions of approval

- Field-level data map completed and approved before any Tier 1 use begins.
- Privacy notice reviewed and, if needed, updated to cover Tier 1 uses before launch.
- Vendor and lending-partner agreements reviewed and amended as needed for each Tier 1 use.
- Underwriting model-input, fairness, accuracy, adverse-action, and lending-partner sign-off obtained before any credit use of new inputs.
- Aggregation and de-identification standards documented before merchant dashboards launch.
- Retention periods and access controls defined for each Tier 1 dataset.
- Tier 2 uses launch only after their gates are met and this decision is revisited.
- Tier 3 uses do not launch under this decision.

## Assumptions

- The program is still in design; no new sharing has started.
- The current privacy notice and contracts cover a narrower product.
- "Approximate location" is not precise geolocation; if it is, sensitive-data treatment applies.
- Sensitive categories and precise location are not approved uses.
- Northstar Pay can stage the launch across tiers.

## Missing facts

- Governing jurisdictions and customer states.
- Exact data fields and derived scores; whether any field affects credit terms.
- Current privacy notice and customer terms text.
- Merchant and lending-partner contract terms, including amendment provisions.
- Vendor roles, locations, and existing agreements.
- Retention schedule and aggregation thresholds.
