# Expanded Data Program — Tiered Launch Decision (Tier 1 in principle, Tier 2 held, Tier 3 rejected)

## Chosen path

Approve Tier 1 uses (product analytics, fraud prevention, underwriting, aggregated merchant dashboards) only in principle and only after each stated gate is satisfied; hold Tier 2 uses (personalized offers, merchant promotions, detailed strategic-merchant reporting) pending their individual gates; reject Tier 3 uses (merchant-owned marketing of identifiable transaction data, advertising-partner sharing) for this launch. Merchant commercial-term changes and customer-facing term changes remain separate tracks not decided by this record.

## Rationale

Tier 1 uses are likely within existing service-provider/internal-operations and fraud-prevention purposes and can launch this quarter subject to verification gates. Tier 2 uses are new purposes not covered by the narrower existing privacy notice; company policy does not assume consent covers a new purpose, and cross-context advertising and sensitive-data rules (especially CA/CO) require notice updates, consent/opt-out flows, and amended agreements first. Tier 3 uses are rejected because company policy prohibits merchants or advertising partners using identifiable repayment history or sensitive customer data for unrelated marketing without a documented legal basis and required customer choice, neither of which exists today. Merchant and customer term changes raise breach and unfair-practices risk and require separate contract review and approval tracks.

## Conditions

- Tier 1 underwriting: each new model input and any changed credit term requires model, fairness, accuracy, adverse-action, and lending-partner sign-off before use
- Tier 1 aggregated merchant reporting: default only, pending de-identification standards, notice coverage, and contract checks; excludes identifiable repayment history absent documented basis and customer choice
- Tier 1 analytics and fraud: notice-coverage check, vendor written-instruction confirmation, sensitive-data and location minimization, documented retention schedule
- Tier 2 uses launch only after their individual gates: notice update, consent/opt-out flow, state-law review, merchant/vendor amendments; opening any Tier 2 gate is a review trigger
- Tier 3 uses may only be pursued as a new scoped matter with documented legal basis and customer choice, not as launch features
- Merchant term changes require contract amendment-rights review before announcement; customer-facing term changes require Alex Morgan approval, advance notice, and possible re-consent
