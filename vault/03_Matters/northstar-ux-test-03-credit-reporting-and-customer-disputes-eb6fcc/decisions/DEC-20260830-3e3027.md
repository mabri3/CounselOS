# Limited Credit-Reporting Pilot — Controlled Preparation, Gated Live Furnishing

## Chosen path

Northstar Pay may proceed with controlled preparation for a limited credit-reporting pilot next quarter, but live furnishing is not authorized until every launch gate is passed and Alex Morgan approves the final launch. The proposed population is eligible longer-term installment loans in approved states with complete and independently verified history. Exclude pay-in-4, accounts with known data gaps, accounts with unresolved disputes, and any expansion beyond the approved population. Before any negative information is furnished, the dispute workflow, tradeline-level suppression and release controls, payment-date normalization, required notice flow, vendor controls, and evidence retention must be live, tested, and owned.

## Rationale

The business objective of supporting customer credit-building and reducing losses is reasonable, but Northstar's known data gaps create a material risk if the company starts with full-population reporting and relies on post-hoc corrections. A narrow, complete-data pilot gives Northstar a practical way to demonstrate accuracy before furnishing and to test the control path for holidays, failed transfers, refunds, plan changes, and disputes. It also produces measurable evidence about dispute volumes, correction rates, suppression events, vendor performance, and operational capacity before any expansion decision. The revised decision therefore separates permission to prepare the controls from permission to furnish live data.

The most important sequencing point is dispute handling. A customer-support dispute and any CRA or e-OSCAR/ACDV item must be logged, routed, investigated, and resolved under a documented process. When a negative field is not verified, the affected tradeline should be held from the next furnishing event until the investigation is complete. If the data is wrong, Northstar should correct the source record, notify affected CRAs as required, notify the consumer of the result, and confirm that the next file carries the correction. This is a proposed control design, and its legal timing requirements must be checked against current authoritative sources.

Material assumptions preserved from the edited proposal: Northstar Pay is the furnisher of record for the pilot population, and if a bank or lending partner is the furnisher for a product or state, the decision must be re-scoped to reflect Northstar's contract-oversight role; the reporting vendor is a service provider or intermediary operating under contract with Northstar keeping oversight of vendor-caused errors; longer-term installment loans are reportable credit for the pilot while pay-in-4 classification remains unresolved and excluded; Northstar can log, suppress, and release furnishing at the tradeline level; and the pilot state list and partner map will be confirmed before launch.

Unresolved facts preserved from the edited proposal: the partner-by-state furnishing map; the extent and location of incomplete historical data; the pilot state list; the CRAs and whether e-OSCAR/ACDV intake is used; the vendor contract status; retention tooling; and available Customer Support and Operations capacity. The business must assign owners for eligibility, dispute operations, engineering suppression, vendor management, and customer-support intake before launch.

Unverified legal and operational leads preserved from the edited proposal: current Reg V sections 1022.41 through 1022.43 and section 1022.42; FCRA section 1681s-2 and the negative-information notice timing; CFPB Circular 2022-07 and Bulletin 2013-07; state notice rules including California Civil Code section 1785.26; CRA onboarding, Metro 2, and e-OSCAR requirements; and whether pay-in-4 is reportable credit. These must be verified against current CFR, CFPB, CRA, and state sources before launch or external use.

Owners preserved from the edited proposal: Brian Harris remains the matter owner; Alex Morgan is the reviewing product lawyer and final decision-maker for the pilot; Product or Eligibility owns the approved population; Operations owns dispute investigation and aging; Engineering owns suppression, release, and audit logging; Customer Support owns intake and consumer communications; Vendor Management owns vendor performance and contract controls; Legal and Compliance own current-source verification, escalations, and launch review. Named individuals must be confirmed before launch.

Launch gates preserved from the edited proposal: (1) furnisher of record confirmed for every product and state; (2) pilot products, states, and complete-data exclusion list approved and tested; (3) payment-date rules tested for holidays, failed transfers, refunds, and plan changes; (4) required notices live, logged, and verified for timing; (5) direct-dispute and CRA/e-OSCAR/ACDV workflows pass end-to-end dry runs; (6) tradeline-level suppression and release controls pass negative-data tests; (7) vendor contract includes accuracy, transmission, dispute, audit, incident, retention, and correction terms; (8) retention schedule, evidence model, and access owners confirmed; (9) owners assigned and trained; (10) legal review of current citations and state rules complete.
