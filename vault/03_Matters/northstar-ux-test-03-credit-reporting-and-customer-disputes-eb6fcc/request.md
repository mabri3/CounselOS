# Original Request

Northstar Pay is considering reporting eligible installment-loan payment activity to one or more consumer reporting agencies. The business goal is to help customers build credit and reduce losses by encouraging on-time payments. The proposed flow will tell customers during onboarding that payment data may be reported. Northstar Pay will send account-opening, balance, payment, delinquency, and account-closure information through a reporting vendor. Customers will be able to view reporting-related information in the app and submit disputes through customer support. The relevant actors are Northstar Pay, the customer, consumer reporting agencies, the reporting vendor, and servicing agents. Product hopes to begin a pilot next quarter.

Known facts are that Northstar Pay has incomplete historical data for some accounts and that payment dates may be affected by bank holidays, failed transfers, refunds, and payment-plan changes. Open questions include which products and customers may be reported, what data may be reported, how Northstar Pay must validate accuracy, and how it must investigate and resolve disputes. The team also needs guidance on notices, correction timelines, record retention, and whether negative information may be reported when a customer has an unresolved billing or payment dispute. Please advise on the legal requirements and the minimum operational process for launch.
