# Proposed Pilot Decision — Limited Credit-Reporting Pilot

Privileged & confidential attorney work product. This is a proposed decision for Alex Morgan's review. It has not been recorded. The recommendations file remains a separate work product.

Decision text (proposed)

Northstar Pay should proceed with controlled preparation for a limited credit-reporting pilot next quarter, but this proposal does not authorize live furnishing until every launch gate below is passed and Alex Morgan records the final decision. The proposed pilot would cover eligible longer-term installment loans in approved states, using only accounts with complete and independently verified history. Pay-in-4, accounts with known data gaps, accounts with unresolved disputes, and any expansion beyond the approved population would remain out of scope. Before any negative information is furnished, the dispute workflow, tradeline-level suppression and release controls, payment-date normalization, negative-information notice flow, vendor controls, and evidence retention must be live, tested, and owned.

Rationale

The business objective of supporting customer credit-building and reducing losses is reasonable, but Northstar's known data gaps create a material risk if the company starts with full-population reporting and relies on post-hoc corrections. A narrow, complete-data pilot gives Northstar a practical way to demonstrate accuracy before furnishing and to test the control path for holidays, failed transfers, refunds, plan changes, and disputes. It also produces measurable evidence about dispute volumes, correction rates, suppression events, vendor performance, and operational capacity before any expansion decision. The revised proposal therefore separates permission to prepare the controls from permission to furnish live data.

The most important sequencing point is dispute handling. A customer-support dispute and any CRA or e-OSCAR/ACDV item must be logged, routed, investigated, and resolved under a documented process. When a negative field is not verified, the affected tradeline should be held from the next furnishing event until the investigation is complete. If the data is wrong, Northstar should correct the source record, notify affected CRAs as required, notify the consumer of the result, and confirm that the next file carries the correction. This is a proposed control design, and its legal timing requirements must be checked against current authoritative sources.

Material assumptions

Northstar Pay is the furnisher of record for the pilot population. If a bank or lending partner is the furnisher for a product or state, the decision must be re-scoped to reflect Northstar's contract-oversight role. The reporting vendor is a service provider or intermediary operating under contract, and Northstar keeps oversight of vendor-caused errors. Longer-term installment loans are reportable credit for the pilot, while pay-in-4 classification remains unresolved and excluded. Northstar can log, suppress, and release furnishing at the tradeline level. The pilot state list and partner map will be confirmed before launch.

Unresolved facts

The team still must confirm the partner-by-state furnishing map; the extent and location of incomplete historical data; the pilot state list; the CRAs and whether e-OSCAR/ACDV intake is used; the vendor contract status; retention tooling; and the available Customer Support and Operations capacity. The business must assign owners for eligibility, dispute operations, engineering suppression, vendor management, and customer-support intake. Owner assignment is a launch gate.

Unverified legal and operational leads

The following are leads, not confirmed conclusions: current Reg V sections 1022.41 through 1022.43 and section 1022.42; FCRA section 1681s-2 and the negative-information notice timing; CFPB Circular 2022-07 and Bulletin 2013-07; state notice rules including California Civil Code section 1785.26; CRA onboarding, Metro 2, and e-OSCAR requirements; and whether pay-in-4 is reportable credit. Verify these items against current CFR, CFPB, CRA, and state sources before launch or external use.

Scope

In scope are eligible longer-term installment loans in approved pilot states, complete and reconciled payment history, account-opening, balance, payment, delinquency, and closure fields, the vendor transmission cycle, direct disputes, and any CRA channel Northstar accepts. Out of scope are pay-in-4, incomplete or unverifiable accounts, unresolved disputes, and any expansion without a separate decision.

Owners

Brian Harris remains the matter owner. Alex Morgan is the reviewing product lawyer and final decision-maker for the pilot. Product or Eligibility owns the approved population; Operations owns dispute investigation and aging; Engineering owns suppression, release, and audit logging; Customer Support owns intake and consumer communications; Vendor Management owns vendor performance and contract controls; Legal and Compliance own current-source verification, escalations, and launch review. The business must confirm named individuals before launch.

Launch gates

1. Furnisher of record confirmed for every product and state.

2. Pilot products, states, and complete-data exclusion list approved and tested.

3. Payment-date rules tested for holidays, failed transfers, refunds, and plan changes.

4. Required notices are live, logged, and verified for timing.

5. Direct-dispute and CRA/e-OSCAR/ACDV workflows pass end-to-end dry runs.

6. Tradeline-level suppression and release controls pass negative-data tests.

7. Vendor contract includes accuracy, transmission, dispute, audit, incident, retention, and correction terms.

8. Retention schedule, evidence model, and access owners are confirmed.

9. Owners are assigned and trained.

10. Legal review of current citations and state rules is complete.

What would change the recommendation

Re-scope or delay if a partner is the furnisher for the intended population, if the exclusion list cannot reliably identify complete-data accounts, if the vendor rejects required controls, if current-source verification changes the proposed timelines or notices, if systemic errors cannot be bounded, or if the suppression capability cannot be tested. If the business later wants to include pay-in-4, it needs a separate classification analysis and decision.

Status

Proposed only. Not recorded. Record only after Alex Morgan confirms the scope, reviews the current-source verification, confirms owners and launch gates, and gives explicit instruction to record the decision.
