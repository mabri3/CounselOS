# Issues — Credit Reporting and Customer Disputes Pilot

1. **Furnisher of record.** Which entity (Northstar Pay vs. bank/lending partner) furnishes per product/state; does the partner contract permit or control reporting? *Assumption to confirm — changes the whole workstream if the partner is the furnisher.*
2. **Pilot scope / eligibility.** Which products (longer-term installments vs. pay-in-4) and which accounts; exclusion of accounts with incomplete historical data.
3. **Reportable fields and timing.** Metro 2 fields, monthly cycle, normalization of payment dates (holidays, failed transfers, refunds, plan changes) to contractual due date and actual receipt.
4. **Notices.** Onboarding disclosure; FCRA §1681s-2(a)(7) negative-information notice before first negative furnishing; state-specific notice rules for pilot states.
5. **Accuracy controls.** Reg V §1022.42 reasonable procedures; pre-furnishing review before negative data; data-quality gating.
6. **Dispute handling.** Direct disputes (in-app/support) under Reg V §1022.43 — 30-day investigation and results to consumer; 5-business-day frivolous-rejection notice; CRA/ACDV channel if disputes also arrive via e-OSCAR; correction notification to each CRA.
7. **Suppression during unresolved disputes.** No express FCRA bar, but accuracy duties + CFPB Circular 2022-07 / Bulletin 2013-07 favor suppressing or flagging disputed negative tradelines pending resolution.
8. **Vendor and servicing-agent controls.** Contract terms (accuracy, Metro 2, SLAs, audit, breach notice, retention, indemnity); furnisher remains liable for vendor error.
9. **Record retention.** Dispute files, investigation records, furnishing logs; documented schedule aligned to limitations periods and vendor contract.
10. **Minimum pilot process.** Verified-data-only gating, dispute workflow live before launch, suppression logic, and go/no-go criteria for next quarter.
