# Adopt Dual-Track Adverse-Action Notice Design for Limited Launch Planning (Fictional Internal Decision — Usability Experiment)

## Chosen path

Adopt the dual-track adverse-action notice design for limited launch planning only. Do not enable customer-facing decline paths until five gates are verified: (1) per-flow creditor, decision-maker, and FCRA report-user matrix; (2) conditional approval classified as counteroffer or final approval; (3) current federal and state requirements verified for [CONFIRM: launch states]; (4) versioned model/reason-code mapping approved; (5) ESIGN consent and delivery evidence confirmed, with postal fallback. Design elements: in-app message is interim and does not satisfy the written-notice requirement unless Legal verifies otherwise; written notice uses email only with confirmed ESIGN consent, otherwise postal mail; any Northstar Pay FCRA report-user role is analyzed separately from a partner's creditor notice.

## Rationale

The dual-track design preserves accurate principal reasons while protecting fraud controls, and the per-flow matrix prevents dual or missing notices in the bank-partner model. Planning-only scope lets Product build toward the holiday shopping window without exposing the company to notice risk. Assumptions (unverified): ECOA/Regulation B coverage; FCRA adverse-action duties when consumer-report information is used; bank/lending-partner structure with creditor varying by flow; ESIGN-compliant electronic delivery where consent is confirmed. Missing facts: product structure and creditor per flow, report user per decline type, state launch list, counteroffer classification, model inputs and reason-code mapping, ESIGN consent evidence, vendor/partner contract allocations. Unverified legal leads: Regulation B adverse action rules, FCRA §615(a) content and timing, ESIGN delivery requirements, and state notice overlays — all must be checked against current authoritative text before launch.

## Conditions

- Per-flow creditor, decision-maker, and FCRA report-user matrix confirmed
- Conditional approval classified as counteroffer or final approval on modified terms
- Current federal and state requirements verified for [CONFIRM: launch states]
- Versioned model/reason-code mapping approved by Legal
- ESIGN consent and delivery evidence confirmed, with postal mail fallback
