# Original Request

Northstar Pay wants to improve its application decision flow for customers who are not approved for financing. The business goal is to give customers useful information without exposing fraud controls or creating confusion about the decision. The proposed experience will display an approval, conditional approval, or decline message in real time. For declined applications, the app may show general reasons such as insufficient credit history, high existing obligations, or inability to verify identity. A written notice may also be sent by email or postal mail. The actors include Northstar Pay, its lending partners, customers, credit-reporting vendors, and customer support teams. Product wants to launch the new flow before the holiday shopping season.

Known facts are that decisions may use credit-report information, application data, bank-account information, fraud signals, and automated models. Some applications are declined by Northstar Pay, while others are declined by a lending partner. Open questions include who must issue the adverse action notice, when it must be delivered, what specific reasons are required, and how the company should handle conditional approvals and applications abandoned after a decision. Please advise on federal and state notice requirements, model-reason documentation, vendor responsibilities, and the exact customer communications needed for launch.
