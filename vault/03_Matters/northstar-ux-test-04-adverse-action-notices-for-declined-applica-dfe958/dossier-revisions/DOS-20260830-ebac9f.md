## Summary

Northstar Pay is redesigning its real-time application decision flow to show approvals, conditional approvals, and declines, with general decline reasons in-app and a written notice by email or mail. Decisions may be made by Northstar Pay or by lending partners, using credit data, application data, bank-account information, fraud signals, and automated models. Product wants to launch before the holiday shopping season. Counsel is involved to determine who must issue adverse action notices, when, with what reasons, and how to handle conditional approvals and abandoned applications.

## Decision question

Who must issue the adverse action notice for each decline path (Northstar Pay vs. lending partner), what reasons and timing are required under ECOA/Reg B and FCRA, and what exact customer communications must ship with the new flow before the holiday launch?

## Open questions

- Which entity (Northstar Pay or which lending partner) is the decision-maker for each product/state combination in the holiday launch?
- Are declines based on credit scores (triggering § 615(h) score disclosures) or other report data?
- Does "conditional approval" in the UX correspond to a counteroffer, an incomplete application, or a conditioned approval?
- What happens operationally when a customer abandons the application after a decision is rendered but before viewing it?
- Which states are included in the holiday launch scope?
