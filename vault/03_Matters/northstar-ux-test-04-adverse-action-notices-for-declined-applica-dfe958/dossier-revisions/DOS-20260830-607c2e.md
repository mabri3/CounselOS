## Summary

Northstar Pay is redesigning its real-time application decision flow (approve / conditional approve / decline) ahead of the holiday shopping season. Declines may be made by Northstar Pay or by a bank/lending partner, using credit-report data, application data, bank-account data, fraud signals, and automated models. Product wants to show general decline reasons in-app and send a written notice by email or postal mail. Counsel must determine who owes adverse action notices, what reasons are required, and how to protect fraud controls without sacrificing notice accuracy.

## Decision question

Who must issue the adverse action notice for each decline path (Northstar Pay vs. lending partner), what principal reasons and timing must appear in the in-app message and written notice, and can the new flow launch before the holiday season without exposing fraud controls or creating dual/missing-notice risk?

## Open questions

- Which entity is the creditor per flow (pay-in-4 vs. longer-term installments), and which entity pulls the consumer report and makes each decline type (credit, fraud, identity, partner)?
- Is "conditional approval" a counteroffer or a final approval on modified terms — this changes the Reg B notice duty?
- What is the ESIGN consent posture for email delivery of written notices, and does the in-app message count as the written notice or a courtesy message?
- What is the internal reason-code-to-principal-reason mapping, especially for fraud/identity declines?
- What are the state denial-notice overlays for the launch states (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA) and the longer-term installment state list?
