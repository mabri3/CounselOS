# # Vendor and partner responsibilities — lawyer response

The creditor and decision-maker must own the legally required notice for the action taken. A lender partner may generate the Regulation B notice for its credit decision. Northstar Pay may have a separate FCRA notice role when it obtains or relies on a consumer report for its own adverse action, including a fraud or identity screen. The operating design must prevent both a missing notice and an unexplained duplicate.

Credit-reporting vendors should supply accurate agency identity and contact fields, report data, and correction support under contract. They should not be treated as the legal decision-maker solely because they supply data. Contracts should allocate notice generation, reason-code delivery, audit access, retention, dispute routing, correction service levels, and incident escalation.

Missing facts: partner contracts, report-user roles, vendor identities, and dispute ownership. Unverified lead: the exact statutory allocation depends on current law and actual conduct. Required evidence: executed agreements, data-flow diagram, sample reports, notice payloads, and a RACI for issuance, records, disputes, and support.
