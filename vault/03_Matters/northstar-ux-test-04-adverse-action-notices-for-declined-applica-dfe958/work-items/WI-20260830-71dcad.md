# # Notice channel, consent, and timing — lawyer response

Use the real-time in-app message as an immediate, plain-language result and as a courtesy or interim message unless Legal confirms it satisfies the written-notice rules for the flow. Send the full written notice promptly and within the applicable federal and state deadlines. Use email only when the customer's ESIGN consent, access, and retention conditions are documented. Otherwise use postal mail to the verified address.

The control record must capture application completion, decision timestamp, notice deadline, issuer, principal reasons, report-user fields, channel, template version, generation time, delivery or mailing evidence, and any bounce or returned-mail follow-up. Customer abandonment after a decision must not remove the queued notice.

Missing facts: consent evidence, address source, state list, and exact timing rule by product. Unverified lead: the first-pass memo's federal and state timing references require current-text verification. Required evidence: consent record, template version, delivery logs, mailing proof, and support resend workflow.
