# # First-pass research review — lawyer response

I reviewed the internal first-pass research packet against the request, facts, and issue map. It identifies the key federal themes: Regulation B notice and principal-reason requirements, counteroffer and incomplete-application classification, FCRA report-user duties, electronic delivery, state overlays, and the dual-notice risk in a bank-partner model. It also proposes a useful dual-track design.

I edited the research memo to separate verified facts from assumptions and unverified legal leads. The memo now states that the in-app message should be treated as interim unless confirmed otherwise, that a report user can have a separate notice role from the creditor, and that a missing product fact must not result in a missing notice. The memo is useful for drafting but is not court-ready.

Open verification items remain: current authoritative text for the cited federal rules, the actual creditor and report-user matrix, launch-state overlays, ESIGN consent evidence, and model reason-code documentation. Completion of this review means the packet has been read and materially edited; it does not mean the law or product facts are finally verified.
