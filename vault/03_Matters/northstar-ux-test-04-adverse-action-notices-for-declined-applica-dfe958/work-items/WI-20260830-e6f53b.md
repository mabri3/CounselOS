# # Model reason-code mapping — lawyer response

Legal should approve a versioned mapping from each model or rule output to the principal customer-facing reason that actually drove the decision. The record should include the product and state, model or rule version, input or output that controlled the outcome, priority when multiple reasons apply, effective date, approving owner, customer wording, support wording, and whether a report was used.

Candidate mappings are placeholders only: thin or absent credit file → “insufficient credit history”; high existing obligations → “your existing debt obligations are too high”; identity failure → “we were unable to verify your identity”; fraud or verification signal → “we were unable to verify the information in your application,” only if that is the truthful principal reason. Do not expose thresholds, scores, vendor names, or detection rules. Do not replace an accurate reason with a vague catch-all.

Status: pending model evidence and legal approval. Missing facts: actual model inputs, reason codes, priority rules, overrides, and report use. Required evidence: model card, reason dictionary, sample decision traces, mapping approval, and regression test showing the notice reason matches the recorded driver.
