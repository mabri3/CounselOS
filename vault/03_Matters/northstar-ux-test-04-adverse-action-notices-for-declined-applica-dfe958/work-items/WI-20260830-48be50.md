# # Conditional approval and abandonment — lawyer response

Status: Pending product classification. A conditional approval that changes amount, price, term, or another material term should be treated as a possible counteroffer until Product and Legal document that the flow is a final approval on modified terms. The notice and acceptance path must match that classification.

For incomplete applications, distinguish missing information from a completed application that is declined. For abandonment before a decision, record the product's incomplete/withdrawn treatment. For abandonment after a decision, preserve the decision event, approved principal reason(s), issuer, deadline, channel, and delivery evidence; the customer's exit must not silently cancel the notice process. Silence after a counteroffer requires the applicable counteroffer handling.

Assumptions and unverified leads: the exact Regulation B classification and timing rules need verification against current authoritative text and product facts. Required evidence: product state machine, terms-difference matrix, acceptance/expiry rules, and event-log samples. Do not ship conditional approval copy until this classification is resolved.
