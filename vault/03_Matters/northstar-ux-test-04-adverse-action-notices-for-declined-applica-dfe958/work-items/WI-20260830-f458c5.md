# # Decision-maker and product structure — lawyer response

Status: Pending confirmation. The intake does not identify the legal creditor, the report user, or the notice issuer for each product and state.

Working matrix: for each pay-in-4 and longer-term installment flow, Product must identify (1) creditor, (2) entity that makes the credit decision, (3) entity that runs or relies on the consumer report, (4) entity that runs identity or fraud screening, (5) notice issuer, (6) notice deadline, and (7) owner of evidence and disputes. Northstar Pay and the lending partner must remain separate entries until contracts and operating evidence confirm the roles.

Assumption: a lending partner may be creditor and Northstar Pay may still be a separate report user for a fraud or identity decision. This is not verified. Required evidence: executed partner agreement, product flow diagram, data-flow diagram, state list, and sample notice payload. Do not enable the flow with a blank issuer or an unresolved dual-notice path.
