# # Working Recommendation — Launch design

## Recommendation

Adopt a dual-track customer communication design. Show a clear real-time in-app result with accurate, approved principal reason(s). Then send the complete written notice through email only when valid ESIGN consent and access/retention procedures are confirmed; otherwise send by postal mail. Treat the in-app message as interim or courtesy communication unless Legal verifies that it is the required written notice for the specific product and channel.

Use a per-flow control matrix. For every product, state, outcome, and decision path, record the creditor, decision-maker, consumer-report user, notice issuer, report source, approved reason code(s), applicable deadline, delivery channel, evidence owner, and support/dispute owner. Keep Northstar Pay and each lending partner as separate possible issuers until the facts confirm otherwise. If Northstar Pay independently uses a consumer report for a fraud or identity decision, analyze its own FCRA notice duty even when a lending partner issues the creditor's Regulation B notice.

## Launch conditions

Before enabling the new decline flow, Product and each lending partner should confirm the matrix; Legal should approve a versioned mapping from model/rules output to truthful principal reasons; Product and Legal should classify conditional approval as counteroffer or final approval on modified terms; Compliance should verify the launch-state overlays; and Operations should test generation, delivery or mailing, retention, and post-decision abandonment handling.

The notice package should never use a vague catch-all or an invented reason. It should not expose fraud thresholds, scores, or investigative rules. Support should use the same approved reason family as the notice. A customer who leaves after a decision should still enter the notice workflow, with the deadline and issuer logged at decision time.

## Risk posture

Proceed to limited drafting and implementation planning with clearly marked placeholders. Do not enable a customer-facing decline path while the creditor or notice issuer is unknown, the principal reason is missing, electronic-consent evidence is unconfirmed, or the counteroffer classification is unresolved. Missing facts are launch dependencies, not a reason to stop useful preparation.

## Evidence status

This recommendation is based on the intake, the edited facts, issue map, and edited first-pass research memo. The Regulation B, FCRA, ESIGN, model-reason, and state-specific references remain unverified legal leads in this run. They must be checked against current authoritative text and actual product evidence before launch. This record is a recommendation only and is not a recorded decision.
