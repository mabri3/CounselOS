# Issue Map

1. **Authorization design.** What information must be shown before a customer authorizes API access, and when must that permission be renewed?
2. **Migration.** Can existing connections continue until technical reauthentication, or must customers make a new affirmative choice before data continues to flow?
3. **Purpose limits.** Which uses are necessary for the customer-selected product, and which uses require a separate optional choice?
4. **Marketing and underwriting.** Should historical transaction data be used to build cross-product offer segments or lending inputs?
5. **Revocation and deletion.** What must stop when a customer disconnects, and what information may be retained for disputes, fraud, legal duties, or model evidence?
6. **Regulatory change.** Which design choices remain useful even while the federal section 1033 rule and compliance dates may change?
7. **Aggregator contract.** Are security, incident notice, subcontractor, purpose, deletion, audit, and transition-assistance terms sufficient?
8. **Customer support.** Can the company explain connection status, permission, refresh failure, and deletion without sending the customer to the aggregator?
