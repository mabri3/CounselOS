# First-Pass Research Packet

## Question

What material source-based issues should counsel research for Northstar UX Test — 09 — Servicing and Collections Contact Strategy?

## Working Analysis

## Matter summary

Northstar Pay (Product team) wants to redesign servicing and collections communications for customers with missed payments, testing over the next eight weeks. The flow uses app notifications, email, SMS, and possibly phone calls, offering payment links, hardship forms, and payment-date changes; persistently delinquent accounts may go to a third-party collections vendor. Lending partners, servicing agents, and collections vendors participate. Legal (Brian Harris) must advise on communication rules, vendor oversight, escalation, and product controls before the test launches.

## Decision question

Which communication rules, contact-frequency limits, required notices, consent and call-recording requirements, and vendor-oversight controls must Northstar Pay build into the eight-week delinquency-contact test so it improves repayment without violating federal and state collections, TCPA, FDCPA, and lending-partner requirements — and at what delinquency point may an account be placed with a third-party collector?

## Open questions

- Which entity (Northstar Pay vs. the bank/lending partner) is the creditor/owner of the delinquent account in each state, and does that change who is a "debt collector" under the FDCPA?
- What consent language and records exist for each channel (app push, email, SMS, calls), and does TCPA prior express consent cover collections messaging?
- Which states are in scope for the test, since quiet hours, contact-frequency limits, recording consent (one-party vs. all-party), and required notices vary by state?
- What do the lending-partner agreements say about servicing standards, escalation timing, and third-party placement authority?
- What hardship and payment-date-change policies apply, and do they trigger loss-mitigation or Regulation B/FCU notice obligations?

## Likely rules and issues

**Federal**
- **FDCPA (15 U.S.C. § 1692)** — applies directly if Northstar Pay or its vendor is a "debt collector"; even first-party creditors generally follow its conduct standards. Key provisions: § 1692c (call frequency, 8 a.m.–9 p.m. local time, third-party disclosure, employer contact restrictions, cease-communication requests), § 1692d (harassment), § 1692e (false or misleading representations, including misrepresenting consequences of nonpayment), § 1692f (unfair practices), § 1692g (validation notice within 5 days of initial communication).
- **CFPB Regulation F (12 C.F.R. Part 1006)** — implements FDCPA: call-frequency limit (7 calls per 7 days per debt, per § 1006.14(b)); limited-content message rules for voicemail (§ 1006.2(j)); validation notice content and timing; electronic communication rules; passive debt sales exemption.
- **TCPA (47 U.S.C. § 227)** — prior express consent for autodialed/prerecorded calls and texts to cell phones; revocation via reasonable means; DNC rules; significant per-call statutory damages.
- **CFPB UDAAP authority** — misleading "pay now or else" messaging, misrepresenting credit reporting, or obscuring hardship options.
- **ECOA/Regulation B** — if hardship or payment-date changes constitute loss mitigation or workout arrangements, adverse-action and notice rules may apply.
- **FCRA** — accuracy of delinquency reporting during disputes; Metro 2 compliance; dispute-handling duties.
- **Call recording** — federal one-party consent (18 U.S.C. § 2511) baseline; state all-party consent states (e.g., California, Washington) require all-party consent for recording.

**State**
- State mini-FDCPAs (often stricter than federal, sometimes applying to creditors directly, e.g., California Rosenthal Act, New York, Texas, Washington).
- State licensing requirements for third-party collectors and for the entity servicing/placing debt.
- State-specific quiet hours, contact caps, and required written notices before placement.
- Language-access requirements (e.g., New York, California) for servicing communications.

**Contract/Partner**
- Lending-partner agreements: who services, who may place with third parties, notice to partner before placement, servicing standards.
- Collections vendor agreement: FDCPA/Reg F compliance reps, audit rights, data-security terms, permissible data fields shared.

**Data/Privacy**
- Permissible account information in messages (avoid third-party disclosure on shared phone numbers; limited-content message rules).
- Disputed contact information: handling cease-and-desist, wrong-number, and disputed-debt flags before further contact.

## Viable paths

1. **Scope the test to first-party, early-delinquency communications only** (before third-party placement), with Reg F-compliant frequency caps, quiet hours, limited-content message templates, and validated consent records per channel. Defer phone calls and third-party placement to a later phase. Lowest risk; fastest to launch within 8 weeks.
2. **Full test including calls and third-party placement**, but only after: state-by-state consent/recording matrix, vendor due diligence and contract addendum, validation-notice workflow, and partner sign-off. Higher lift but tests the complete strategy.
3. **Hybrid**: launch channels 1–3 (app, email, SMS) now with strict caps; run phone and placement workstreams in parallel for a phase-2 gate at week 4.

## Facts that could change the answer

- Whether Northstar Pay is the creditor or a servicer/debt collector for the delinquent accounts (changes FDCPA/Reg F applicability and validation-notice duty).
- Actual consent language and channel-level opt-in records (changes TCPA exposure for SMS/calls).
- Whether any state in the live footprint (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA) is an all-party recording state and whether calls are recorded (changes recording controls).
- Whether hardship/payment-date changes are structured as loss mitigation (changes Reg B/notice analysis).
- Whether the lending-partner agreement reserves placement authority to the partner (changes escalation design).

## Recommended last-mile verification

- Confirm the state list for the test and pull state mini-FDCPA, recording-consent, and licensing rules for each (unverified — no state-specific sources in this workspace yet).
- Verify current TCPA consent language in the customer agreement and app flows against planned collections messaging.
- Review the collections vendor contract and lending-partner agreement for placement authority, audit rights, and compliance reps.
- Confirm Reg F validation-notice templates and limited-content message scripts with the servicing team.
- Confirm with Product which channels are actually in the 8-week test before finalizing the control set.
