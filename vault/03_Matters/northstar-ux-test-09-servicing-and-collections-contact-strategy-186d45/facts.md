# Known Facts

- Intake response: yes (confirmed the orientation reflects the request).
- Product wants to redesign servicing and collections communications for customers with missed payments, tested over eight weeks.
- Proposed channels: app notifications, email, text messages, and possibly phone calls.
- Customer options in the flow: payment link, hardship-request form, payment-date change.
- Accounts that remain delinquent may be transferred to a third-party collections vendor.
- Participants: Northstar Pay, lending partners, customers, servicing agents, collections vendors.
- Known customer-base facts: customers may have multiple loans, shared phone numbers, language preferences, or disputed contact information.
- The team is considering automated contact limits, quiet hours, employer-contact restrictions, and early- vs. late-delinquency messaging tiers.
- Open questions raised by Product: consent requirements, contact-frequency limits, required collection notices, permissible account information in messages, call-recording rules, and the point at which an account may be placed with a third party.

## Assumptions

- The bank or lending partner is the creditor of record in at least some configurations; the responsible entity for communications may differ by product and state. Partner map still being finalized.
- The test runs in the currently live pay-in-4 states (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA) unless Product says otherwise.
- Third-party placement and call recording are "possible" but not confirmed as in-scope for the test.
