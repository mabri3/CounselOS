# Original Request

Northstar Pay wants to redesign its servicing and collections communications for customers with missed payments. The business goal is to improve repayment rates while helping customers understand their options and avoid unnecessary escalation. The proposed flow will send app notifications, email, text messages, and possibly phone calls. Customers may receive a payment link, a hardship-request form, or an option to change a payment date. Accounts that remain delinquent may be transferred to a third-party collections vendor. Northstar Pay, lending partners, customers, servicing agents, and collections vendors will participate. Product wants to test the new strategy over the next eight weeks.

Known facts are that customers may have multiple loans, shared phone numbers, language preferences, or disputed contact information. The team is considering automated contact limits, quiet hours, employer-contact restrictions, and different messaging for early and late delinquency. Open questions include consent requirements, contact-frequency limits, required collection notices, permissible account information in messages, call-recording rules, and the point at which an account may be placed with a third party. Please advise on the communication rules, vendor oversight, escalation process, and product controls needed for the test.
