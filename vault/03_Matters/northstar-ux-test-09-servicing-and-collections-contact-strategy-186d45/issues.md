# Issues

## 1. Consent and channel rules (TCPA)
SMS and prerecorded/artificial-voice calls to wireless numbers require prior express consent; scope of origination consent matters; revocation must be honored; quiet hours 8 a.m.–9 p.m. local time as federal floor, stricter in some states.

## 2. FDCPA-style conduct and state extensions
Northstar as creditor is likely not an FDCPA "debt collector," but the collections vendor is; state statutes (e.g., California Rosenthal) may extend FDCPA duties to first-party collectors. Content limits: no false/misleading statements, no unfair practices, no third-party disclosure, no employer contact.

## 3. Validation notices and dispute handling
Validation notice generally required within five days of initial communication by a debt collector; disputes must pause escalation pending verification; disputed contact information must be suppressed.

## 4. Contact frequency and harassment
No bright-line federal cap; multi-channel daily contact invites harassment claims. Need per-channel and aggregate caps with early/late delinquency tiers; dedupe across multiple loans.

## 5. Permissible message content
Identify sender; avoid disclosing debt status to third parties who may see the device; avoid overshadowing validation/dispute rights; secure, branded payment links that do not leak account data.

## 6. Call recording
All-party-consent states (including CA and WA, both live) require consent before recording; obtain consent prompt or disable recording by state.

## 7. Third-party placement and vendor oversight
Documented placement trigger (delinquency threshold, post-validation, post-hardship-review); written vendor agreement with FDCPA/FCRA/GLBA reps, audit rights, complaint monitoring, chain-of-attorney rules, state licensing/registration checks.

## 8. Customer options and servicing obligations
Hardship requests should pause escalation pending review; payment-date changes may implicate modification/re-aging rules; partner workflow confirmation needed.

## 9. Responsible entity by partner/state
Partner map determines who must send required notices and how vendor oversight flows; open implementation fact to confirm before launch.
