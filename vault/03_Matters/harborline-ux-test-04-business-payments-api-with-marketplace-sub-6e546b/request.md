# Original Request

Harborline wants to offer an API that lets software platforms accept customer payments and direct funds to their participating small businesses. A platform would create a Harborline profile for each business, submit onboarding data through the API, display payment status in its own interface, and instruct Harborline to allocate card or ACH proceeds among the business, the platform, and Harborline’s fees. Funds would move through accounts held by our banking partner. Harborline would manage payment processing, risk controls, reserves, returns, and support escalations. We want a limited beta in four months. Known facts are that platforms will not be permitted to hold customer funds directly and that Harborline will contract with each participating business. We have not decided whether funds will be held in a pooled custodial account or individual accounts, whether platforms may control refunds, or how long reserves may be held.

Please advise whether this structure creates money-transmission, payment-facilitator, funds-transfer, agency, or stored-value obligations for Harborline or the platforms. We need legal’s decision on the acceptable funds flow, required account titles and agreements, who may issue payment instructions, and whether the platform can deduct its fee before settlement. Please also identify required disclosures, licensing assumptions, BSA/AML allocation, error-resolution duties, return and chargeback responsibilities, and facts that must be confirmed with the partner bank and card or ACH participants before product and engineering finalize the architecture.
