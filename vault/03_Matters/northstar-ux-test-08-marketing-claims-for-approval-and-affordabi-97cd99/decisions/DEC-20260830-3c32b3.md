# Cycle 8 — Fictional Internal Decision — Northstar Pay Marketing Claims

## Chosen path

Approve Path A only — a product/state/partner-gated campaign. Permitted at launch: qualified "pay in four" and careful budget-planning language only. Modified: "no interest" becomes a product-gated statement only after M3/M4 and partner confirmation; "easy approval" becomes "quick to apply" or "see if you qualify" only after M2 support. Held: "build your credit" until M1 confirms reporting by product, state, partner, and agency. Fallback: use only qualified pay-in-four and budget-planning language if M1/M2 data is late.

## Rationale

Path A supports the checkout/app growth objective and builds reusable controls. The material risks are routing errors and unsupported claims. Fictional internal usability-test decision — not a real approval.
