# Formal Recommendation — Northstar Pay Marketing Campaign Claims

Privileged & Confidential — Attorney Work Product

To: Brian Harris (Legal Owner)
Re: Recommended approval path for the proposed marketing campaign
Status: Recommendation for decision. No final decision is recorded.

## 1. Recommendation

Approve **Path A: a product-, state-, and partner-gated launch**, subject to the conditions below.

- **Launch-ready now:** qualified "pay in four" messaging and carefully framed budget-planning language ("more control over your budget"), each with same-view qualifiers.
- **Modified wording required:** "no interest" (limited to "no interest on eligible pay-in-four purchases," gated by product and partner confirmation) and "easy approval" (replaced with "quick to apply" / "see if you qualify," supported by approval-rate data).
- **Held:** "build your credit" until the credit-reporting partner/state/product map (M1) is confirmed.
- **Week-1 data gates:** M1 (reporting map), M2 (approval-rate data), M3 (late-fee schedules), M4 (partner confirmation of pay-in-four interest terms), M5 (payment-example and card-comparison data).
- **Fallback:** if M1 or M2 is not confirmed by end of week 1, launch with the low-risk claim set only (qualified pay-in-four and budget-planning language) and phase in gated claims later. No claim is added informally mid-campaign.

## 2. Business reason

Path A is the only path that serves the stated objective — increasing use at merchant checkout and in the standalone app — because approval ease and credit building are the primary conversion drivers for BNPL adoption. It also builds the claims matrix as durable infrastructure: each future state or product launch updates the matrix rather than restarting the analysis. The one-month timeline is feasible if partner and operations data arrive in week 1.

## 3. Legal risk

- **Moderate and manageable.** Risk concentrates in execution: creative must be correctly routed to the right states and products, and the matrix must be accurate. A mis-routed ad (e.g., "build your credit" shown in a non-reporting state) is live deceptive-claim exposure.
- **Mitigations:** channel-level geo/product gating, pre-launch QA spot checks, mandatory same-view qualifiers, and the publication gate (no channel goes live without matrix signoff, complete evidence file, and Legal approval of final creative).
- **Residual risk:** unverified legal leads (U1–U7) mean the underlying standards (FTC substantiation, Reg Z trigger terms, Endorsement Guides, FCRA, state guidance, typical-APR source) have not been last-mile verified in this run.

## 4. Conditions of approval

1. Claims matrix completed and signed off by Legal before any channel goes live.
2. Required qualifier appears in the same user view as the claim in every channel; character limits never excuse a missing qualifier.
3. Evidence/substantiation file complete for each claim before publication.
4. Influencer agreements in place: approved language, material-connection disclosure, pre-approval of scripts/posts, monitoring, takedown rights.
5. Merchants and agencies submit co-branded materials for approval and use only approved versions.
6. Week-1 data gates met for any claim beyond the launch-ready set; fallback applies if late.
7. Last-mile verification of unverified legal leads (U1–U7) before the memo is treated as a final legal conclusion.
