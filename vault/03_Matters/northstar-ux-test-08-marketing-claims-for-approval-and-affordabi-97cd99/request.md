# Original Request

Northstar Pay’s marketing team wants to launch a campaign describing the service as a simple and flexible way to pay over time. The business goal is to increase use of Northstar Pay in merchant checkout and the standalone app.

Proposed claims include “pay in four,” “no interest,” “easy approval,” “build your credit,” and “more control over your budget.” The campaign will use paid search, social media, merchant-site banners, email, app screens, and influencer content.

The actors include Northstar Pay marketing, merchants, agencies, influencers, customers, and lending partners. The campaign is scheduled to begin in one month.

Known facts are that not every customer qualifies, late fees may apply in some situations, credit reporting may not be available for every product, and longer-term loans may carry interest. The team also wants to display representative payment examples and compare Northstar Pay with credit cards.

Open questions include substantiation standards, required qualifiers, prominence and proximity of disclosures, influencer controls, merchant approval of co-branded materials, and whether claims may be used across states and products.

Please review the proposed claims and advise which statements are permitted, which require modification, and what compliance process should apply before publication.
