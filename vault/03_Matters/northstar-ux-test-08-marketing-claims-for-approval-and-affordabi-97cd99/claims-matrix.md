# Claims Matrix — Path A (Product/State-Gated)

**Matter:** Northstar UX Test — 08 — Marketing Claims for Approval and Affordability
**Status:** Draft for legal review — not yet approved for publication
**Purpose:** Gate each proposed claim by product, state, and partner; specify permitted/prohibited wording, required qualifiers, and evidence needed before any channel goes live.

**Legend:** ⚠ = assumption / missing fact / unverified legal lead — see Assumptions & Missing Facts section.

---

## 1. Claim Matrix

### Claim 1: "Pay in four"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Permitted with qualification |
| **Permitted wording** | "Pay in four interest-free payments" (pay-in-4 only); "Split your purchase into 4 payments" |
| **Prohibited wording** | Any use adjacent to or promoting longer-term installment products; "pay over time" used interchangeably with pay-in-4; implying availability in all states |
| **Product/state/partner gate** | Pay-in-4 only; live states only (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA) ⚠; per-state partner terms may vary fee structure ⚠ |
| **Qualifier** | Payment schedule (4 payments, dates), late-fee disclosure where fees apply, "not available in all states" if campaign could reach excluded states |
| **Evidence needed** | Current pay-in-4 product terms; state availability list; late-fee schedule by state |
| **Channel examples** | Paid search: "Pay in 4 at checkout — see terms"; app screen: payment schedule shown before acceptance; influencer: must use approved script, no improvisation on terms |

### Claim 2: "No interest"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Modify — high risk without gating |
| **Permitted wording** | "No interest on pay-in-4 purchases"; "0% interest on 4-payment plans" |
| **Prohibited wording** | Unqualified "no interest" in any campaign that also promotes longer-term products; "no interest, ever" |
| **Product/state/partner gate** | Pay-in-4 only; must never appear in creative promoting longer-term installments (which may carry interest) |
| **Qualifier** | "Late fees may apply"; product identifier ("on pay-in-4 purchases") in same visual field as the claim |
| **Evidence needed** | Written confirmation from each lending partner that pay-in-4 carries no interest in every live state ⚠ |
| **Channel examples** | Banner: "0% interest on pay-in-4*" with asterisk to fee disclosure in same view; email: interest statement and fee disclosure in same section, not footer |

### Claim 3: "Easy approval"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Modify — high risk |
| **Permitted wording** | "Quick to apply"; "See if you qualify in seconds"; "Simple application" |
| **Prohibited wording** | "Easy approval"; "Everyone gets approved"; "Guaranteed approval"; "No credit check" (unless verified true for all products/states ⚠) |
| **Product/state/partner gate** | All products, all live states — but wording must reflect actual approval rates ⚠ |
| **Qualifier** | "Approval is not guaranteed. Eligibility requirements apply." Proximate to the claim, same screen/view |
| **Evidence needed** | Approval-rate data by product and state from operations ⚠; underwriting criteria summary from partners ⚠ |
| **Channel examples** | Paid search: "Apply in seconds — eligibility requirements apply"; influencer: prohibited from describing their approval experience as guaranteed or typical |

### Claim 4: "Build your credit"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Modify — highest risk; do not run until gated |
| **Permitted wording** | "On-time payments may be reported to credit bureaus" (only where reporting is live); "Build credit with on-time payments" (only in confirmed reporting states/products) |
| **Prohibited wording** | Unqualified "build your credit"; any claim in states/products where reporting is not enabled; "improve your credit score" (outcome promise) |
| **Product/state/partner gate** | **Blocked pending the credit-reporting partner/state matrix** ⚠ — claim may run only in confirmed product/state/partner combinations where reporting is live |
| **Qualifier** | "On-time payments don't guarantee a credit score improvement. Late or missed payments may hurt your credit. Reporting not available for all products or in all states." |
| **Evidence needed** | Partner/state reporting matrix (decisive missing fact); FCRA accuracy and dispute-handling confirmation from reporting vendor ⚠ |
| **Channel examples** | App screen: reporting disclosure at point of claim; social: only in geo-targeted placements matching reporting states; influencer: prohibited from making credit-building claims unless pre-approved script |

### Claim 5: "More control over your budget"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Permitted with care |
| **Permitted wording** | "More control over your budget"; "Choose how you split your payments" |
| **Prohibited wording** | Affordability implications: "makes purchases affordable"; "for when money is tight"; targeting financially distressed consumers; suggesting the product is a solution to financial difficulty |
| **Product/state/partner gate** | All products, all live states |
| **Qualifier** | None mandatory, but must not be paired with affordability framing; standard fee/date disclosures still apply to the surrounding creative |
| **Evidence needed** | None beyond standard product terms; substantiation file notes claim is descriptive of payment-scheduling feature |
| **Channel examples** | Banner: "More control over your budget — 4 set payments, dates shown upfront"; email: avoid pairing with debt-relief or cash-flow-struggle framing |

## 2. Representative Payment Examples

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Permitted with controls |
| **Requirements** | Example must be representative of typical transactions (not best-case); show total purchase amount, each payment amount, payment dates or intervals, any fees, and any interest for longer-term products; label as "example" |
| **Prohibited** | Cherry-picked low-fee or atypical examples; examples that omit late fees where they commonly apply |
| **Evidence needed** | Typical transaction data from operations to confirm representativeness ⚠ |
| **Channel examples** | Merchant banner: "Example: $200 purchase → 4 payments of $50, due every 2 weeks. Late fees may apply."; app: full schedule before acceptance |

## 3. Credit-Card Comparisons

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Permitted with substantiation |
| **Requirements** | Comparison must use current, typical credit-card APR figures from an identified, citable source; compare against actual Northstar Pay terms for the same purchase type; state the basis of comparison |
| **Prohibited** | Comparisons using outlier card APRs; comparing pay-in-4 against a card carrying a balance long-term without disclosing the different structures; implying cards are always worse |
| **Evidence needed** | Current typical-APR source (e.g., Federal Reserve G.19 or similar — **unverified lead** ⚠); internal total-cost model |
| **Channel examples** | Email: "A typical credit card APR is ~21% (source, date). Pay-in-4: 0% interest, late fees may apply." |

## 4. Channel Disclosure Standards (all claims)

- **Proximity:** qualifier must appear in the same visual field/screen as the claim — not behind a link, asterisk-only, or a separate page.
- **Paid search:** character limits are not an excuse; if the qualifier doesn't fit, the claim doesn't run.
- **Social/influencer:** material-connection disclosure (#ad) plus approved claim language; no claim improvisation.
- **Email/app:** disclosure in the same section as the claim, not the footer.
- **Merchant banners:** only pre-approved creative; merchants contractually prohibited from modifying claims or disclosures.

## 5. Influencer Controls

- Written agreement with: approved claims list, prohibited claims list, mandatory disclosure language, pre-approval of all scripts/posts, monitoring obligation, and takedown rights.
- Influencers may not make credit-building, approval-guarantee, or comparison claims outside approved scripts.
- **Unverified legal lead:** FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) placement/adequacy standards for social disclosures ⚠.

## 6. Merchant Approvals

- All co-branded materials submitted to Northstar Pay legal before publication.
- Merchant agreements must require use of approved creative only and prohibit claim modification.
- **Missing fact:** current merchant agreement approval workflow and turnaround time ⚠.
