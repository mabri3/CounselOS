## Beacon: Instant Business Onboarding — Launch Review

**Request:** Remove manual review for low-risk business applicants, launch next sprint, and route non-approvals to "contact support."

### What's in place (strengths)
- **Scoped segment.** The change is limited to low-risk applicants, which narrows the exposure and is the right way to pilot an automated flow.
- **Correct issue framing already underway.** The matter has already identified the three material questions (legal characterization, notice/explanation on non-approval, partner approval constraint) and drafted a clarifying email to the PM.
- **Reasonable fallback intent.** Routing non-approvals to support is a sensible human-touch safety valve — *if* the message and process satisfy applicable notice rules.

### Material gaps (blockers to a green-light)
1. **Undefined decision type.** The single biggest unknown: is this flow making an **identity/fraud** decision, a **deposit-account eligibility** decision, or a **credit** decision? This determines whether ECOA/Reg B adverse-action notice and FCRA rules attach. The facts file flags this as unconfirmed.
2. **Fallback copy not confirmed.** A bare "contact support" message may not satisfy adverse-action notice requirements if the flow is making a credit or eligibility decision. The exact customer-facing copy is still an open checkbox.
3. **Partner approval unconfirmed.** Whether a partner's change-control/approval process constrains the rollout is still an open work item.

### Recommendation: **Go with conditions**

The direction is sound and appropriately scoped, but it is not ready to green-light for next sprint until three conditions are met:

1. **Confirm the decision type** (fraud vs. deposit eligibility vs. credit) and obtain the decisioning spec.
2. **Provide the exact fallback copy** and confirm it meets adverse-action notice requirements if applicable.
3. **Confirm no partner approval** is required, or obtain it.

These map to the two open work items and the two open fact checkboxes already on the matter. The drafted email to the PM is the right next step to close them.
