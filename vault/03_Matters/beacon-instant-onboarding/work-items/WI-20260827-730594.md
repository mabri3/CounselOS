# Clarify which manual review step is being removed

Ask the submitter (Product Manager) to specify which manual review step the Beacon flow removes. The request says "remove manual review for low-risk business applicants," but the legal characterization and notice obligations differ materially depending on whether the removed step is (a) identity/fraud screening, (b) deposit-account eligibility decisioning, (c) credit decisioning, or (d) a partner-required manual review. This gates the ECOA/Reg B, FCRA, and partner-obligation analysis.
