# Confirm decisioning scope and non-approval fallback for Beacon onboarding

Confirm what the automated flow actually decides and what applicants see when it cannot approve them. Two open facts gate the legal characterization and notice analysis: (1) whether the flow decides credit or deposit-account eligibility (vs. only identity/fraud screening), and (2) the exact customer-facing fallback message. If the decision is a credit/eligibility decision, a bare "contact support" message may not satisfy adverse-action notice obligations (ECOA/Reg B, FCRA). Obtain the decisioning spec and the exact fallback copy from Product, and confirm whether any partner agreement requires manual review for this segment.
