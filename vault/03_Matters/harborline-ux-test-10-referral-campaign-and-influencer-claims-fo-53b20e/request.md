# Original Request

Harborline wants to accelerate business-account growth through a referral program and paid creator campaign. Existing customers would receive a cash reward when a referred business opens an account and completes qualifying payment volume. Financial-education creators would demonstrate the Harborline dashboard, describe fast onboarding and payment features, and compare the experience with common fintech account and payment products. Marketing wants to launch in six weeks. Harborline would supply talking points but creators could use their own words. Known facts are that the deposit account is provided by our banking partner, rewards will be reported in the account interface, and creators will have written sponsorship agreements. Missing facts include the reward amount, qualifying period, creator review process, whether testimonials will discuss earnings or approval speed, and whether paid social platforms will create lookalike audiences from customer lists.

Please advise on the disclosures, substantiation, endorsement, bank-partnership, deposit-insurance, privacy, anti-spam, and state incentive rules that apply. We need legal’s decision on permissible claims about account approval, payment speed, fees, safety, and deposit insurance; the required placement and wording of sponsorship and referral disclosures; and whether Harborline must preapprove every creator post. Please also assess use of customer lists for audience matching, conditions for paying referral rewards, treatment of self-referrals or related businesses, and the monitoring and recordkeeping program needed after launch.
