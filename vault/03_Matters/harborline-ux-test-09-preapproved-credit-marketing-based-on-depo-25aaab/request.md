# Original Request

Harborline’s growth team wants to market a partner-issued credit product to consumer account holders whose deposit history suggests steady income and sufficient cash flow. Harborline would analyze payroll deposits, average balances, overdraft history, and account tenure to select customers. Selected users would see an in-app banner stating that they are “preapproved,” with a proposed limit range, and could complete a short application with the issuing partner. Harborline would receive a marketing fee for successful originations. The team wants to test the campaign next month. We know that the partner, not Harborline, would make the final credit decision. We have not determined whether the selection model is a firm offer of credit, whether a credit report will be used, how declined applicants will be handled, or whether deposit data will be shared before the customer clicks.

Please decide whether Harborline may use account data for this campaign and whether “preapproved” is accurate. We need advice on Fair Credit Reporting Act, Equal Credit Opportunity Act, financial privacy, state privacy, marketing-consent, and unfair-practices requirements. Please specify whether the offer must include firm-offer disclosures, whether customers need a way to opt out of this use of transaction data, and what fair-lending testing is necessary. We also need approved boundaries for the partner data exchange, the in-app wording, audience exclusions, record retention, and the distinction between Harborline’s recommendation and the partner’s credit decision.
