# Original Request

Harborline Financial, Inc. wants to reduce abandonment in consumer account onboarding before our October growth campaign. We propose a mobile flow in which an applicant enters identity details, consents to verification, and receives provisional access to the app while our banking partner completes customer identification and sanctions screening. During this provisional period, the customer could view account details and receive an incoming ACH credit, but could not send money, use a virtual card, or withdraw funds. Harborline would operate the interface and customer support. Our banking partner would open and hold the account. A third-party vendor would verify identity documents, device data, and government records. Known facts are that all applicants will be U.S. residents, the partner bank must approve our customer identification program procedures, and failed sanctions checks will prevent account opening. We have not settled the maximum provisional period, the permitted deposit amount, how to handle inconclusive identity results, or whether the account legally exists before final approval.

Please advise whether this provisional-access model is permissible under customer identification, BSA/AML, sanctions, funds-availability, and consumer disclosure rules. We need a decision on which functions can be available before verification is complete, whether receiving an ACH credit creates unacceptable risk, and what status language the interface must use so that applicants do not believe they have a fully active bank account. Please also identify the facts we must resolve with the partner bank and verification vendor before launch, including any controls or contract terms that should be mandatory.
