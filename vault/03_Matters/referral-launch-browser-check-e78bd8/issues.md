# Issues

## Business objective
Marketing wants to launch a customer referral feature next month. The reward terms and eligible regions are not final.

## Material legal issues

### 1. Reward terms (what the referrer/referred party gets)
- **What is the reward?** Cash, credit, discount, or other value. Cash-equivalent rewards can implicate different rules than service credits.
- **Who is rewarded?** Referrer only, referred party only, or both (double-sided).
- **Conditions to earn the reward** (e.g., referred party must complete a purchase, maintain an account for X days, or remain active). These conditions drive whether the reward is a "bounty" or a discount and affect disclosure and accounting treatment.
- **Reward caps / anti-abuse limits** (e.g., per-user caps, self-referral prohibitions, fraud controls).

### 2. Eligible regions (where the program runs)
- **Which jurisdictions are in scope?** The company profile is US-only, but "eligible regions" implies a possible multi-state or international footprint.
- **State-specific referral / anti-rebate rules** (e.g., insurance, financial services, and certain licensed industries restrict referral fees).
- **Tax treatment of rewards** — rewards may be taxable income to the recipient; disclosure and 1099 reporting may apply.
- **Consumer protection / unfair-deceptive-acts (UDTPA) exposure** — reward terms must be clear, not misleading, and not contingent on undisclosed conditions.

### 3. Terms of service / program rules
- Referral program terms need to be drafted and linked from the feature.
- Eligibility exclusions, reward redemption mechanics, expiration, and dispute handling.

### 4. Data / privacy
- Referral necessarily involves the referrer providing a third party's contact information. Confirm this is consistent with the company's data practices (business contact data) and any consent/notice obligations.

## Open questions that change the path
- [ ] What is the reward type and amount?
- [ ] Which regions are eligible (US-only vs. international)?
- [ ] Is the reward double-sided (referrer + referred)?
- [ ] What conditions must be met before a reward vests?
