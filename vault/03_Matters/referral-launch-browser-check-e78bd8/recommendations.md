# Recommendations

## Recommended path

### 1. Lock the two open variables first
The reward terms and eligible regions are the gating facts. Everything else (terms drafting, disclosures, tax/1099, state-specific review) depends on them. Get Product/Marketing to commit to:
- Reward type + amount (credit vs. cash, single vs. double-sided).
- Eligible regions (US-only vs. international).

### 2. Draft referral program terms
Once the reward and regions are set, draft the referral program terms covering:
- Who is eligible and who is excluded.
- How the reward is earned (conditions/vesting).
- Reward redemption, expiration, and caps.
- Anti-abuse rules (self-referral, fraud).
- Dispute handling and right to modify/terminate.

### 3. Confirm disclosure and tax treatment
- Ensure reward terms are clear and not misleading (UDTPA exposure).
- Determine whether rewards are taxable income and whether 1099 reporting applies.

### 4. Check state-specific rules for eligible regions
- If any eligible region involves licensed industries (insurance, financial services), confirm referral fees are permitted.
- If international, add cross-border consumer and data considerations.

### 5. Data/privacy check
- Referral requires the referrer to submit a third party's contact info. Confirm this fits the company's data practices and notice/consent posture.

## Next action
Confirm the reward type/amount and eligible regions with Product/Marketing, then draft the referral program terms.
