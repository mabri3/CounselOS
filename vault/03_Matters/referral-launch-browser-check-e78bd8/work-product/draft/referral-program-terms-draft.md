## 3. Draft referral program terms

### 3.1 Overview
This Referral Program (the "Program") lets eligible customers ("Referrers") earn a reward by
referring new customers ("Referred Customers") to [Company]'s subscription service.

### 3.2 Eligibility
- **Referrer eligibility:** The Referrer must be a current customer in good standing with an active
  account, and must be an individual (not a business entity acting as a reseller or agent).
- **Referred Customer eligibility:** The Referred Customer must be a new customer who has not
  previously held an account with [Company], and must not be an employee, contractor, or affiliate
  of [Company] or its resellers.
- **Self-referral prohibited:** Referrers may not refer themselves, use multiple accounts, or use
  any automated or fraudulent means to generate referrals.

### 3.3 How a reward is earned
- A Referrer earns a reward when a Referred Customer (i) signs up using the Referrer's unique
  referral link and (ii) completes a qualifying purchase within 30 days of sign-up.
- Only one reward is earned per qualifying Referred Customer.
- Rewards are subject to the caps and anti-abuse limits in Section 3.5.

### 3.4 The reward
- **Referrer reward:** [service credit amount] applied to the Referrer's account.
- **Referred Customer reward:** [service credit amount] applied to the Referred Customer's account
  upon qualifying purchase.
- Rewards are issued as service credits and have no cash value. They are non-transferable and may
  not be redeemed for cash.

### 3.5 Caps and anti-abuse
- **Per-Referrer cap:** A Referrer may earn a maximum of [X] rewards per [period].
- **Fraud controls:** [Company] may withhold or claw back any reward obtained through fraud, abuse,
  or violation of these terms.
- **Right to modify/terminate:** [Company] may modify, suspend, or terminate the Program at any
  time, with or without notice. Pending rewards that have not yet vested may be forfeited.

### 3.6 Reward redemption and expiration
- Service credits are applied automatically to the account and expire [X] days after issuance if
  unused.
- Credits may be applied only to [Company] subscription fees and may not be combined with other
  offers except as [Company] permits.

### 3.7 Taxes
- Rewards may be taxable income to the recipient. Recipients are responsible for any taxes arising
  from participation. [Company] may issue tax forms (e.g., Form 1099) as required by law.

### 3.8 Data and privacy
- By participating, the Referrer confirms they have the right to share the Referred Customer's
  contact information and that the Referred Customer has consented to being contacted.
- Referred Customer contact information will be processed in accordance with [Company]'s Privacy
  Policy.

### 3.9 Disputes and governing law
- These terms are governed by the laws of [State], without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
- Any dispute arising from the Program is subject to [Company]'s standard Terms of Service dispute
  provisions.

### 3.10 Entire agreement
- These Program terms supplement, and are incorporated into, [Company]'s Terms of Service. In the
  event of conflict, these Program terms control with respect to the Program.
