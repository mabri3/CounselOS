# Research: Referral Launch Browser Check

## 1. Executive orientation

Marketing wants to launch a customer referral feature **next month**, but the two gating variables — **reward terms** and **eligible regions** — are not final. This is a product-change matter in the **Research** stage, US-only company footprint (per company profile), moderate risk posture, subscription business software.

The controlling question is not "what laws apply" but **"what are the reward and the regions?"** Until those two are locked, every downstream workstream (terms drafting, tax/1099, state-specific review, data check) is speculative. The research should therefore be structured to (a) surface the rules that will apply once those variables are set, and (b) force Product/Marketing to commit to the gating facts so the legal work is targeted rather than generic.

## 2. Likely rules / issues (source-based)

Grounded in the matter's `issues.md` and `recommendations.md`, the material source-based issues are:

**A. Reward characterization (drives everything)**
- **Cash vs. service credit vs. discount.** Cash or cash-equivalent rewards implicate different rules than service credits (tax, 1099, potentially state gift-card/rebate rules).
- **Single vs. double-sided** (referrer only, referred only, or both).
- **Vesting conditions** — what must happen before a reward is earned (e.g., referred party completes a purchase, maintains account X days). This determines whether the reward is a "bounty" vs. a discount, and affects disclosure and accounting treatment.
- **Caps / anti-abuse** — per-user caps, self-referral prohibitions, fraud controls.

**B. Eligible regions**
- **US-only vs. international.** Company profile is US-only, but "eligible regions" implies a possible multi-state or international footprint. This is the single biggest scope driver.
- **State-specific referral / anti-rebate rules** — especially if any eligible region touches licensed industries (insurance, financial services, real estate, healthcare), where referral fees are often restricted or prohibited.
- **Tax treatment** — rewards may be taxable income to the recipient; 1099 reporting may apply.
- **Consumer protection / UDTPA** — reward terms must be clear and not misleading; undisclosed conditions create deceptive-acts exposure.

**C. Program terms (T&Cs)**
- Referral program terms need drafting and linking from the feature: eligibility/exclusions, redemption mechanics, expiration, caps, dispute handling, right to modify/terminate.

**D. Data / privacy**
- Referral inherently requires the referrer to submit a **third party's contact information**. Must confirm this fits the company's data practices (business contact data) and any notice/consent obligations.

## 3. Viable paths

**Path 1 (recommended): Lock the gating facts first, then targeted research.**
Get Product/Marketing to commit to (1) reward type + amount, (2) eligible regions, (3) vesting conditions. Then run targeted research on the specific rules that apply. This avoids a generic, low-value research dump and matches the matter's own recommendations.

**Path 2: Run broad first-pass research now.**
Validate the issue map across all four workstreams without waiting for the gating facts. Useful for building a checklist, but risks rework once the reward/regions are set. Lower efficiency given the one-month launch window.

**Path 3: Draft program terms on reasonable assumptions.**
Proceed with a default assumption (e.g., service-credit, double-sided, US-only) and draft terms + disclosures that can be adjusted. Keeps momentum toward the launch date, but requires clearly labeling assumptions and a re-review once facts are locked.

## 4. Facts that could change the answer

These are the few facts that materially shift the analysis:

1. **Reward type** — cash vs. credit vs. discount (drives tax/1099, state rules, disclosure).
2. **Eligible regions** — US-only vs. international (drives cross-border consumer/data law, state-specific anti-rebate rules).
3. **Double-sided vs. single-sided** — affects who is rewarded and the disclosure/tax surface.
4. **Vesting conditions** — what must happen before a reward is earned (bounty vs. discount characterization).
5. **Whether any eligible region touches a licensed industry** (insurance, financial services, healthcare, real estate) — triggers anti-referral-fee restrictions.

## 5. Recommended last-mile verification

Before this research is treated as reliable for launch decisions, verify:

- **Confirm the three gating facts with Product/Marketing** (reward type/amount, eligible regions, vesting conditions) — this is the single highest-value action and is already flagged as the next action in `recommendations.md`.
- **Confirm the company's actual data practices** for third-party contact submission (the company profile says "business contact data," but the referral flow's consent/notice posture needs confirmation against the real privacy program).
- **Confirm whether any eligible region involves a licensed industry** — if so, pull the specific state anti-referral-fee / anti-rebate statutes for those jurisdictions.
- **Confirm tax/1099 posture** with finance/tax — whether rewards are taxable income and whether 1099 reporting applies.
