# Original Request

Northstar Pay plans to make autopay enrollment easier for customers who use installment loans. The business goal is to reduce missed payments while giving customers control over the bank account or debit card used for repayment. The proposed experience will ask customers to select autopay during checkout or in the app. It will display the next payment amount and date, allow customers to change payment methods, and send reminders before scheduled withdrawals. Northstar Pay may also offer a discount or other incentive for customers who enroll. The relevant actors are customers, Northstar Pay, payment processors, banks, lending partners, and servicing vendors. The proposed launch is in six weeks.

Known facts are that some payment amounts can change after refunds, partial disputes, returned payments, or account adjustments. The product team is considering retrying failed payments and using a backup payment method when the primary method fails. Open questions include the required authorization language, notice timing, consent standards, limits on recurring electronic transfers, debit-card rules, cancellation rights, and permissible retry practices. Please advise on the proposed enrollment flow, reminders, payment changes, failed-payment handling, and customer-service procedures.
