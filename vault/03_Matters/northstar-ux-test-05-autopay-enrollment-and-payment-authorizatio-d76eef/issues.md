# Issues

**Status note:** All legal propositions in this issue map are unverified leads pending jurisdiction and product confirmation. They are generated analysis, not verified law, and must be confirmed against the applicable states, products, and partners before launch.

## 1. Recurring ACH authorization (Regulation E / NACHA)
- Written authorization required for preauthorized electronic fund transfers; content and retention requirements.
- Notice of varying amounts: when payment amounts change (refunds, disputes, adjustments), advance notice before the transfer.
- Consumer right to stop payment; error resolution procedures.

## 2. Debit-card recurring payments (Regulation E / card-network rules)
- Recurring debit authorization standards; card-network rules on stored credentials, retries, and merchant-initiated transactions.
- Differences vs. ACH for cancellation and dispute rights.

## 3. Consent and enrollment UX
- Clear, conspicuous, and affirmative consent at checkout vs. in app; avoiding dark patterns / pre-checked boxes.
- Authorization language must state amount (or variable-amount method), timing, and how to cancel.
- E-SIGN / UETA for electronic consent and disclosures.

## 4. Failed payments, retries, and backup methods
- Permissible retry practices under Reg E, NACHA rules, and state law; limits on retry counts and fees.
- Whether a backup payment method requires separate, specific authorization.
- Late/returned-payment fees: state limits and disclosure.

## 5. Enrollment incentive
- Whether a discount for autopay enrollment raises credit, UDAP/UDAAP, or state incentive concerns; disclosure of terms.

## 6. Reminders and notices
- Timing and content of pre-withdrawal reminders; changes to amount or date; channel and consent to messaging (TCPA/communications preferences).

## 7. Cancellation rights
- Easy, self-service cancellation; timing before next withdrawal; confirmation of cancellation.

## 8. Servicing, collections, and vendor oversight
- Servicing-vendor and payment-processor responsibilities; state servicing and collections conduct rules; credit reporting accuracy if late payments result.
- Partner-map confirmation before launch (per company rollout policy).

## 9. State-by-state availability
- Autopay experience must be validated per state/product/partner; no state is automatically available.

## Open issue questions (added per counsel instruction)

1. **Amount changes:** Do payment amount changes require fresh consent, or is advance notice sufficient? How do refunds, partial disputes, returned payments, and account adjustments affect the scheduled amount and required notices?
2. **Reminders:** Are the proposed reminders legally sufficient (content, timing, channel) and operationally reliable (delivery, servicer coordination)?
3. **Incentive presentation:** How is the enrollment discount or incentive presented — is it clear, non-misleading, and adequately disclosed?
4. **Evidence retention:** What evidence of consent, authorization, and cancellation must be retained, for how long, and in what form?
