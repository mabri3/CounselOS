# Decision — Autopay pilot scope and authorization controls

## Chosen path

Approved with conditions: gated ACH-first pilot using Path B only after the exact variable-amount formula, authorization copy, E-SIGN evidence, reminder timing/delivery records, retry limits, dispute-pause workflow, cancellation cutoff, incentive terms, and partner/state map are confirmed. Use Path A fixed-amount authorization plus advance notice for any unconfirmed rail, state, product, or servicer, including debit-card autopay. Require separate specific authorization for backup-method charges or disable automatic backup charges. Pause disputed or unclear withdrawals and all retries. Require self-service cancellation effective before the next withdrawal with confirmation. The internal six-week target is not approval; launch is governed by the gates.

## Rationale

Fictional test decision. All legal leads remain unverified pending jurisdiction, product, and partner confirmation; this is not final legal advice. Rationale drawn from the launch-advice memo and working recommendation: Path B variable-amount authorization is only defensible where the disclosed method is precise and pre-withdrawal reminders reliably show the current amount; Path A fixed-amount plus advance notice is the fallback where controls are unconfirmed; automatic backup-method charges without separate specific authorization create unauthorized-transfer risk; disputed or unclear withdrawals are paused pending recalculation and notice; self-service cancellation effective before the next withdrawal is required. Joint owners: Product, Servicing, and Legal (before the internal six-week target). Alex Morgan is decision owner; Brian Harris is legal gate owner.

## Conditions

- Confirm exact variable-amount formula for Path B authorization
- Confirm final authorization copy
- Confirm E-SIGN consent capture and evidence
- Confirm reminder timing and delivery records
- Confirm retry limits
- Confirm dispute-pause workflow
- Confirm cancellation cutoff before next withdrawal
- Confirm incentive terms and disclosure
- Confirm partner/state map
- Path A applies to any unconfirmed rail, state, product, or servicer, including debit
- Separate specific authorization required for backup-method charges, or backup charges disabled
- Disputed or unclear withdrawals and all retries paused
