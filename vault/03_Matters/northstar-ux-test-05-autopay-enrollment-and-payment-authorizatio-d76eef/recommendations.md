# Working Recommendation

**Status:** This is a recommendation for counsel review, not a recorded decision. All legal propositions and risk assessments are unverified leads pending jurisdiction, product, and partner confirmation.

## Recommendation

Recommend a gated ACH-first pilot using Path B only after Northstar confirms the precise variable-amount formula, authorization wording, E-SIGN consent capture, pre-withdrawal reminder delivery, and evidence retention. Use Path A fixed-amount authorization plus advance notice for any product, state, servicer, or rail where those controls are not confirmed. Do not auto-retry against a backup payment method without separate specific authorization. On any refund, partial dispute, returned payment, or account adjustment, recalculate transparently, show the next amount and date, send the required notice, and pause the withdrawal if the amount or authorization is unclear. Provide self-service cancellation effective before the next withdrawal. Hold the six-week launch to the gates, not the date.

## Supporting detail

- Full analysis, sample authorization copy, launch gates, and implementation checklist are in the main memo: "Autopay Enrollment and Payment Authorization — Launch Advice."
- Path B (variable-amount authorization with a precise disclosed method) is ACH-only in the pilot; debit rails use Path A.
- Backup-method retries require separate specific authorization; disputed or unclear withdrawals are paused.
- Cancellation must be self-service and effective before the next withdrawal.
- The six-week launch is an internal planning target; launch is governed by the gates, not the date.
