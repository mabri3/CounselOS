# Original Request

Harborline plans to add instant outbound and inbound payments for eligible consumer and business customers. Product proposes a risk engine that scores each transfer using account age, device history, beneficiary history, transaction velocity, and fraud signals. Low-risk outbound payments would be released immediately. Medium-risk transfers could be delayed for up to 24 hours for review, and high-risk transfers would be rejected. Inbound payments that appear suspicious could be credited to the visible balance but made unavailable while operations investigates. Customers, recipients, Harborline fraud and AML teams, the partner bank, and the payment network are involved. The target launch is before the holiday payment peak. We know that instant payments are generally final once sent. We have not fixed transfer limits, review service levels, notice language, or the line between a fraud hold and an AML investigation.

Legal needs to decide whether the proposed holds, rejects, and balance displays are consistent with network rules, account agreements, applicable funds-transfer law, funds-availability duties, and unfair-practices risk. Please specify when Harborline may delay an instruction, what notice can be given without revealing sensitive monitoring, and whether a customer must be allowed to cancel during review. We also need advice on handling mistaken payments, unauthorized payments, sanctions alerts, suspicious activity, and requests from recipients who are not Harborline customers. Please identify which rules differ for consumer and business customers and which operating decisions require partner-bank approval.
