## Themis · 2026-08-30T20:19:46+00:00

Confirmed. I've oriented to the request and mapped the issues. Here's the working picture:

## What Product is asking for

A "linked external accounts + automated cash sweeps" feature for U.S. business customers: link accounts at other banks (via a data-aggregation provider), set a minimum-balance rule, and Harborline or its bank partner originates an ACH debit from the external account when the Harborline balance falls below the threshold. Role-based permissions for finance teams. Twelve-week target launch.

## Core legal issues

1. **Authorization / consent (Nacha + Reg E/UCC 4A analog for business accounts)**
   - Exact consent language and process for standing authorization to originate debits; renewal/reauthorization cadence; whether each automated debit requires advance notice (Nacha generally does not require per-entry notice if the standing authorization is properly obtained, but the authorization must be clear about amount/timing variability).
   - Handling of revoked authority — Nacha requires the ODFI to ensure the originator stops debits promptly after revocation; need a kill-switch process and cutoff timing.
   - Returned entries (R08 stop payment, R10 unauthorized, R29) — reinitiation rules and customer remediation.

2. **Account validation**
   - Nacha account-validation rule (for WEB debits; TEL/PPD considerations) — the missing fact on validation method (instant account verification vs. micro-deposits vs. aggregation-provider data) drives compliance posture. Using aggregator balance data alone may not satisfy validation requirements.

3. **Ownership controls**
   - Preventing an employee from linking an account the business doesn't own — name matching, beneficial ownership/business verification, treatment of joint accounts and affiliate-owned accounts (both open questions in the request).

4. **Privacy / data use**
   - Showing external balances and using them in automated decisions — GLBA notice implications, aggregation-provider contract terms, data minimization, and whether balance-based automated decisions trigger additional disclosure.

5. **Security / permissions**
   - MFA for rule changes (already planned); role-based permissions for creating/editing sweeps; audit logging.

## Key missing facts (from the request itself)

- Account-validation method
- Debit limits
- Treatment of joint accounts
- Whether customers may link affiliate-owned accounts
- Who originates: Harborline or the bank partner (affects money-transmission and partner-bank governance analysis)

## Suggested next step

I can run first-pass research on the Nacha authorization/validation/revocation requirements and the data-use question, and draft an issue map and recommendation on the consent process. Want me to proceed with that?
