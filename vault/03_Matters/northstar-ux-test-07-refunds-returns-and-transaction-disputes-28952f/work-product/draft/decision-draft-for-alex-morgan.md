# Draft Decision for Review — Refund and Dispute Flow Standard

Matter: Northstar UX Test — 07 — Refunds, Returns, and Transaction Disputes

Status: DRAFT — reviewed and edited by Alex Morgan. Not recorded. This is a fictional decision for review purposes.

Decision Question

Should Northstar Pay adopt the card-equivalent dispute standard and last-payment-first refund allocation rule for its standardized refund, return, and dispute flow ahead of retail-category expansion?

Proposed Decision

Adopt the card-equivalent dispute standard across all Northstar Pay products: for unauthorized-transaction disputes, the disputed amount is not owed during investigation, with acknowledgment within five business days and resolution within two complete billing cycles or ninety days; merchant-conduct disputes route through card-network chargebacks with continued payment obligations disclosed up front. Adopt a last-payment-first refund allocation rule documented in the customer agreement, with same-day application, next-business-day in-app notice of the recalculated schedule, and an idempotent refund identifier to prevent duplicate refunds. Amend merchant contracts to require refund SLAs, item-level data where available, unique refund identifiers, and merchant liability for retransmitted refunds.

Conditions

The decision is conditioned on completion of the launch prerequisites: verification of the CFPB interpretive rule status, the state-law overlay for all live states, card-network and bank-partner agreement requirements, and confirmation of the creditor/servicer-of-record map with the lending partners.

Alex Morgan Review and Edits

I approve a uniform card-equivalent standard for this MVP, subject to product- and state-specific changes if verified law or partner requirements require them. I approve the last-payment-first allocation rule as a customer-agreement term, with an order-level fallback when item-level refund data is missing. I approve merchant contract amendments for refund SLAs, item-level data where available, unique refund identifiers, retransmission liability, and investigation cooperation. For merchant-conduct disputes, payments may continue only with clear up-front disclosure and a documented support path for case-specific forbearance. Launch remains conditioned on the listed legal, partner, and engineering prerequisites.

Decision status: Reviewed and edited by Alex Morgan; not yet recorded. The decision may be recorded after this review.
