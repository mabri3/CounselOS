# Original Request

Northstar Pay wants to standardize its process for merchant refunds, customer returns, and payment disputes. The business goal is to reduce duplicate refunds and ensure that a customer’s repayment schedule is adjusted promptly when a purchase is canceled or reduced. The proposed flow will receive refund information from the merchant, apply the amount to the outstanding balance, recalculate future payments when needed, and notify the customer in the app. Customers may also report unauthorized transactions, defective goods, or merchant non-delivery through Northstar Pay support. The relevant actors are customers, merchants, Northstar Pay, card networks, banks, and dispute-resolution vendors. Product wants the new process live before expanding to additional retail categories.

Known facts are that merchants may issue full or partial refunds at different times, and some merchants do not reliably send item-level information. A customer may continue to owe payments while a dispute is under review. Open questions include the required investigation process, payment obligations during disputes, refund allocation rules, timing for schedule changes, error-resolution notices, and how Northstar Pay should handle disputes involving merchant conduct rather than payment authorization. Please advise on the customer rights, merchant obligations, notices, and operational timelines that should govern this flow.
