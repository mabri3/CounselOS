## Matter summary — Orbit: Bank-Transaction Data Expansion

**What it is:** DemoCo wants to use linked bank-transaction data (categories + cash-flow trends) as a new eligibility signal to qualify applicants for a **larger advance**. The question on the table: what changes in the **application and decline experience**.

**Status:** Intake · High risk · Target 2026-09-20 · Privileged & confidential
**Owner:** Brian Harris (legal) · Requester: Product Manager · Business owner: Product Lead

**Where the analysis stands:**
- **Facts:** Applicants voluntarily link a bank account; transaction categories and cash-flow trends influence eligibility. Two open confirmations: (1) is a credit report also used? (2) is a smaller offer immediately available?
- **Issues:** (1) application/adverse-action path, (2) what specific reasons the model can produce, (3) consent and data-use disclosure changes.
- **Work product planned:** a decision-flow notice matrix and a model reason-code checklist.
- **Work item WI-1** ("Run adverse-action research") is **open, high priority** — but the research folder is **empty**, so the packet hasn't been built yet.
- **No decisions recorded** yet.

## Next steps

1. **Run the adverse-action research** (WI-1) — this is the open, high-priority item and the research folder is empty. It will produce the notice-timing and reason-specificity packet.
2. **Confirm the two open facts** — they materially change the legal path:
   - If a **credit report** is also used → FCRA adverse-action obligations attach, not just ECOA/Reg B.
   - If a **smaller offer is immediately available** → the "decline" may be a counteroffer, which changes the adverse-action trigger and notice design.
3. **Then build the deliverables:** decision-flow notice matrix (application → decision → notice timing) and model reason-code checklist (what reasons the model can produce vs. what must be disclosed).
