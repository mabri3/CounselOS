# Original Request

Harborline plans to launch business checking accounts for single-member LLCs and small corporations in the first quarter. The proposed onboarding flow lets an employee or owner start an application, enter company information, identify a control person and beneficial owners, and invite each relevant person to complete an identity check. To reduce delay, product wants to let the applicant fund the account and receive payment credentials after the control person passes verification, even if one beneficial owner’s check remains pending. The applicant, beneficial owners, Harborline operations staff, our identity vendor, and the partner bank are the main actors. We know the product will initially serve U.S.-formed entities with no more than five owners. We do not yet know whether nonprofit entities, trusts, foreign owners, or layered ownership structures will be eligible. The partner bank has not confirmed whether Harborline may apply exception handling without manual bank approval.

Legal must decide whether the proposed staged activation is acceptable under the applicable beneficial-ownership and customer due diligence requirements, including any current or upcoming regulatory changes that affect what the bank must collect or verify. Please specify which person must certify the ownership information, what evidence we should retain, when the account can receive or send funds, and which entity types require a different flow or should be excluded from the first release. We also need advice on how often ownership details must be refreshed and what Harborline should do if an owner ignores an invitation, disputes the submitted information, or fails identity screening after the account has received money.
