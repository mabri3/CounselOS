Done. Here's where the matter stands:

**Bottom line:** Product's proposed flow — fund the account and issue payment credentials after the control person passes verification while a beneficial owner's check is pending — is **not acceptable as proposed**. The defensible version is **restricted activation**:

- After the control person is verified and the applicant signs the ownership certification, the account may **receive incoming funds only** (deposits land immediately — preserves most of the speed benefit).
- **Outbound payments and credentials are gated** until all ≥25% beneficial owners complete verification, with a hard 30-day clock and automatic escalation.
- Any more aggressive staging requires the partner bank's **written exception procedure** — which is currently unconfirmed. Escalate that question to the bank in writing now; it gates everything.

**Key points from the first-pass analysis:**
- **Certification:** the individual opening the account on behalf of the entity certifies ownership info (Appendix A-style form); owners are subjects of verification, not certifiers. The bank verifies each named individual's identity but not the ownership percentages themselves.
- **CTA/BOI doesn't help:** FinCEN's 2025 interim final rule removed BOI reporting for U.S. domestic entities, and banks never got registry access for CDD — the bank must still collect and verify ownership directly.
- **Entity types:** sole proprietors and nonprofits get a simplified flow (no BO certification); **exclude trusts, layered ownership, and non-U.S.-resident owners** from release 1.
- **Refresh:** risk-based per the bank's program (commonly 1–3 years by tier) plus trigger events — build an in-product ownership-change attestation.
- **Non-cooperation after funding:** reminders → 30-day clock → restrict outbound → close per bank procedures; screening failures mean immediate outbound restriction, bank escalation, and SAR/confidentiality handling.

**Caveats:** this is first-pass analysis — verify 31 CFR 101.230, the FinCEN CDD FAQs, and the current CTA/BOI status before final advice, since that area moved repeatedly in 2024–2025.
