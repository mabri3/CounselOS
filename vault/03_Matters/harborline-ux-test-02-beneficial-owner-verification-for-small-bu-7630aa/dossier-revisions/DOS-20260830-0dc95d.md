# Matter dossier

## Summary

Harborline plans a Q1 launch of business checking accounts for single-member LLCs and small corporations (U.S.-formed, ≤5 owners) through its partner bank. Product proposes staged activation: the applicant funds the account and receives payment credentials once the control person passes identity verification, even if one ≥25% beneficial owner's check is still pending. The partner bank has not confirmed whether Harborline may apply exception handling without manual bank approval. Legal must decide whether staged activation satisfies CDD/beneficial-ownership requirements and define certification, evidence, refresh, and remediation rules.

## Decision question

Can Harborline's partner bank lawfully allow a small-business deposit account to receive funds and issue payment credentials after the control person passes verification while one or more ≥25% beneficial owners' identity checks remain pending — and if so, under what conditions (certification, evidence, timelines, exception approval, entity-type exclusions) — or must the account stay restricted until all beneficial-owner checks complete?

## Open questions

- Has the partner bank confirmed in writing that Harborline may apply exception handling without manual bank approval, and what is the bank's CDD program standard for pending-owner accounts?
- Will nonprofits, trusts, foreign owners, or layered ownership structures be eligible in the first release, or excluded?
- What is the actual funding/credential timing product needs (days of delay being avoided), and can funds be held in a restricted/incoming-only state pending completion?
- Which identity vendor is used, and does its verification output satisfy the bank's documentary/non-documentary record requirements for the 5-year retention file?
- What is the partner bank's risk-based refresh cycle and trigger list for ownership changes?
