# Project Apex: AI Voice Telemetry — Launch Review

## Recommendation: **Go with conditions**

The feature is viable for the September release, but only as a **bounded, opt-in launch** with a shorter default retention period and strict vendor controls. Do not ship it as currently proposed (90-day raw retention + vendor transcription with no confirmed training purpose).

## Key legal issues

1. **Notice and choice.** The feature is optional, which is good — but collection of voice/audio is sensitive. Users need explicit, informed opt-in before any recording, and the disclosure must be accurate about what happens to the audio.
2. **Purpose limitation & retention.** 90-day raw-audio retention is not clearly necessary for the stated purpose. Retention should be tied to the actual purpose (transcription/telemetry) and minimized — a shorter default (e.g., 30 days, consistent with the prior pilot decision) is the safer path.
3. **Model training as a separate purpose.** This is the pivotal issue. If raw clips are used for model improvement, that is a *separate* purpose that must be separately disclosed and consented to. This is currently **unconfirmed** (open work item).
4. **Vendor controls.** The transcription vendor must be contractually restricted from training on or retaining the audio, and deletion must propagate to the vendor. This is also **unconfirmed**.
5. **State voice-recording rules.** US state wiretap/recording consent laws vary; confirm compliance before launch.

## Sources used

- **Request** (original ask: ship voice telemetry, retain raw clips, vendor transcription)
- **Facts** (optional feature, 90-day retention, vendor transcription, two open confirmations)
- **Issues** (notice/choice, retention necessity, vendor controls)
- **Recommendations** (working path: limited opt-in launch)
- **First-pass research packet** (three viable paths; last-mile verification items)
- **Prior decision** (30-day retention cap precedent for pilot voice logging)
- **Open work items** (training-purpose confirmation; counsel review)

## Important assumptions

- The pilot is internal/employee-facing (per the prior decision's framing), which lowers consumer-privacy exposure — but this should be confirmed.
- No minors use the feature (flagged in research as unverified).
- The vendor contract is not yet reviewed for no-training and deletion-propagating terms.
- The two open confirmations in the facts — **training purpose** and **vendor deletion propagation** — are unresolved and materially change the recommendation.

## The decision counsel must make

**Confirm the training purpose and final retention period.** Specifically:

1. **Is raw audio used for model training?** If yes, that requires a separate, disclosed consent path and likely delays the model-improvement use (Path 3 in research) even if the telemetry feature itself launches.
2. **What is the final retention period?** The recommendation is a shorter default (30 days) rather than the proposed 90 days, unless product demonstrates 90 days is necessary.

Until those two facts are confirmed, the launch should proceed **with conditions**, not as a clean go.

## Conditions for a go

- Explicit, informed opt-in before any recording.
- Shorter default retention (30 days) unless necessity for 90 days is shown.
- Vendor contract: no training on audio, deletion propagates to vendor, and deletion is tested.
- Separate disclosure/consent if audio is used for model training.
- Confirm state voice-recording compliance and no-minor usage.

I have **not changed any records** — this is a review only. The two open work items (training purpose and counsel review) remain open and are the gating items. Would you like me to draft the vendor-contract terms or the user-facing disclosure language as a next step?
