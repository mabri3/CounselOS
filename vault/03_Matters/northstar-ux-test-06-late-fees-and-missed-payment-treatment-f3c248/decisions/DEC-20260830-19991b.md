# Late-Fee Policy — Conditional Gated Launch Decision (Pay-in-4 Conservative Default; Longer-Term Installments Gated)

## Chosen path

Conditional launch decision only: approve the conservative, gated late-fee path — mandatory 21-day grace period with pre-fee reminders; objective, automated, audited first-fee waiver; no stacking of late fees with returned-payment fees for the same event; late fees excluded from credit reporting and collection balances until accuracy and ownership controls pass; longer-term installment fees blocked until each state, partner, license, and cap is confirmed. No exact fee amount is approved; the conservative cap language operates as a ceiling only.

## Rationale

The two product lines travel different legal paths: pay-in-4 turns on the unsettled CFPB BNPL/Reg Z interpretive-rule status [unverified lead], while longer-term installments turn on a state-by-state, partner-by-partner analysis where true-lender and license-type questions determine the applicable caps [missing fact — partner matrix]. A conservative uniform default satisfies the card-style framework if the rule applies and state law if it does not, aligns with the company's customer-clarity risk posture [supplied fact], and reduces complaint drivers (fee stacking, surprise fees, discretionary waivers). Based on the revised decision-review work product (reviewed/edited by Brian Harris, approved by Alex Morgan), not the original draft.

## Conditions

- No exact fee amount is approved until Product supplies numbers and counsel confirms the state/partner matrix
- 21-day grace period and pre-fee reminders are mandatory conditions of launch
- First-fee waiver criteria must be objective, automated, and audited; discretionary waivers prohibited
- Late fees and returned-payment fees may never be stacked for the same event, in ledger and disclosures
- Late fees excluded from credit reporting and collection balances until accuracy workflows and partner fee-ownership terms pass review; inclusion requires separate re-review
- Longer-term installment fees blocked per state until state, partner, license, and cap are confirmed
- Six minimum launch controls in place, including pre-launch reconciliation proving disclosed fee equals charged fee in every state/product/partner combination
