# Original Request

Northstar Pay is reviewing its late-fee policy for pay-in-4 and longer-term installment products. The business goal is to recover reasonable servicing costs while reducing customer complaints and keeping the product affordable. The proposed policy would provide a grace period, send reminders before a fee is charged, and display any late fee in the app and account history. Northstar Pay is also considering waiving a first late fee for customers who meet certain criteria. The actors include customers, Northstar Pay, lending partners, payment processors, servicing vendors, and collections vendors. Product wants to implement the policy before the next major product release.

Known facts are that state law may limit late fees or prohibit them for certain products. Fees may also differ based on loan type, customer location, payment method, and the reason a payment was missed. Open questions include permissible fee amounts, grace periods, notice requirements, fee-waiver rules, treatment of failed payments, and whether late fees may be reported or included in collection balances. Please advise on the legal limits for each product, the required disclosures, and the controls needed to prevent fees from being charged incorrectly.
