# Issues

## Core legal questions

1. **Permissible late fees by product and state.** Whether late fees are permitted (and at what amount) for pay-in-4 vs. longer-term installments in each live state (CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, WA), given the bank/lending-partner structure for each product. Key sub-issues:
   - State rate/fee caps and whether late fees count toward them
   - Whether the true-lender / bank-partner structure shifts the applicable state limits
   - Product-specific rules (e.g., CA financing-law treatment of pay-in-4)
2. **Grace periods and notice.** Minimum grace period and pre-fee reminder/notice requirements by state and product; whether notice must be delivered in a specific form or timing.
3. **Disclosures.** Required disclosures of the late fee (amount, timing, conditions) at checkout, in the app, in account history, and in the loan agreement; consistency with the company's customer-clarity risk posture.
4. **Fee-waiver rules.** Whether a first-late-fee waiver conditioned on customer criteria raises fair-lending / disparate-treatment or UDAAP consistency concerns; documentation of waiver criteria.
5. **Failed payments.** Treatment of failed/returned payments (NSF/ACH return fees vs. late fees), payment-method-based differences, and whether different fees by payment method are permissible.
6. **Reporting and collections treatment.** Whether late fees may be included in credit reporting and collection balances; when a fee-inclusive balance may be referred to collections; collections-conduct limits.
7. **Operational controls.** Controls to prevent incorrect charging: state/product/partner fee configuration, fee-calculation logic, waiver logic, refund/reversal workflow, complaint monitoring, and audit records.

## Framing

- Business objective: recover reasonable servicing costs, reduce complaints, keep product affordable; implement before next major product release.
- Legal owner: Brian Harris. Requester: Product.
- Open implementation fact: state-by-state product/partner matrix must be confirmed before any fee schedule is finalized.
