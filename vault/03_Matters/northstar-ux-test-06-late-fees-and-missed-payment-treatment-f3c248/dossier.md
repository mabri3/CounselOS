# Matter dossier

## Summary

Northstar Pay (Product requester; legal owner Brian Harris) is redesigning its late-fee policy for pay-in-4 and longer-term installment products ahead of the next major product release. The proposed policy includes a grace period, pre-fee reminders, in-app/account-history fee display, and a possible first-late-fee waiver. Pay-in-4 is live in CA, CO, GA, IL, NY, TX, and WA; longer-term installments run on a smaller, still-unfinalized state/partner list. The goal is to recover servicing costs while reducing complaints and keeping the product affordable.

## Decision question

What late-fee structure (fee amount, grace period, reminder timing, first-fee waiver criteria, and failed-payment treatment) can Northstar Pay lawfully implement for pay-in-4 and longer-term installments in each live state, and what disclosures and controls must be in place before the next major product release so fees are not charged incorrectly?

## Open questions

- What are the proposed fee amounts, grace-period length, and waiver criteria? (Not supplied; drives state-cap analysis.)
- Which states and lending partners are approved for longer-term installments? (Partner map still being finalized.)
- Are late fees intended to be credit-reported or included in collection balances?
- Which payment methods are in scope (ACH, debit, card), and is a separate returned-payment fee proposed?
- What is the launch date for the next major release?
