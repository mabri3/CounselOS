# Original Request

Northstar Pay wants to add a longer-term installment option at checkout for purchases above $500. The business goal is to give customers more repayment flexibility and allow merchants to offer financing for higher-value goods. The proposed experience will show several repayment terms, an estimated monthly payment, the annual percentage rate, total amount paid, and any down payment. After the customer selects a term, Northstar Pay will conduct underwriting, present the final offer, and obtain acceptance of the loan agreement. Northstar Pay, the merchant, the customer, its lending partner, and servicing vendors will be involved. The target launch is eight weeks after legal approval.

Known facts include that the product may be offered through different lending entities in different states. Rates, fees, term lengths, and licensing requirements may vary by state. The product team has not decided whether merchants may subsidize interest or offer promotional rates to selected customer groups. Open questions include the applicable Regulation Z requirements, state lending and licensing rules, rate and fee limits, required loan disclosures, broker or lender responsibilities, and restrictions on merchant involvement in credit terms. Please identify the legal approvals, product constraints, and required operating controls for this product.
