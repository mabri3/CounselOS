# First-Pass Research Packet

## Question

What material source-based issues should counsel research for Northstar UX Test — 02 — Longer-Term Installment Loan Compliance?

## Working Analysis

## Matter summary

Northstar Pay (Product requester; legal owner Brian Harris) wants to add a longer-term installment option at checkout for purchases above $500, showing repayment terms, estimated monthly payment, APR, total paid, and down payment, followed by underwriting, a final offer, and loan acceptance. The product runs through different lending entities by state, with merchants, servicing vendors, and lending partners involved. Target launch is eight weeks after legal approval. Rates, fees, terms, and licensing vary by state, and the merchant-subsidy/promotional-rate question is undecided.

## Decision question

What legal approvals, product constraints, and operating controls must be in place before Northstar Pay launches a longer-term installment loan (>$500 purchases) at checkout across its approved states, given state-varying lending partners, rates, and licensing, and an eight-week post-approval launch target?

## Open questions

- Which states and lending partners are in scope for the longer-term product, and what is the state-by-state partner matrix?
- Will merchants subsidize interest or offer promotional rates to selected customer groups, and on what basis?
- What are the term lengths, APR/fee ranges, and down payment structures contemplated?
- Is Northstar Pay acting as broker, lender, or bank-partner program manager in each state — and does licensing follow?
- What credit reporting, servicing, and collections arrangements apply per partner?

## Likely rules and issues

**Federal (Regulation Z / TILA)**
- Longer-term installment loans are "credit" under Reg Z; closed-end credit disclosures required (§1026.17, §1026.18): APR, payment schedule, total of payments, finance charge, late fees.
- **Advertising**: showing terms, monthly payment, APR, and total paid at checkout is likely an advertisement triggering §1026.24 trigger terms; if payment and term are shown, APR must be shown (which the design includes).
- **Estimated vs. final disclosures**: pre-selection display is an advertisement; after underwriting, the final offer requires TILA/Reg Z closed-end disclosures before consummation, with timing and content rules.
- **Ability to repay / underwriting**: no general federal ATR rule for non-credit-card closed-end consumer credit, but fair-lending (ECOA/Reg B) applies to underwriting and any customer-group targeting of promotional rates (disparate impact risk in selective offers).
- **Promotional/subsidized rates**: merchant-subsidized financing raises Reg Z advertising and ECOA concerns if terms differ by customer group; also state-level restrictions on merchant involvement in credit terms.

**State law**
- Licensing: lender, broker, or loan-servicer licenses vary by state and by role; the bank-partner model may exempt the bank but not Northstar Pay's own activities.
- Rate and fee caps: usury limits, state APR caps, fee limits (origination, late) vary by state and by loan size/term.
- Longer-term loans (vs. pay-in-4) often fall under state installment loan statutes requiring specific licenses and disclosures.
- Merchant involvement: some states restrict merchants arranging credit or receiving referral compensation.

**Operating controls**
- Servicing, collections conduct, credit-reporting accuracy, dispute handling, e-sign/e-consent, privacy/data sharing with partners, vendor oversight, and record retention per state and partner requirements.

## Viable paths

1. **Bank-partner model per state** (consistent with existing pay-in-4 structure): confirm partner map, preemption analysis for state rate caps where an FDIC-insured bank is the lender, and Northstar Pay's own licensing exposure for broker/servicer activities.
2. **State-by-state launch matrix**: launch only in states where legal, ops, and the lending partner confirm licensing, rate/fee compliance, disclosures, servicing, and reporting — matching the company's phased-rollout posture.
3. **Defer merchant subsidies/promotional rates** until a separate legal review of ECOA targeting, Reg Z advertising, and state restrictions is complete; launch with standard terms first.

## Facts that could change the answer

- The final state/partner matrix (which entities lend in which states).
- Whether merchants subsidize rates or target promotional offers to customer groups.
- Loan amounts, terms, and APR ranges (affects state caps and Reg Z treatment).
- Whether Northstar Pay takes applications/underwrites in its own name (broker vs. lender role).
- Whether credit reporting will be enabled for this product.

## Recommended last-mile verification

- Confirm the state-by-state partner and licensing matrix with the lending partners before launch approval (Alex Morgan should see the matrix per company profile).
- Verify Reg Z disclosure timing/content for the two-stage flow (advertisement → final offer) against the actual UX screens.
- Check state installment-loan statutes and rate/fee caps for each launch state against the actual product terms.
- Confirm ECOA/fair-lending review of any customer-group targeting before promotional rates are enabled.
