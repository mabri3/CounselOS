# Decision Record — Merchant-Subsidized Interest and Promotional Rates

**Decision:** Defer merchant-subsidized interest and promotional rates to phase 2 of the longer-term installment loan launch. Phase 1 (v1) will use neutral pricing and neutral term presentation.

**Rationale:** Phase 1 should use neutral pricing and neutral term presentation because the state, partner, disclosure, licensing, and fair-lending facts are unresolved. Phase 2 requires objective eligibility criteria, a documented business purpose, disclosure review, fair-lending analysis, and anti-steering controls before any merchant-subsidized or promotional pricing is offered.

**Assumption:** v1 has no merchant-funded subsidy and no selected-customer promotional rate. Neutral presentation of available terms applies to all similarly situated customers.

**Scope limitation:** This decision does not constitute launch approval. The remaining required work items (state-by-state partner matrix, Reg Z disclosure set and checkout display review, underwriting/adverse-action/credit-reporting responsibility confirmation, and servicing/vendor controls) must still be completed and approved before launch.

**Related recommendation:** The recommendation memo (work item "Decide on merchant-subsidized interest / promotional rates") remains a separate record from this decision.

**Recorded:** 2026-08-30, at the direction of the reviewing lawyer after review and edit of the decision memo.
