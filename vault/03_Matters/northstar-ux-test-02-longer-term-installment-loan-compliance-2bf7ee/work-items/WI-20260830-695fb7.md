# Decide on merchant-subsidized interest / promotional rates

Recommendation — separate from recorded decision

Defer merchant-subsidized interest and promotional-rate treatment to phase 2 of the longer-term installment launch. The eight-week target and unresolved state, partner, disclosure, and licensing facts make a neutral, standardized offer the safer v1 scope. A phase-1 design should show the same eligibility and pricing logic to similarly situated customers, without merchant-specific rate overrides or selected-group promotions.

Legal and product rationale

Merchant subsidies or promotional rates would change both the economics and the compliance analysis. The product would need a documented business purpose and objective eligibility criteria for any subsidy, a fair-lending review of the targeting and outcomes, and a review of whether the merchant is advertising or steering credit terms. Any APR, payment, fee, or promotional-period representation must remain accurate and must be paired with the required triggering-term disclosures. The partner agreement must also state who owns the disclosure, pricing, underwriting, and complaint obligations.

The current record does not identify the lending entity by state, the proposed subsidy formula, the customer groups that would receive a promotion, or whether the merchant would select or influence terms. These are material missing facts. I am treating the phase-1 assumption as no merchant-funded subsidy, no selected-customer promotional rate, and neutral presentation of available terms. This assumption is an implementation constraint, not a legal conclusion.

Required controls before any phase-2 approval

Product must maintain a versioned pricing and disclosure matrix by state, lender, term, APR, fees, down payment, and promotion. Compliance must review the customer-selection criteria and a disparate-impact analysis before launch. The checkout must display the same material terms and required disclosures for the same offer path, and the system must log the offer shown, the acceptance version, the merchant subsidy source, and the final creditor. Marketing and merchant training must prohibit steering or statements that imply a customer will receive a better rate because of a protected characteristic.

Proposed decision for later recording

Defer merchant-subsidized interest and promotional rates to phase 2. Record this only after the complete decision and rationale have been reviewed by Alex Morgan. The v1 launch may proceed on the neutral-pricing assumption only if the remaining Reg Z, state matrix, underwriting, and servicing work items are completed and approved.
