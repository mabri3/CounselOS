# Company profile

## Company Name

Harborline Financial, Inc.

## Website Url

Not provided.

## Summary

Fictional U.S. fintech (not affiliated with Manifest Financial, Chime, Stripe, or any other reference company) offering consumer and small-business bank accounts through a regulated U.S. banking partner, plus payments services including ACH, cards, instant payments, business payment acceptance, platform integrations, refunds, disputes, and account-to-account transfers. Harborline runs the software, onboarding, risk operations, customer support, and product experience; the partner bank opens and holds deposit accounts and retains bank-level compliance oversight. Legal owner is Maya Chen, Senior Product Counsel (BSA/AML, banking regulation, payments).

## Business Model

Consumer and small-business deposit accounts offered through a sponsor bank partner, combined with payments services (ACH, cards, instant payments, business payment acceptance, platform integrations, refunds, disputes, account-to-account transfers). Customers are U.S. consumers, sole proprietors, LLCs, small corporations, and software platforms serving small businesses. Harborline operates the software, onboarding interface, risk operations, customer support, and product experience; the partner bank holds deposit accounts and bank-level compliance oversight. Key partners include the sponsor bank, identity and business-verification vendors, sanctions and adverse-media screening vendors, payment networks, card and ACH processors, cloud and analytics providers, and customer-support vendors. Internal teams include Product, Engineering, Compliance, BSA/AML, Fraud, Risk Operations, Marketing, Privacy, Security, Customer Support, Finance, and Legal.

## Products Services

Consumer and small-business bank accounts (via partner bank); ACH, card, and instant payments; business payment acceptance; platform integrations; refunds and disputes handling; account-to-account transfers; onboarding, risk operations, and customer support.

## Jurisdictions

United States only (initial operating footprint).

## Regulatory Context

BSA/AML, customer identification, beneficial ownership, sanctions, money transmission, payment-network rules, Regulation E and UCC Article 4A, funds availability, privacy and data use, FCRA and background screening, account restrictions and closures, consumer protection, marketing claims, endorsements, deposit insurance statements, vendor oversight, and partner-bank governance.

## Data Practices

Operating principles: use the minimum customer data needed; protect confidential monitoring and suspicious-activity information; preserve accessible records; document funds flow and control ownership. Detailed data-sharing, retention, and customer-choice practices not yet provided.

## Risk Posture

Cautious but practical. Supports useful product launches when controls are clear, responsibilities are documented, and high-risk cases receive human review. Does not promise zero risk or legal perfection. Operating principles: give customers clear status and notice; keep recommendations separate from recorded decisions; do not imply an account is open before the bank approves it; protect confidential monitoring and suspicious-activity information; use minimum customer data; document funds flow and control ownership; preserve accessible records; escalate material partner-bank questions early.
