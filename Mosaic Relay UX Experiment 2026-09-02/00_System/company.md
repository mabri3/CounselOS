## Summary

Mosaic Relay is a fictional test company created for the Counsel OS UX Experiment on September 2, 2026. It has no affiliation with Stripe, Checkout.com, Marqeta, Manifest Financial, Chime, or any other real company. This fictional status must remain conspicuous in the company profile and in any generated work. Mosaic Relay is a payment-infrastructure provider serving online businesses, marketplaces, and software platforms. Its business model is B2B infrastructure: it earns service and processing-related fees for enabling payment acceptance, routing, reconciliation, risk controls, and payouts, while relying on regulated financial and technology partners for parts of the payment chain. It does not offer bank accounts, hold deposits, or operate as a bank. Its products are APIs and hosted tools for card payments, ACH, local payment methods, payment routing and splitting, refunds, disputes, and payouts to sellers, contractors, and other recipients. It offers merchant onboarding, KYC and KYB checks, sanctions screening, fraud monitoring, transaction-risk scoring, payout controls, reconciliation reports, and operations dashboards. Customers are online merchants, two-sided marketplaces, SaaS products, and other software platforms that need embedded payments and controlled movement of funds. Partners include acquiring banks, payment processors, card networks, identity and fraud vendors, and cloud providers. Mosaic Relay coordinates these relationships but does not present itself as a bank or as a deposit-taking institution. Its operating footprint is fictional and primarily remote, with product, engineering, risk, compliance, operations, finance, sales, and legal teams working across North America and Europe. The company should be treated as a test scenario, not as a real business or regulated entity. The fictional legal owner is Jordan Lee, General Counsel and Secretary of Mosaic Relay. Jordan owns legal strategy, contracting, regulatory coordination, privacy, intellectual property, employment matters, disputes, and records. The legal team supports commercial agreements, payment and money-movement regulation, licensing and partner allocation, AML and sanctions controls, KYC/KYB, consumer and merchant protection, privacy and security, data use, card-network rules, ACH rules, outsourcing, incident response, tax, employment, and litigation readiness. Mosaic Relay has a risk-aware posture. It uses layered onboarding, sanctions and fraud controls, transaction monitoring, payout limits, reserves or holds where contractually permitted, reconciliation, access controls, audit trails, and incident response. Material risks include fraud, account takeover, sanctions exposure, payment reversals and disputes, partner or processor outages, data security and privacy events, operational errors, regulatory perimeter questions, misdirected payouts, concentration risk, and cross-border requirements. Its principles are clear ownership, accurate records, least-privilege access, explainable decisions, customer and recipient safety, timely escalation, reliable reconciliation, honest labeling of uncertainty, and lawyer review for material decisions.

## Business Model

B2B payment infrastructure; earns service and processing-related fees for enabling payment acceptance, routing, reconciliation, risk controls, and payouts. Relies on regulated financial and technology partners for parts of the payment chain. Does not offer bank accounts, hold deposits, or operate as a bank.

## Products Services

APIs and hosted tools for card payments, ACH, local payment methods, payment routing and splitting, refunds, disputes, and payouts to sellers, contractors, and other recipients. Also offers merchant onboarding, KYC/KYB checks, sanctions screening, fraud monitoring, transaction-risk scoring, payout controls, reconciliation reports, and operations dashboards.

## Jurisdictions

Fictional and primarily remote; teams across North America and Europe.

## Regulatory Context

Coordinates relationships with acquiring banks, payment processors, card networks, identity/fraud vendors, and cloud providers. Not a bank or deposit-taking institution. Legal team supports payment and money-movement regulation, licensing and partner allocation, AML/sanctions, KYC/KYB, consumer/merchant protection, privacy/security, card-network rules, ACH rules, outsourcing, and tax.

## Data Practices

Handles personal and transactional data for onboarding, KYC/KYB, sanctions screening, fraud monitoring, and transaction-risk scoring. Uses access controls, audit trails, and incident response. Privacy and security are supported by legal team.

## Risk Posture

Risk-aware with layered onboarding, sanctions and fraud controls, transaction monitoring, payout limits, reserves/holds where contractually permitted, reconciliation, access controls, audit trails, and incident response. Material risks include fraud, account takeover, sanctions exposure, payment reversals/disputes, partner/processor outages, data security/privacy events, operational errors, regulatory perimeter questions, misdirected payouts, concentration risk, and cross-border requirements.
