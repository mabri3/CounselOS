      rules are likely engaged:

      third countries require an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs),

      affects lawful basis, transparency, and accountability.

      (irreversible de-identification) may remove data from GDPR scope, but the standard
      is high and varies by jurisdiction.

      data-minimization and storage-limitation principles. Deletion procedures must
      be documented and enforceable.

      7. **Contractual Limits:** Merchant contracts and DPAs may restrict cross-merchant
      aggregation, secondary use, or sharing with vendors. These must be reviewed
      and potentially amended.

What are the cross-border transfer requirements (GDPR Chapter V, adequacy, SCCs) for processing and storing transaction data across regions?

1. **GDPR Chapter V (Cross-Border Transfers):** If any merchants, payers, or recipients are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, transfers of personal data to third countries require an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs), or another valid transfer mechanism. 6. **Retention and Deletion:** Seven-year retention must be justified under data-minimization and storage-limitation principles. Deletion procedures must be documented and enforceable.
7. **Contractual Limits:** Merchant contracts and DPAs may restrict cross-merchant aggregation, secondary use, or sharing with vendors. These must be reviewed and potentially amended.

4. **Confirm human review:** Confirm with product and risk teams whether the model's decisions are subject to human review and whether consumers receive notice or have opt-out rights.

6. **Document lawful basis:** Prepare a legitimate-interests assessment and document the lawful basis for processing.
