# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants a privacy-center workflow that lets merchants and payers request deletion of their personal information. The workflow would verify the requester, identify linked accounts and transactions, delete or de-identify eligible records, and explain any data retained for fraud prevention, accounting, disputes, legal obligations, or audit. The actors are data subjects, merchant administrators, Mosaic Relay Privacy, Compliance, Support, processors, and cloud providers. Requests would be acknowledged immediately and completed within the applicable legal deadline.

Known facts are that payment records, sanctions-screening results, dispute evidence, support tickets, and ledger entries are stored in separate systems. Some data is supplied by merchants, while other data is generated by Mosaic Relay. The company wants one global response template and a centralized deletion job. Missing facts include applicable jurisdictions, retention schedules, controller and processor allocations, identity-verification methods, backup deletion capability, household or employee requests, appeal rights, and whether deletion could impair suspicious-activity investigations. Legal advice is requested on scope and exemptions, response deadlines, required notices, data minimization, retention exceptions, and the technical evidence needed to show that the request was handled correctly.
