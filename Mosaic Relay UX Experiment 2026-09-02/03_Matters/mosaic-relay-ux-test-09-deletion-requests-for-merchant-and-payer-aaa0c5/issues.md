# Issues and workstreams

- Controller/processor allocation between Mosaic Relay and merchants is unresolved, determining who must honor a deletion request for merchant-supplied data and how obligations flow.
- Retention schedules and exemption categories are not defined; interim retention matrix, legal-hold flag, owner, retention end date, and documented exception reason needed before automation.
- Whether deletion could impair suspicious-activity/AML investigations is unresolved; a legal-hold/retention exception should be designed in by default.
- Deletion must propagate to backups; backup deletion capability needs to be confirmed as feasible.
- Appeal process for denied requests must be designed and implemented.
