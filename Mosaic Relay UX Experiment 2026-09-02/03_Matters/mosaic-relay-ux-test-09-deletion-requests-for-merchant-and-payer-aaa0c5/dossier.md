# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Deletion request workflow for merchant and payer personal data, anchored to multiple US state privacy laws, with a legal-hold/retention exception recommended by default and an appeal process and backup propagation required.

## Decision question

Design a privacy-center deletion workflow that lets merchants and payers request deletion of their personal information. The workflow verifies the requester (tiered by risk/data sensitivity), identifies linked accounts and transactions, deletes or de-identifies eligible records, and explains retained data. Requests are acknowledged immediately and completed within the applicable legal deadline. Anchored to multiple US state laws (global default). Includes an appeal process for denied requests and deletion propagation to backups; household and employee requests are out of scope. A legal-hold/retention exception is recommended by default given uncertainty about AML/suspicious-activity impact. No fixed launch date or deadline for the legal answer.
