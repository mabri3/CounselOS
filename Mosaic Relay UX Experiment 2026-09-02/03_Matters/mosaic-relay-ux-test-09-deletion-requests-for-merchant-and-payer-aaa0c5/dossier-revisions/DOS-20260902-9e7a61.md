# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Privacy review of a deletion-request workflow for merchant and payer data, anchored to multiple US state privacy laws.

## Decision question

Design a privacy-center deletion workflow that lets merchants and payers request deletion of their personal information: verify the requester (tiered by risk/data sensitivity), identify linked accounts and transactions, delete or de-identify eligible records, explain retained data, acknowledge immediately, and complete within the applicable legal deadline. Anchored to multiple US state laws (global default). Must propagate deletion to backups; household and employee requests are out of scope; include an appeal process for denied requests. No fixed launch or legal-answer deadline; design/control mapping starts now with interim manual intake and hold controls.
