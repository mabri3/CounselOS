# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 02 — Background Checks for Support and Payment Operations Staff

## Chosen path

Adopt a global background-screening framework with jurisdiction-specific carve-outs; use identity and sanctions screening as the initial baseline, exclude credit checks by default, and require role-based review before criminal-record or employment-history checks.

## Rationale

The same search and decision process is not lawful or proportionate across California, New York, Texas, Ireland, and the United Kingdom. A role-based baseline with local overrides supports data minimization, individualized review, and controlled access while preserving a path to verify current law.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Confirm exact permissions and role necessity for all four access categories
- Complete local-law and primary-authority verification
- Finalize standalone notices, authorizations, and adverse-action workflow
- Implement a worker dispute and correction process
- Set raw-report, decision-record, and audit-log retention schedules
- Confirm contractor-agency screening and vendor data-protection terms
- Do not launch production screening until conditions are complete

## Not decided

- Which searches will be selected for each role
- Whether contractor agencies already perform checks
- Lawful lookback periods by search and jurisdiction
- Retention and deletion periods
- Whether three-year and role-change rechecks are lawful and necessary
