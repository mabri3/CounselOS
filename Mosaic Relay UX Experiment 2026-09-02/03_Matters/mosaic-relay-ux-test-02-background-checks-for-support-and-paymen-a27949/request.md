# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans to require background checks for employees and contractors who can view payment data, change payout controls, approve refunds, or investigate disputes. The proposed operation would collect consent, send identifying information to a background-check vendor, and block access until the check is complete. Results would be categorized as clear, review, or ineligible. The actors are applicants, current staff, contractors, Human Resources, Security, Legal, the vendor, and managers requesting access. Checks would occur before access is granted and every three years, with additional checks after certain role changes.

Known facts are that the company has employees in California, New York, Texas, Ireland, and the United Kingdom. The vendor offers criminal-record, identity, sanctions, credit, and employment-history searches, but the business has not selected which searches to use. Mosaic Relay wants to use one global policy and does not want managers to see detailed reports. Missing facts include the roles that legally require screening, the intended lookback periods, local notice and consent rules, adverse-action procedures, retention and deletion periods, and whether contractor agencies already perform checks. Legal advice is requested on which checks are lawful and necessary for each role, the required notices and authorizations, the review standards, and the process for disputing inaccurate results.
