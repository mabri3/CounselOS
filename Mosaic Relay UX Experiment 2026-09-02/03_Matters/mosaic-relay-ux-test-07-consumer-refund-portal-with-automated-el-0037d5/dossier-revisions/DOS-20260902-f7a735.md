# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Consumer self-service refund portal for card and ACH payments with automated 30-day eligibility, full refunds only, merchant notice, and routing of card-network disputes and ACH unauthorized/error claims to the appropriate external processes.

## Decision question

Mosaic Relay wants to launch a self-service refund portal for card and ACH payments. A payer enters a transaction reference, verifies a phone number or email, selects a reason, and requests a refund. The system auto-approves requests within 30 days for defined reasons (not as described/defective; did not receive goods/services; cancellation/change of mind; duplicate or incorrect charge), denies requests outside the window, and sends the merchant a notice. Full refunds only. Funds return to the original payment method when possible. Legal advice is requested on the portal's role, eligibility and notice wording, authentication and fraud controls, timing requirements, recordkeeping, and when a denied request must route to the merchant or another dispute process. Working assumptions: limited pilot in ~60 days subject to legal review; do not silently deny (show reason, window, refund options, merchant-support route); route card-network disputes to issuer/network and ACH unauthorized/error claims to bank/processor return process; retain a defined immutable audit trail. Remaining open item: accessible-language and accessibility standards for portal notices across the footprint.
