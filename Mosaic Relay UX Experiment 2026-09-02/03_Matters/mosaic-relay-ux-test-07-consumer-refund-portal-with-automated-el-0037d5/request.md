# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to launch a self-service refund portal for card and ACH payments. A payer would enter a transaction reference, verify a phone number or email, select a reason, and request a refund. The system would automatically approve requests within 30 days for defined reasons, deny requests outside the window, and send the merchant a notice. The actors are payers, merchants, recipients, Mosaic Relay, processors, banks, and customer-support staff. The portal would be available at all times, with funds returned to the original payment method when possible.

Known facts are that Mosaic Relay wants to lower support volume and give consumers faster outcomes. Merchants remain responsible for their underlying goods or services, and the portal would not resolve card-network disputes. Missing facts include whether Mosaic Relay is acting as the merchant's agent, the applicable refund and cancellation laws by product and location, authentication strength, treatment of partial refunds and recurring payments, ACH return rules, accessible-language requirements, and escalation rights after denial. Legal advice is requested on the portal's role, eligibility and notice wording, authentication and fraud controls, timing requirements, recordkeeping, and when a denied request must be routed to the merchant or another dispute process.
