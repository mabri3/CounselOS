    trigger. But the "no custody" posture must be *structurally real*, not just contractual
    — if Mosaic Relay ever holds seller funds (even briefly for payouts), it can be

    right. Beneficial-owner identification (25%+ owners + control person) is **required**

    finish before activation, not after.

    data set** and may override Mosaic Relay''s own tiering. This is the key unresolved
    dependency.

    **5. Adverse-action notices.** FCRA/ECOA adverse-action notices are triggered
    **if** the vendor data used to reject a seller is a "consumer report." Whether

    **6. Privacy/retention.** BSA recordkeeping requires retaining CDD/transaction
    records for **five years** after the relationship ends — recommend that as the
    retention floor.

    the seller relationship/description; Mosaic Relay owns verification, sanctions,
    risk scoring, and the activation decision. Document it in the marketplace agreement
