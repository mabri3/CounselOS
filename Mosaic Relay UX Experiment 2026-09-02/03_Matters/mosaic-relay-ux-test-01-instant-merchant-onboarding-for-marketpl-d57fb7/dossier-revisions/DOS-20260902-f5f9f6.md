# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Mosaic Relay, a payment-infrastructure provider, wants to reduce marketplace seller activation from two business days to five minutes by running automated KYB, identity verification, sanctions screening, and risk scoring during signup. Low-risk sellers would receive immediate limited processing access (capped at $10,000 monthly volume with delayed payouts), while higher-risk sellers remain pending. Counsel is involved because the model must satisfy card-network rules, acquiring-bank requirements, and US AML/KYC obligations before the first payment is processed. The business needs the legal answer before a planned launch, though no fixed date has been set.

## Decision question

Can Mosaic Relay activate marketplace sellers for immediate limited payment processing (up to $10,000/month with delayed payouts) based solely on automated KYB, beneficial-ownership collection, and sanctions screening completed during a five-minute signup, or must manual review or additional verification be completed before the first payment?
