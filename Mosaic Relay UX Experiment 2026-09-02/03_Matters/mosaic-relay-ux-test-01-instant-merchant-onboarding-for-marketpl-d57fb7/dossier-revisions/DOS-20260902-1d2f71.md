# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Mosaic Relay, a payment-infrastructure provider, wants to cut marketplace-seller activation from two business days to five minutes by running automated KYB, identity verification, sanctions screening, and risk scoring during signup. Low-risk sellers would get immediate limited processing access (capped at $10,000/month with delayed payouts); higher-risk sellers stay pending. Counsel is involved because the instant model raises KYC/KYB, beneficial-owner, sanctions, disclosure, state money-transmission, and consumer-protection questions that must be answered before a planned launch. The consumer-protection piece matters because sellers and their customers can be harmed by unclear disclosures, mishandled rejections, or deceptive onboarding representations.

## Decision question

Before launch, which consumer-protection and disclosure obligations must Mosaic Relay build into the instant-onboarding flow — covering seller-facing disclosures (fees, payout timing, holds, rejection/adverse-action notices) and end-customer protections — so that the five-minute activation model does not create unfair/deceptive-practices exposure under federal and state law, and what minimum notices and records must exist before a seller's first payment?
