# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Determine whether Mosaic Relay's proposed five-minute instant merchant onboarding for marketplace sellers (limited to $10K monthly volume, delayed payouts, low-risk only) is permissible, and identify the minimum facts and records required before activation, covering KYC/KYB, beneficial-owner, sanctions, disclosures, state money-transmission, and consumer-protection controls.

## Decision question

Determine whether Mosaic Relay's proposed five-minute instant merchant onboarding for marketplace sellers (limited to $10K monthly volume, delayed payouts, low-risk only) is permissible, and identify the minimum facts and records required before activation, covering KYC/KYB, beneficial-owner, sanctions, disclosures, state money-transmission, and consumer-protection controls.

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay is not a bank and does not hold deposits.
- Instant sellers would initially be limited to $10,000 in monthly volume.
- Instant sellers would receive delayed payouts.
- The proposed experience collects legal name, business type, tax ID, beneficial owners, government ID, business address, expected monthly volume, payment methods, and payout destination.
- Automated KYB, identity verification, sanctions screening, and risk scoring would run during signup.
- Low-risk sellers would receive limited processing access immediately; higher-risk sellers would remain pending.
- The marketplace customer would provide the seller relationship and business description.
- Timing is before the seller accepts its first payment, with periodic rescreening after activation.
- Where are the marketplace customer and its sellers located? This drives which state money-transmission, consumer-protection, and disclosure regimes apply. — Not yet determined
- Who owns customer due diligence for the sellers — the marketplace or Mosaic Relay? — Shared / to be allocated
- Have the acquiring partners set specific requirements for what must be verified before a seller processes its first payment? — Under discussion with partners

## Assumptions

- Mosaic Relay relies on regulated acquiring partners for parts of the payment chain, so acquiring-bank requirements may govern what data must be collected before activation.
- The instant-onboarding flow is intended for US-based sellers unless otherwise stated.

## Issues and workstreams

- Whether limited-access activation before full KYB completion is permissible under acquiring-bank and card-network rules
- Which minimum facts and records must be collected and verified before a seller can accept its first payment
- State money-transmission licensing perimeter for the instant-onboarding model
- Consumer-protection and disclosure obligations for marketplace sellers and their customers
- How the shared CDD obligation between marketplace and Mosaic Relay should be allocated

## Open questions

- Exact customer and seller locations (jurisdictions)
- Acquiring-bank requirements for the instant-onboarding flow
- Data retention period for collected KYC/KYB records
- Vendor decision logic for KYB/identity/risk scoring
- How rejected sellers receive notice
- Whether the $10K monthly cap and delayed payouts are hard limits or adjustable
