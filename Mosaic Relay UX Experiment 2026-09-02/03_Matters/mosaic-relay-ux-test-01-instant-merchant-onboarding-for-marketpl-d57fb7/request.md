# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to reduce seller activation time from two business days to five minutes. The proposed experience would collect legal name, business type, tax ID, beneficial owners, government ID, business address, expected monthly volume, payment methods, and payout destination. Automated KYB, identity verification, sanctions screening, and risk scoring would run during signup. Low-risk sellers would receive limited processing access immediately, while higher-risk sellers would remain pending. The actors are the seller, its beneficial owners, Mosaic Relay’s onboarding service, verification vendors, acquiring partners, and the marketplace customer. The timing is before the seller accepts its first payment, with periodic rescreening after activation.

Known facts are that Mosaic Relay is not a bank, does not hold deposits, and would initially limit instant sellers to $10,000 in monthly volume and delayed payouts. The marketplace customer would provide the seller relationship and business description. Missing facts include the exact customer and seller locations, the acquiring-bank requirements, the data retention period, the vendor decision logic, how rejected sellers receive notice, and whether the marketplace or Mosaic Relay owns customer due diligence. Legal advice is requested on the required KYC/KYB, beneficial-owner, sanctions, disclosures, state money-transmission, and consumer-protection controls. Legal must decide whether the limited-access model is permissible and identify the minimum facts and records required before activation.
