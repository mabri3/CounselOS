# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 01 — Instant Merchant Onboarding for Marketplace Sellers

## Chosen path

Proceed with a conditional, gated build of limited-access instant onboarding; do not launch the five-minute tier until the legal and partner gates below are confirmed.

## Rationale

The design is conditionally permissible on the current US-only assumption because Mosaic Relay is not a bank, does not hold deposits, uses a $10,000 cap and delayed payouts, and plans low-risk activation. This is a build decision, not launch approval. Partner requirements, fund-flow/custody, state licensing, card-network rules, CDD allocation, vendor logic, and notice duties remain unresolved.

## Recommendation disposition

Modified: Modified to make clear that approval is for a gated build only, with no launch authorization until the listed conditions are met.

## Conditions

- Acquiring-partner minimum pre-activation requirements confirmed in writing
- Flow of funds and custody confirmed in writing; Mosaic Relay never holds funds
- 50-state licensing survey completed and launch states gated to a verified path
- Identity and sanctions screening completed before activation
- Card-network payfac registration and underwriting confirmed
- CDD allocation documented with one accountable owner per control
- Rejected-seller adverse-action and notice process designed
- FinCEN MSB status analyzed separately

## Not decided

- Exact customer and seller states
- Data retention period and final schedule
- Vendor decision logic and consumer-report status
- How rejected sellers receive notice
- Current card-network rule versions
- Final acquiring-partner requirements
- Final CDD allocation and payfac/MSB status
