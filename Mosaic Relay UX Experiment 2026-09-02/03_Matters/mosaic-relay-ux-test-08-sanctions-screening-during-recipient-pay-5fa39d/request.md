# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to screen every payout recipient immediately before release. The system would compare recipient names, addresses, business details, and bank information against sanctions and restricted-party lists. Potential matches would place the payout into a manual-review queue. A confirmed match would stop the payout and restrict the account. The actors are merchants, sellers, contractors, recipients, Mosaic Relay Compliance, screening vendors, banks, and government authorities. Screening would occur at onboarding, before each payout, and when list updates are received.

Known facts are that recipient information can be incomplete, names may be transliterated, and some payouts cross borders. The product team wants to show recipients only that “additional review is required” to reduce evasion. Missing facts include the exact countries and currencies supported, the lists used, match-scoring thresholds, escalation deadlines, blocked-funds handling, reporting obligations, false-positive correction procedures, and whether merchants can receive match explanations. Legal advice is requested on required screening scope, blocking and rejection duties, reporting and record retention, communications to affected parties, and the controls needed to prevent discriminatory or inconsistent treatment of legitimate recipients.
