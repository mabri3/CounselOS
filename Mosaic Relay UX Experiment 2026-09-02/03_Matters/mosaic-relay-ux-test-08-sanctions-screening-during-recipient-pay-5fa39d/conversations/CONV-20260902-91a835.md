    ## Summary of the request

    Mosaic Relay (Product) wants to screen **every payout recipient immediately before
    release** against sanctions and restricted-party lists, comparing names, addresses,
    business details, and bank information. Potential matches go to a manual-review
    queue; a confirmed match stops the payout and restricts the account. Screening
    would run at onboarding, before each payout, and on list updates. Known constraints:
    recipient data can be incomplete, names may be transliterated, and some payouts
    cross borders. The product team wants to tell recipients only that "additional
    review is required" to reduce evasion.

    **Legal advice requested:** required screening scope, blocking and rejection duties,
    reporting and record retention, communications to affected parties, and controls
    to prevent discriminatory or inconsistent treatment of legitimate recipients.

    ## What I''ve recorded

    - **Working ask** — legal review of the pre-release sanctions screening control
    across those five areas.

    - **8 reported facts** — the screening design, match handling, screening triggers,
    data-quality constraints, and the "additional review" communication choice.

    - **6 issues** — screening scope, blocking/reporting duties, record retention,
    the evasion-sensitive communication, and fair-treatment/false-positive controls.

    - **3 assumptions** — jurisdiction scope, lists used, and thresholds/handling
    are not yet defined.

    ## What I need to move the analysis

    The single most decision-changing unknown is **jurisdiction scope**, because it
    determines which sanctions regimes and blocking/reporting duties apply. I''ve
    prioritized four questions:

    1. **Which countries and currencies** will the payout screening cover? (drives
    the applicable sanctions regimes)

    2. **Which lists** are planned for use (OFAC SDN, EU/UK, vendor-aggregated)?

    3. **Target launch timing** for the control?

    4. **Funds handling** — does Mosaic Relay hold funds while a payout is in review
    or blocked?

    Answering these will let me scope the legal workstreams (US OFAC vs. EU/UK regimes,
    blocking vs. rejection, reporting, and the evasion-sensitive messaging) rather
    than analyze in the abstract.'
