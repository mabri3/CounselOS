# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Mosaic Relay, a B2B payment-infrastructure provider, plans to screen every payout recipient against sanctions and restricted-party lists at onboarding, before each payout, and when lists update. Potential matches route to manual review; confirmed matches stop the payout and restrict the account. Counsel is involved because the program implicates sanctions blocking and rejection duties, reporting and record-retention obligations, tipping-off constraints on recipient communications, and fair-treatment controls for legitimate recipients. Several scoping facts — jurisdictions, lists, thresholds, and blocked-funds handling — remain undefined, and the matter is rated high risk.

## Decision question

What false-positive correction and appeal procedures must Mosaic Relay build into its pre-release payout screening so that legitimate recipients flagged by name, address, business-detail, or bank-data matching can be cleared quickly and consistently — given incomplete data, transliterated names, and cross-border payouts — without violating sanctions blocking duties or tipping-off constraints?

## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay proposes to screen every payout recipient immediately before release against sanctions and restricted-party lists.
- Screening would compare recipient names, addresses, business details, and bank information against sanctions and restricted-party lists.
- Potential matches would place the payout into a manual-review queue.
- A confirmed match would stop the payout and restrict the account.
- Screening would occur at onboarding, before each payout, and when list updates are received.
- Recipient information can be incomplete, names may be transliterated, and some payouts cross borders.
- The product team wants to show recipients only that 'additional review is required' to reduce evasion.
- Actors include merchants, sellers, contractors, recipients, Mosaic Relay Compliance, screening vendors, banks, and government authorities.

- How will Mosaic Relay handle funds while a payout is in review or blocked? — Not yet defined
- Can merchants receive match explanations for their recipients' payouts, or is match information restricted to Compliance? — Not yet decided
- What match-scoring thresholds and escalation deadlines are planned for the screening control? — Not yet defined; match-scoring thresholds and escalation deadlines remain to be set.

## Assumptions

- Jurisdiction scope is not yet defined; analysis will need to be scoped to specific countries/currencies once known
- The specific sanctions/restricted-party lists used are not yet identified
- Match-scoring thresholds, escalation deadlines, and blocked-funds handling are not yet defined

## Issues and workstreams

- Required screening scope and whether pre-release screening of every recipient is legally required or best practice across the jurisdictions served
- Blocking and rejection duties when a match is confirmed, including whether funds must be blocked, rejected, or reported
- Reporting and record-retention obligations to government authorities (e.g., OFAC/SDN-style reporting) and how long records must be kept
- Communications to affected parties, including whether the 'additional review is required' message is permissible or risks tipping off / evasion concerns
- Controls to prevent discriminatory or inconsistent treatment of legitimate recipients, including false-positive correction procedures and match-scoring thresholds

## Open questions

- Which jurisdictions and currencies will the screening cover? This determines which regimes' delisting, unblocking, and due-process expectations apply (e.g., OFAC SDN reconsideration vs. EU/UK delisting and licensing routes).
- Which sanctions and restricted-party lists will be used, and will any non-government vendor lists drive account restrictions beyond legal blocking duties?
- What match-scoring thresholds and escalation deadlines are planned? These set the volume of false positives the correction procedure must absorb and the service-level expectations for clearing them.
- How will funds be held while a payout is in review or blocked, and does that handling differ for a false positive versus a confirmed match?
- Can merchants receive match explanations for their recipients' payouts, or is match information restricted to Compliance? This shapes what the appeal channel can disclose.
