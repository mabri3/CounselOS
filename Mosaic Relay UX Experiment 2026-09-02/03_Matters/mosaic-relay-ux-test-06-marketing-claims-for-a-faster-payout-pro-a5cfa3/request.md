# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans a campaign for a new payout feature. Draft copy says, “Get your sellers paid instantly, with no hidden holds,” and “The fastest way to move money to contractors.” The product supports same-day payouts for eligible recipients, but payments can be delayed by risk reviews, bank outages, weekends, cutoff times, sanctions screening, or recipient verification. The actors are Mosaic Relay Marketing, Product, Sales, merchants, sellers, contractors, payment partners, and a creative agency. The campaign would launch in the United States and Canada next quarter through email, paid search, social media, and customer presentations.

Known facts are that payout speed depends on the recipient’s bank and payment rail. Fees may apply to expedited payouts, and some recipients must complete identity verification. Mosaic Relay wants a simple headline and plans to use customer performance statistics in case studies. Missing facts include substantiation for “fastest,” the tested payout-time distribution, fee disclosures, audience targeting, approval ownership, consent for marketing emails, and Canadian language and privacy requirements. Legal advice is requested on compliant claims, qualifiers, comparative advertising, fee and eligibility disclosures, evidence standards, testimonial approvals, and channel-specific marketing rules.
