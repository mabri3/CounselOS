## Decision question

Legal review of the payout-feature marketing campaign for US/Canada launch next quarter. Draft copy claims 'instantly,' 'fastest,' and 'no hidden holds' — none of which are substantiated (no tested payout-time data). Claims must be softened. Recommend compliant claims, qualifiers, fee/eligibility disclosure approach, evidence standards, testimonial approvals, and channel-specific rules.
