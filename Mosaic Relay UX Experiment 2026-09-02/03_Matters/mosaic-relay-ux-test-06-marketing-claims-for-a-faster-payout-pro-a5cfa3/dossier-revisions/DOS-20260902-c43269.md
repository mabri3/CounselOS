- Draft copy claims 'Get your sellers paid instantly, with no hidden holds' and 'The fastest way to move money to contractors.'
- The product supports same-day payouts for eligible recipients only.
- Payments can be delayed by risk reviews, bank outages, weekends, cutoff times, sanctions screening, or recipient verification.
