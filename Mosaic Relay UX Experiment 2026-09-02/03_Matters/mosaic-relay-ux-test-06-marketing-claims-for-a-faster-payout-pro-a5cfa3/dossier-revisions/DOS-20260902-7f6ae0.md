# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Marketing review of a faster-payout feature campaign. Core legal issues: unsubstantiated superlative claims, comparative advertising, fee/eligibility disclosures, testimonial evidence standards, and Canadian language/CASL requirements. Output modeled on Path 01 format with pre-launch gates.

## Decision question

Provide legal advice on compliant marketing claims for Mosaic Relay's new faster-payout feature launching in the US and Canada next quarter. Draft copy claims "Get your sellers paid instantly, with no hidden holds" and "The fastest way to move money to contractors." The product supports same-day payouts for eligible recipients only, with delays possible from risk reviews, bank outages, weekends, cutoff times, sanctions screening, or recipient verification. Output should be modeled on Path 01's format (clear bottom line, sourcing tags, pre-launch gates). Legal answer needed before launch.
