# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Mosaic Relay (a fictional test company) plans a US–Canada marketing campaign next quarter for a new payout feature, with draft copy claiming "Get your sellers paid instantly, with no hidden holds" and "The fastest way to move money to contractors." The product actually supports same-day payouts only for eligible recipients, and payouts can be delayed by risk reviews, bank outages, weekends, cutoff times, sanctions screening, or recipient verification. Counsel is involved because the claims are currently unsubstantiated (no tested payout-time data exists), fee and eligibility disclosures are undecided, no written customer consent exists for case-study testimonials, and the Canadian consent/language approach is unresolved. The business needs the legal answer before the planned launch.

## Decision question

Before next quarter's US–Canada launch across email, paid search, social, and customer presentations, which replacement headline claims and qualifier/disclosure package can Mosaic Relay run without tested payout-time data, and what consent, French-language, and testimonial-approval steps must Marketing complete first so the campaign does not create deceptive-advertising or CASL exposure?
