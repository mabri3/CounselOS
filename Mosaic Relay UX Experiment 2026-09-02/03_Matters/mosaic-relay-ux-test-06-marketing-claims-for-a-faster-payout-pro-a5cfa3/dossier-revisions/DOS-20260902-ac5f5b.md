## Material facts

- Mosaic Relay plans a marketing campaign for a new payout feature launching in the US and Canada next quarter.
- Draft copy claims 'Get your sellers paid instantly, with no hidden holds' and 'The fastest way to move money to contractors.'
- The product supports same-day payouts for eligible recipients only.
- Payments can be delayed by risk reviews, bank outages, weekends, cutoff times, sanctions screening, or recipient verification.
- Payout speed depends on the recipient's bank and payment rail.
- Fees may apply to expedited payouts.
- Some recipients must complete identity verification.
- The campaign will run through email, paid search, social media, and customer presentations.
- Mosaic Relay wants a simple headline and plans to use customer performance statistics in case studies.
- Do you have tested payout-time data (actual distribution of payout times, including any delays) that could substantiate 'fastest' or 'instant'? This is the single most decision-changing fact for the headline. — No tested data yet — we need to soften the claims
- How will same-day eligibility and any expedited-payout fees be disclosed in the copy? — Not decided yet — recommend an approach

## Assumptions

- Campaign launch timing is 'next quarter' from the request date (Q4 2026).
- The payout feature is not yet publicly launched and this is pre-launch marketing review.

## Issues and workstreams

- 'Instantly' and 'fastest' are absolute superlative claims the product cannot support given same-day-only timing and no tested payout-time data.
- 'No hidden holds' conflicts with the fact that risk reviews, sanctions screening, and verification can delay or hold payouts.
- Comparative claim 'fastest' requires substantiation against competitors, which is not available.
- Fee and eligibility disclosures needed for expedited payouts and verification requirements.
- Customer performance statistics in case studies require evidence standards and testimonial/endorsement approvals.
- Canadian language (French) and privacy/marketing-consent requirements (CASL) apply to the Canada launch.

## Open questions

- Tested payout-time distribution (actual range of payout times, not just 'same-day').
- Fee structure for expedited payouts and whether fees vary by rail/bank.
- Eligibility criteria for same-day payouts and how they will be disclosed.
- Audience targeting for the campaign.
- Approval ownership for final marketing copy.
- Consent status for marketing emails (US CAN-SPAM and Canadian CASL).
- Canadian language (French) and privacy requirements for the Canada launch.
- Do you have written consent from customers to use their performance data and quotes as testimonials in case studies?
- Who owns final approval of the marketing copy before launch?
- Will the Canada campaign run in French, and do you have CASL-compliant consent for Canadian marketing emails?
