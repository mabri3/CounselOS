# Issues and workstreams

- 'Instantly' and 'fastest' are absolute superlative claims the product cannot support given same-day-only timing and no tested payout-time data
- 'No hidden holds' conflicts with the fact that risk reviews, sanctions screening, and verification can delay or hold payouts
- Comparative claim 'fastest' requires substantiation against competitors, which is not available
- Fee and eligibility disclosures needed for expedited payouts and verification requirements; fee schedule itself not yet provided
- Customer performance statistics in case studies require evidence standards and testimonial/endorsement approvals; no written consent obtained yet
- Canadian language (French) and privacy/marketing-consent requirements (CASL) apply to the Canada launch; approach undecided
- Final approval owner for campaign copy not yet assigned
