# Recommendation

## Recommended path
Proceed with the proposed $250 account-credit (fee-offset) structure for a six-month U.S.-focused pilot. Do not use cash rewards at this stage. Treat expansion to any non-U.S. supported market as a separate launch decision.

## Conditions before launch
1. Use clear, conspicuous material-connection language at each referral touchpoint: referral links, email invitations, in-product banners, sales scripts, and testimonials. State that the referring merchant may receive a $250 account credit after the referred business processes $5,000 in eligible volume.
2. Publish plain-language terms covering the $5,000 eligible-volume test, six-month window, non-cash credit, caps, exclusions, fraud/abuse rules, and when the credit is applied.
3. Use only genuine, current testimonials. Keep substantiation for any savings, volume, or other performance claim. Add a clear qualification if results are not typical.
4. Use CAN-SPAM controls for email. Exclude promotional SMS until prior-consent and opt-out controls are confirmed.
5. Confirm whether the credit changes the referred business's pricing disclosure. Obtain tax counsel and accounting sign-off on credit classification, aggregate-credit reporting, and any W-9/1099 process.
6. Identify supported markets and screen regulated industries and payment-partner restrictions before any non-U.S. or regulated-vertical launch.

## Approval process
Marketing, Sales, Product, Tax/Accounting, and payment-partner owners should approve the final terms, copy, consent flow, testimonial file, eligibility rules, and market matrix. Legal should complete a documented pre-launch review and a post-launch monitoring check during the six-month window.

## Key assumptions and gaps
The market list, launch date, pricing impact, referral disclosures, testimonial status, consent design, tax treatment, eligibility exclusions, regulated-industry treatment, and partner restrictions remain unconfirmed. The research dossier is model-generated and reports no external authority retrieved; verify primary sources before relying on it for launch.
