# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to offer merchants a referral credit for introducing new businesses. A referring merchant would receive a $250 account credit after the new business processes $5,000 in eligible volume. The proposed experience would use referral links, email invitations, in-product banners, and customer testimonials. Sales staff could also offer the incentive during onboarding calls. The actors are referring merchants, referred businesses, Mosaic Relay Sales and Marketing, payment partners, and the new businesses’ owners and beneficial owners. The program would run for six months and could be promoted in all supported markets.

Known facts are that the credit would reduce future platform fees and would not be paid as cash. The company wants to say, “Earn rewards for growing the Mosaic Relay community,” and may display the number of successful referrals. Missing facts include whether the credit changes pricing disclosures, how referral relationships are disclosed, whether testimonials are genuine and representative, email and text consent, tax treatment, eligibility exclusions, treatment of regulated industries, and rules outside the United States. Legal advice is requested on incentive and endorsement disclosures, marketing-consent requirements, consumer-protection risks, tax and accounting treatment, geographic restrictions, and the final terms and approval process.
