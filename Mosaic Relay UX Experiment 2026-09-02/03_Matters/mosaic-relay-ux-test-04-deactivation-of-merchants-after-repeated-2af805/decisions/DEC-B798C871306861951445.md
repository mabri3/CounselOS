# Approve tiered, contractually grounded merchant-deactivation process for internal pilot

## Chosen path

Proceed with the recommended tiered, contractually grounded deactivation process for an internal pilot: internal chargeback threshold at or slightly below verified network thresholds; month-1 warning; remediation plan and payment-method restriction; month-3 deactivation; 30-day cure with written appeal; documented-exception marketplace override; and 90-day refund/dispute access after deactivation.

## Rationale

The merchant agreement already permits suspension for excessive disputes but leaves notice content, cure period, and appeal process undefined. A tiered, contractually grounded process provides consistency, satisfies network monitoring obligations, and gives merchants fair notice and cure while preserving Mosaic Relay's ability to deactivate for sustained excessive chargebacks. The internal threshold set at or slightly below verified network thresholds ensures Mosaic Relay acts before network-level exposure triggers, and the documented-exception override preserves marketplace flexibility without ceding final decision authority.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Confirm applicable jurisdictions, network programs, and local notice/cure rules with Risk and Compliance before rollout
- Confirm network-specific threshold values and program tiers (Visa/Mastercard monitoring programs) with acquiring-bank partners
- Define how the internal threshold interacts with network thresholds (operative trigger) before rollout
- Confirm post-deactivation timelines for refunds, pending authorizations, and dispute responses against network rules
- Define required reporting and customer communications after deactivation
