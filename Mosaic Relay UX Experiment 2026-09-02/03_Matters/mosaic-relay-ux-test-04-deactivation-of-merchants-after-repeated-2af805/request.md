# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to deactivate merchants whose chargeback rate remains above a defined threshold for three consecutive months. The proposed experience would warn the merchant, require a remediation plan, restrict new payment methods, and then disable processing if performance does not improve. Existing refunds and dispute responses would remain available for 90 days. The actors are merchants, their customers, Mosaic Relay Risk and Support, card networks, acquiring banks, and marketplace customers. Warnings would begin after the first month above threshold, and deactivation would occur after the third month unless a documented exception is approved.

Known facts are that the company has network monitoring obligations and wants a consistent process. The merchant agreement permits suspension for excessive disputes but does not define the notice content, cure period, or appeal process. Some merchants sell digital goods, while others sell physical goods or recurring services. Missing facts include network-specific thresholds, whether consumer-law complaints are included in the metric, how false or friendly-fraud disputes are treated, the treatment of pending authorizations, and whether marketplace customers can override the decision. Legal advice is requested on the metric, warning and termination notices, cure and appeal rights, refund and dispute obligations after deactivation, and any required reporting or customer communications.
