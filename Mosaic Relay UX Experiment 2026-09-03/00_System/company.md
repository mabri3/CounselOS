# Company profile

## Company Name

Mosaic Relay

## Website Url

Not provided.

## Summary

Mosaic Relay is a fictional payments technology company with no affiliation with Stripe, Checkout.com, Marqeta, Manifest Financial, Chime, or any other real company. It provides payment infrastructure through APIs and hosted tools. Customers use it to accept card, ACH, and local payment methods; route and split funds; issue refunds; manage disputes; pay sellers, contractors, and other recipients; and operate payment programs. Its services include merchant onboarding, identity verification, KYB/KYC, sanctions screening, fraud monitoring, transaction risk scoring, payout controls, reconciliation reports, and operations dashboards. Mosaic Relay does not provide deposit accounts or banking services, and it does not hold customer deposits. This company profile and the active vault named Mosaic Relay UX Experiment 2026-09-03 are for this fictional UX experiment only and must not be treated as a real business record. The fictional legal owner is Jordan Lee, Senior Product Counsel, with responsibility for BSA/AML, payments regulation, consumer protection, privacy, and marketing compliance. Mosaic Relay serves online marketplaces, platforms, SaaS businesses, and other merchants that need embedded payment operations. It operates primarily in the United States in this fictional scenario, with any expansion outside the U.S. subject to separate legal review. Key partners include acquiring banks, payment processors, card networks, identity and fraud vendors, sanctions-data providers, and cloud providers; partner names and commercial terms are fictional and not supplied. Teams include Product, Engineering, Payments Operations, Risk and Fraud, Compliance, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Security, Privacy, and Legal. Legal topics include money transmission and payments licensing, BSA/AML and sanctions controls, KYC/KYB, network and processor rules, funds flow and safeguarding, consumer protection, disputes and refunds, privacy and data security, vendor and cloud risk, accessibility, and marketing claims and consent. The risk posture is cautious and control-focused: launch decisions should document assumptions, map funds flows, confirm partner requirements, test operational controls, and escalate material uncertainty to Jordan Lee. Operating principles are clear ownership, least-privilege access, auditability, plain-language customer communications, privacy by design, reliable reconciliation, quick issue escalation, and useful best-effort work product. Recommendations must remain separate from recorded decisions.

## Business Model

Mosaic Relay operates a B2B SaaS and API platform, charging fees for payment processing, platform usage, and value-added services such as fraud monitoring and reconciliation. Revenue is generated through transaction-based fees and subscription tiers, with no interest income from customer funds as it does not hold deposits.

## Products Services

Core products include payment APIs for card, ACH, and local methods; hosted payment pages; and dashboards for operations. Services encompass merchant onboarding, identity verification (KYB/KYC), sanctions screening, fraud monitoring, transaction risk scoring, payout controls, dispute management, refunds, reconciliation reports, and split-funding capabilities.

## Jurisdictions

Operates primarily in the United States in this fictional scenario. Any expansion outside the U.S. is subject to separate legal review.

## Regulatory Context

Subject to U.S. payments regulations, including money transmission licensing, BSA/AML and sanctions, KYC/KYB, card network and processor rules, consumer protection, and privacy laws. Compliance is overseen by fictional legal owner Jordan Lee.

## Data Practices

Processes personal and transactional data for identity verification, fraud monitoring, and compliance. Data is stored and processed via cloud providers, with privacy by design and least-privilege access principles. No deposit accounts or holding of customer funds.

## Risk Posture

Cautious and control-focused. Launch decisions require documented assumptions, mapped funds flows, confirmed partner requirements, tested operational controls, and escalation of material uncertainty to Jordan Lee. Emphasis on auditability, clear ownership, and separation of recommendations from recorded decisions.
