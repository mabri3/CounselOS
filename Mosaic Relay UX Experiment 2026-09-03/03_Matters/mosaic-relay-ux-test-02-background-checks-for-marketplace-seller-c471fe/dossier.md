# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

FCRA consumer-report framework applies because the marketplace may use criminal-record and adverse-media results for eligibility decisions; the marketplace is the FCRA 'user' and Mosaic Relay is the workflow provider contracting with the vendor.

## Decision question

May Mosaic Relay provide a seller-screening workflow (identity verification, sanctions screening, criminal-record, and adverse-media checks) for a marketplace customer, where the marketplace may use results to deny, restrict, or deactivate sellers? Which notices and consents are required, what restrictions apply to criminal or adverse-media data, and how should compliance duties be allocated between Mosaic Relay and the marketplace? Answer needed before a planned launch (customer wants the operation live in eight weeks).

## Material facts

- A Mosaic Relay customer operates a marketplace for home-service providers and wants to screen sellers before enabling payouts.
- The proposed experience asks each seller for consent, legal name, date of birth, address, and government identification.
- Mosaic Relay would send the information to an identity and background-check vendor and display a status such as 'cleared,' 'review needed,' or 'not eligible.'
- The marketplace makes the final activation decision.
- Actors are sellers, the marketplace, Mosaic Relay, the screening vendor, and Mosaic Relay support staff.
- The customer wants this operation available in eight weeks.
- Business goal is to reduce fraud and protect marketplace customers.
- The check may include identity verification, sanctions screening, criminal-record information, and adverse media, but the exact package is unsettled.
- It is not known whether the marketplace will use results for employment-like decisions.
- It is not known whether sellers are independent businesses or individuals.
- It is not known which countries will be included.
- Report retention period is not decided.
- Dispute and false-match handling is not decided.

- Are the sellers individuals, businesses, or both? — Individuals (natural persons)
- What does the screening package include? — Not yet decided
- Which countries will be included in the screening scope? — Not yet decided

The marketplace should own the seller-facing appeal; Mosaic Relay should require the vendor to investigate and correct inaccurate data. Final periods and roles need privacy and FCRA review.

- Duty allocation: Mosaic Relay contracts with the vendor and provides the workflow; the marketplace is the FCRA 'user' making adverse decisions and owning adverse-action and dispute duties.
- Whether adverse media gathered through interviews creates an investigative consumer report with additional FCRA duties.
- Privacy and data minimization for collecting DOB, address, and government ID, and retention limits.
- Whether the marketplace's use of results for eligibility decisions creates employment-like decision duties.
