# Issues and workstreams

- Whether the screening reports constitute 'consumer reports' under the FCRA, triggering permissible-purpose, disclosure/authorization, and adverse-action notice duties.
- Whether criminal-record and adverse-media data are subject to FCRA and state background-check restrictions (e.g., ban-the-box, adverse-action timing).
- Duty allocation: Mosaic Relay contracts with the vendor and provides the workflow; the marketplace is the FCRA 'user' making adverse decisions and owning adverse-action and dispute duties.
- Whether adverse media gathered through interviews creates an investigative consumer report with additional FCRA duties.
- Privacy and data minimization for collecting DOB, address, and government ID, and retention limits.
- Whether the marketplace's use of results for eligibility decisions creates employment-like decision duties.
