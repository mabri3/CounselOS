# Original Request

A Mosaic Relay customer operates a marketplace for home-service providers. The customer wants to use Mosaic Relay to screen sellers before enabling payouts. The proposed experience asks each seller for consent, legal name, date of birth, address, and government identification. Mosaic Relay would send the information to an identity and background-check vendor, display a status such as “cleared,” “review needed,” or “not eligible,” and allow the marketplace to make the final activation decision. The actors are sellers, the marketplace, Mosaic Relay, the screening vendor, and Mosaic Relay support staff. The customer wants this operation available in eight weeks.

The known business goal is to reduce fraud and protect marketplace customers. The requested check may include identity verification, sanctions screening, criminal-record information, and adverse media, but the exact package is unsettled. We do not know whether the marketplace will use the results for employment-like decisions, whether sellers are independent businesses or individuals, or which countries will be included. We also have not decided how long reports will be retained or how disputes and false matches will be handled. Please advise whether Mosaic Relay may provide this screening workflow, which notices and consents are required, what restrictions apply to criminal or adverse-media data, and how the parties should allocate compliance duties.
