# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 06 — Marketing Claim for Faster Settlement

## Chosen path

Do not launch or publish “instant settlement for every merchant.” Use only interim capability wording until substantiation and channel review are complete.

## Rationale

The proposed wording is an express, universal speed claim. Known timing varies and the current record has no universal service level, aggregate data, defined eligibility or exclusions, geographic scope, or settled meaning of settlement. A reasonable merchant could read settlement as external-bank receipt.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Product and Operations produce dated, segmented timing data before any speed claim.
- Product, Operations, Processor, and Compliance define eligibility, exclusions, and geography.
- Marketing uses the defined event and adjacent channel-appropriate disclosures.
- Paid-search speed claims remain paused until the qualification fits and platform rules are confirmed.

## Not decided

- Operational meaning of settlement.
- Geographic scope.
- Exact speed and measurement window.
- Paid-search network requirements.
