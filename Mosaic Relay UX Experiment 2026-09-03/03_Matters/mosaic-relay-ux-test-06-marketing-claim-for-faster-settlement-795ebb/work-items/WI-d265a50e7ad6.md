# Produce dated, segmented settlement/availability timing data

Product and Operations must produce dated, segmented records of actual settlement/availability timing to substantiate any speed claim. Currently only unverified examples of same-day or near-real-time availability exist for selected transactions. Needed: a defined measurement window, a denominator, and the percentage of transactions meeting any stated speed, segmented by payment method, risk review, bank processing, reserves, disputes, and merchant configuration.
