# Original Request

The marketing team wants to advertise Mosaic Relay as providing “instant settlement for every merchant.” The proposed campaign would appear on the website, in sales presentations, and in paid search ads beginning in four weeks. Product says the platform can make some funds available quickly through processor and payout controls, but actual timing depends on payment method, risk review, bank processing, reserves, disputes, and merchant configuration. The actors are Mosaic Relay marketing, sales, product, merchants, their customers, processors, acquiring banks, and payout recipients. The business goal is to increase qualified leads among growing marketplaces.

Known facts are that Mosaic Relay does not offer bank accounts or hold deposits. We have examples of same-day or near-real-time availability for selected transactions, but no universal service level. We have not defined the qualifying conditions, exclusions, geographic scope, or supporting evidence for the claim. We also have not decided whether “settlement” means processor settlement, availability in a Mosaic Relay-controlled workflow, or receipt in the merchant’s external bank account. Please review the claim, required qualifications and disclosures, substantiation standard, and acceptable replacement language for website, sales, and advertising use.
