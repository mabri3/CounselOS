# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to reduce merchant onboarding time for small online businesses. Product proposes a tiered flow: the business provides legal name, registration details, owners, expected volume, countries, and payment methods; Mosaic Relay runs KYB, identity verification, sanctions screening, and risk scoring; and low-risk applicants receive API credentials within minutes. Higher-risk or incomplete applications move to a manual review queue. The actors are the business applicant, beneficial owners, Mosaic Relay onboarding operations, identity and fraud vendors, acquiring partners, and payment processors. We want to launch this in six weeks before the holiday sales period.

Known facts are that Mosaic Relay provides payment infrastructure and does not hold deposits or operate as a bank. The product team expects to apply lower verification requirements to businesses below a proposed monthly volume threshold, but we have not confirmed whether that threshold is suitable for all payment methods or jurisdictions. We have not settled which beneficial-owner information must be collected, how often it must be refreshed, or whether a merchant may process limited transactions while review is pending. Please advise on the required onboarding checks, permissible tiering and transaction limits, recordkeeping, customer notices, and whether the proposed launch flow is legally acceptable.
