# Mosaic Relay UX Test — 01 — Tiered Merchant Onboarding for Small Businesses

## Chosen path

Do not launch the lower-verification tier until its safeguards are evidenced.

## Rationale

The working recommendation is followed because the threshold, scope, beneficial-owner data, pending-review treatment, partner acceptance, and primary authorities are not yet settled. The baseline memo supports a gated pilot only after these dependencies are closed.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Complete KYB/KYC and beneficial-owner/control-person checks required by law and partners
- Complete sanctions screening before credentials or transaction access, with ongoing rescreening
- Document a method- and jurisdiction-specific risk-based threshold
- Obtain written acquiring-partner and processor acceptance
- Choose pending-review processing; default to no transactions until review completes
- Confirm MSB/state licensing status and implement applicable AML, reporting, retention, and testing controls
- Publish clear customer and privacy notices

## Not decided

- Payment methods and jurisdictions in scope
- Monthly volume threshold and risk basis
- Pending-review processing and caps
- Beneficial-owner fields and refresh cadence
- Partner and processor acceptance
- Current primary-source authority verification
