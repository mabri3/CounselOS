# Original Request

Mosaic Relay wants to help marketplaces pay sellers and contractors by automatically assigning recipients to payout tiers. The proposed system would use identity-verification results, sanctions-screening status, transaction history, country, payout amount, and risk score to decide whether to release a payout immediately, require additional information, or send the payout to review. The recipient would see a status message and a request for documents when needed. The actors are sellers, contractors, marketplace customers, Mosaic Relay compliance and operations, identity and fraud vendors, and payout processors. Product wants to test this with three customers next quarter.

Known facts are that the system may include individuals and small businesses, and that some recipients may be outside the United States. The team has not determined whether a rejected payout is a compliance block, a fraud control, or a customer-directed decision. We have not settled the explanation shown to recipients, human-review standard, correction process, retention period, or treatment of funds during review. We also do not know which data fields will be used in the model or how often decisions will be re-evaluated. Please advise on legal requirements for automated screening and payout restrictions, notice and appeal duties, privacy and discrimination risks, human oversight, and the minimum controls needed for the pilot.
