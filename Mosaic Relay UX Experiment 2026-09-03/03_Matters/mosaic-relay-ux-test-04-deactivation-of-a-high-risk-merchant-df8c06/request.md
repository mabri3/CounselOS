# Original Request

Risk operations recommends deactivating a Mosaic Relay merchant after repeated sanctions-screening alerts, rapid volume growth, and transactions involving a newly identified high-risk country. The proposed operation immediately blocks new payments and payouts, preserves relevant records, and sends the merchant a short notice stating that access is suspended under the agreement. A later review would decide whether to terminate the relationship or restore service. The actors are the merchant, its beneficial owners, Mosaic Relay risk, compliance, support, and legal teams, plus processors and acquiring partners. Operations wants a decision within ten business days.

Known facts are that two alerts were false positives, one alert remains unresolved, and the merchant has pending customer refunds and seller payouts. The contract includes a general right to suspend service for risk or compliance concerns, but we have not confirmed whether it addresses pending funds, notice timing, or post-termination access. We also do not know whether filing or considering a suspicious-activity report limits what Mosaic Relay may tell the merchant. Please advise whether immediate suspension is justified, what communications are permitted, how to handle pending transactions and funds, what records must be preserved, and what process should govern reinstatement or termination.
