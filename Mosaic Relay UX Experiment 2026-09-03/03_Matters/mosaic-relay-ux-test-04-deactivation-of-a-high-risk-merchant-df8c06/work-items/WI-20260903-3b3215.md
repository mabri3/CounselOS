# # Orient to the request

## Business objective and timing

Risk Operations wants Mosaic Relay to suspend a merchant immediately by blocking new payments and payouts, preserving relevant records, and issuing a short contractual suspension notice. Operations requests a legal decision within ten business days (target 17 September 2026). A later review will determine whether to terminate the relationship or restore service.

## Known facts

Two sanctions-screening alerts were false positives. One alert is unresolved. The merchant has pending customer refunds and seller payouts. Mosaic Relay is a payment-infrastructure company, not a bank, and holds no deposits. The agreement includes a general suspension right for risk or compliance concerns.

## Material unknowns

We have not confirmed the unresolved alert, the country classification, or whether a suspicious-activity report was filed or is being considered. We have not confirmed contract terms for pending funds, notice timing, post-termination access, or the amounts and deadlines for refunds and payouts.

## Legal work to frame

Assess the contractual and regulatory basis for immediate suspension; define permitted merchant communications without disclosing protected SAR information; set a controlled process for refunds, payouts, and any reserve or hold; identify preservation requirements; and design a documented reinstatement-or-termination review.

## Assumptions for a first pass

This is a fictional test matter. I assume Mosaic Relay can control payment and payout flows under its processor and acquiring arrangements, will not confiscate funds, and can preserve records without contacting an external person through this workspace. I will label sources as verified, supplied, unverified leads, or assumptions.
