# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans to promote support for several local payment methods in Latin America and Europe. The proposed campaign would use the statements “accept local payments everywhere,” “lower-cost payments,” and “fewer failed transactions,” with country-specific landing pages and sales emails. Product expects to route transactions through local processors and show merchants a comparison of payment-method fees, refund support, and expected approval rates. The actors are Mosaic Relay marketing and sales, merchants, consumers, local processors, acquiring banks, payment networks, and customer-support teams. The launch target is the start of the next regional sales campaign in seven weeks.

Known facts are that support, fees, settlement timing, refund behavior, and consumer protections differ by payment method and country. Some methods may require local disclosures or restrictions, and some approval-rate data is based only on a small internal pilot. We have not confirmed the permitted wording in each jurisdiction, the evidence for cost and performance claims, whether fees include processor and foreign-exchange charges, or how the campaign will describe exclusions. Please advise on advertising and comparative-claim substantiation, country-specific disclosures, pricing transparency, consumer-protection issues, required approval or partner review, and exact language that legal can approve for the campaign.
