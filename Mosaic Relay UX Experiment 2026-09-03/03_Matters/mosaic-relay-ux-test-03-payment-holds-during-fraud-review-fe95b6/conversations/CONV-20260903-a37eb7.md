    ## Summary of the request

    Mosaic Relay''s Product team wants to add a payment-operations control that **temporarily
    holds a merchant''s card or ACH proceeds** when transaction-risk signals indicate
    possible fraud. The UX shows the merchant a banner that a review is in progress,
    prevents payout for the affected amount, and lets the merchant submit invoices
    or fulfillment evidence. Operations can release funds, extend the review, reverse
    the payment, or deactivate processing. Product wants an initial version live in
    **one quarter**.

    You''ve asked for legal advice on **lawful hold authority, timing limits, disclosure
    language, treatment of funds, appeal rights, and regulatory/network requirements**
    before approving the design.

    **What we already know (recorded as facts):** Mosaic Relay already calculates
    transaction risk scores and manages payout controls; the proposed UX mechanics;
    the operations actions; and the one-quarter target.

    **What''s still missing (the key unknowns):** the maximum hold period (and whether
    card vs. ACH differ), what the merchant agreement currently permits, whether held
    funds can cover chargebacks/returns/fees, what customer-facing notices are required,
    and whether a specific appeal process is required.

    **Initial legal workstreams I''ve mapped:** (1) lawful hold authority — contractual
    basis in the merchant agreement plus any regulatory basis; (2) timing limits for
    card vs. ACH; (3) treatment of held funds; (4) disclosure language and notice
    timing; (5) appeal rights; (6) card network and processor rules on holds, reversals,
    and deactivation; and (7) money-transmission/funds-flow implications of holding
    proceeds.

    I''ve recorded the intake and prioritized the first questions. The most decision-changing
    one first:

    **1. What is the proposed maximum hold period, and will it differ for card vs.
    ACH?** This drives the entire timing-limits analysis and the design.

    - Under 30 days / 30–60 days / Over 60 days / Not decided yet / Differs between
    card and ACH

    **2. What does the current merchant agreement permit regarding holds on proceeds?**
    This is the primary lawful basis for the control — if there''s no hold provision,
    the agreement must be amended before launch.

    - Express right to hold / General reserve clause / No hold provision / Unknown
    — need to check

    **3. May the held funds be used to cover chargebacks, returns, or fees?** This
    changes the treatment-of-funds analysis and network rules.

    - Yes, chargebacks only / Yes, all three / No, hold only / Not decided yet

    **4. Is a specific appeal process required for merchants to contest a hold?**

    - Yes, formal appeal / Evidence submission only / Not decided yet

    Answer these (or tell me which to skip) and I''ll reassess whether any material
    question remains before we move into the analysis.'
