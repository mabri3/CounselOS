# Original Request

Mosaic Relay proposes a consortium service with fraud and identity vendors. Participating merchants would allow Mosaic Relay to share selected transaction, device, identity, and dispute signals with vendors, who would return risk indicators that could improve approval and fraud detection for other participants. The proposed merchant dashboard would include an opt-in setting and a general explanation, while consumer data would be pseudonymized where possible. The actors are Mosaic Relay, merchants, consumers, fraud and identity vendors, processors, acquiring banks, and cloud providers. The business goal is to improve fraud detection before the next major product release.

Known facts are that the data may include device identifiers, IP addresses, payment tokens, names, addresses, dispute outcomes, and identity-verification results. We have not identified the lawful purpose for every field, whether merchants will be required to participate, whether vendors may combine the data with their own datasets, or how long shared signals will remain available. We also have not settled regional hosting, consumer access and deletion handling, vendor model training, or whether the service changes Mosaic Relay’s role under privacy law. Please advise on permissible data sharing, notices and consent, de-identification standards, contractual controls, cross-border transfers, retention, consumer rights, and whether the opt-in design is sufficient.
