Resolve whether Mosaic Relay determines the purposes and means of the shared-data processing (controller), acts on merchants' instructions (processor), or is a joint controller with merchants/vendors. This is foundational: it allocates notice, consent, de-identification, and contractual-control obligations across the consortium.
