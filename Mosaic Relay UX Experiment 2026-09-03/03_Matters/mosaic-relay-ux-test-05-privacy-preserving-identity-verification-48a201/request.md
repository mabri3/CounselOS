# Original Request

Mosaic Relay plans to replace its current identity-verification flow with a vendor that extracts data from identification documents and performs a facial comparison. The proposed user experience asks an individual to upload an identification document and take a live selfie, then displays only a pass/fail result to Mosaic Relay and the customer. The vendor would retain images for a proposed period, while Mosaic Relay would retain the result, audit metadata, and a limited data record. The actors are merchant applicants, beneficial owners, payout recipients, Mosaic Relay, the verification vendor, and cloud-storage providers. Product wants to begin implementation next month.

Known facts are that the data may include biometric information, government identifiers, and precise address details. We have not settled where the vendor will process the data, whether the customer or Mosaic Relay will determine the purpose of processing, or whether customers may reuse the result for their own compliance programs. Retention periods, deletion workflows, vendor model training, and handling of minors or failed matches are also unsettled. Please advise on the parties’ privacy roles, required notices and consent, biometric and cross-border requirements, data minimization, retention, vendor restrictions, and whether the proposed pass/fail interface creates any legal problem.
