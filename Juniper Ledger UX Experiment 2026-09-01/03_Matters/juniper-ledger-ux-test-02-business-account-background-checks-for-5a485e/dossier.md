# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Product launch of automated background checks for small-business operating accounts; legal needs to determine permissible data sources, required notices and dispute rights, human/sponsor-bank decision points, and pre-launch controls.

## Decision question

Juniper Ledger plans to offer faster approval for small-business operating accounts by running automated background checks on owners, controllers, and authorized users. The proposed flow would collect beneficial-owner information, retrieve identity and public-record data, screen sanctions and PEP lists, and assign a risk result. Low-risk businesses receive an account and virtual card within minutes; higher-risk cases go to compliance and, where required, the sponsor bank. Target launch is six months from now. Legal needs to decide which checks and data sources are acceptable, what notices and dispute rights are required, which decisions must remain with a human or the sponsor bank, and what controls are needed before launch.
