# Original Request

Juniper Ledger plans to offer faster approval for small-business operating accounts by running automated background checks on owners, controllers, and authorized users. The proposed flow would collect beneficial-owner information, retrieve identity and public-record data, screen sanctions and politically exposed person lists, and assign a risk result. Low-risk businesses would receive an account and virtual card within minutes. Higher-risk cases would go to Juniper Ledger’s compliance team and, where required, the sponsor bank. Product, compliance, the sponsor bank, applicants, and the screening vendor would participate. The target launch is six months from now.

Known facts include a 25% beneficial-ownership threshold and a requirement to support manual review. Product has not settled which records may be used, how criminal or civil records would affect eligibility, how discrepancies will be handled, or whether an owner may see and challenge the result. We also do not know the permissible use of information about non-applicant owners, the retention period, or the language for an account denial. Please decide which checks and data sources are legally acceptable, what notices and dispute rights are required, which decisions must remain with a human or the sponsor bank, and what controls are needed before launch.
