# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Product launch of automated background-check screening for small-business operating accounts. Key legal workstreams: FCRA/CRA vendor status, permissible data sources, ECOA/fair-lending on automated denials, ban-the-box on criminal records, adverse-action and dispute notices, human/sponsor-bank decisioning, and pre-launch controls.

## Decision question

Juniper Ledger plans to offer faster approval for small-business operating accounts by running automated background checks on owners, controllers, and authorized users. The flow would collect beneficial-owner information, retrieve identity and public-record data, screen sanctions and PEP lists, and assign a risk result. Low-risk businesses get an account and virtual card within minutes; higher-risk cases escalate to compliance and, where required, the sponsor bank (Cedar Harbor). Target launch is six months out. Legal needs to decide which checks/data sources are acceptable, what notices and dispute rights are required, which decisions must remain with a human or the sponsor bank, and what controls are needed before launch.
