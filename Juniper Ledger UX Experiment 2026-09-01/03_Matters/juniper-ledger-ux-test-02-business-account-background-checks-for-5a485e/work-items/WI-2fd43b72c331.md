# Review screening vendor contract to determine FCRA consumer-reporting-agency status

Determine whether the background-check/screening vendor is a consumer-reporting agency under the FCRA (i.e., whether it furnishes consumer reports that would be used to deny an account). This single fact drives whether FCRA adverse-action and dispute duties attach to every denial. Review the vendor contract and confirm with the vendor/legal.
