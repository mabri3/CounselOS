# Original Request

Juniper Ledger is considering a household feature that lets an adult account holder issue a virtual or physical card to a teenager. The proposed flow would require the adult to invite the teenager, set spending categories and limits, view transactions, and approve selected merchants. The teenager might use the card for purchases and receive refunds, but could not initiate ACH or account-to-account transfers. Juniper Ledger, the adult, the teenager, the sponsor bank, card networks, and support would participate. Product wants research-ready requirements for a limited beta next summer.

Known facts are that the adult is the primary account holder and that the teenager may be under 18. Product has not decided the minimum age, whether the teenager becomes a customer, how consent is collected, who receives privacy notices, or how disputes and unauthorized use will work. We also need to know whether marketing, transaction data, and behavioral analytics may include the teenager. Please advise on age and consent rules, account and cardholder status, privacy and marketing restrictions, dispute liability, required disclosures, parental controls, and the product decisions that must be made before user testing.
