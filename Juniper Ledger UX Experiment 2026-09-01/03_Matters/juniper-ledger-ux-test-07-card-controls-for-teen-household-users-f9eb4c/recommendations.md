# Recommendation

Proceed with a limited beta only after Cedar Harbor and the relevant card networks confirm the design. Use an authorized-user model: the adult remains the primary customer and funder, and the teen is a named restricted supplementary cardholder. Start at age 13+ (or an older sponsor-approved floor); do not include under-13 users unless Juniper Ledger implements COPPA-compliant verifiable parental consent and child-directed privacy controls.

Keep the beta debit-only. Prohibit ACH and account-to-account transfers, credit, overdraft, and teen-controlled cash-out. Give the adult category limits, periodic caps, merchant approval, pause/freeze, notifications, and transaction/refund visibility. Give the teen an age-appropriate explanation and only the access needed for card use.

Provide clear notices and agreements to the adult and, where required by the selected model, the teen or parent. Keep personalized marketing, sale/share, ad targeting, and behavioral analytics based on teen data off by default. Separate any optional consent from card access.

Before user testing, document age/state scope, customer and cardholder status, consent evidence, direct-notice recipients, data map and retention, dispute and unauthorized-use allocation under Reg E/network/account rules, sponsor-bank approval, support permissions, and refund/chargeback handling. This recommendation is provisional until those facts are confirmed.
