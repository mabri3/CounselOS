# Original Request

Juniper Ledger wants to reduce abandonment for freelancers opening consumer spending accounts. The proposed flow would collect identity details, tax identification information, occupation, expected monthly volume, and funding source. The app would use automated identity verification, sanctions screening, device-risk signals, and a small initial deposit. A customer could receive a virtual card after preliminary approval, while physical cards, ACH transfers, and higher limits would remain restricted until review. Juniper Ledger, its sponsor bank, the identity-verification provider, and the customer-support team would act in the flow. Product wants to launch a test with selected applicants next quarter.

Known facts are that freelancers may have irregular income and may use one account for personal and business expenses. Product has not decided whether to classify the account by intended use, whether to request proof of income, or when sponsor-bank review must occur. We also need to know the proposed transaction and balance limits, the required adverse-action notices, the data-retention period, and whether a failed verification can be appealed. Please advise on the permitted onboarding sequence, required disclosures and notices, account classification, review triggers, and the exact point at which Juniper Ledger may issue a card or enable ACH.
