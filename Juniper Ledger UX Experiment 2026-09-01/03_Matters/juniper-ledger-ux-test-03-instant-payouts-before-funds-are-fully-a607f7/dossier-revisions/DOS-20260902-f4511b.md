## Material facts

- The proposed service would let online merchants receive instant payouts from card-acceptance proceeds before the underlying transactions fully settle.
- The proposed flow would maintain a reserve, evaluate merchant history and fraud signals, and make funds available to a Juniper Ledger operating account or an external account.
- Juniper Ledger would later debit the reserve or account for chargebacks, refunds, reversals, and network adjustments.
- Merchants would see an estimated fee and an explanation of the reserve before confirming.
- The service would be optional for merchants.
- Juniper Ledger may suspend payouts when fraud risk increases.
- Product wants a pilot with established merchants this quarter.
- Participants include Juniper Ledger, the merchant, the sponsor bank (Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A.), card networks, and the payment processor.
