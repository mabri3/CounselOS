    ## Summary of the request

    The payments team wants to let **established online merchants** receive **instant
    payouts from card-acceptance proceeds before the underlying transactions fully
    settle**. The proposed flow would maintain a **reserve**, evaluate **merchant
    history and fraud signals**, and make funds available to a Juniper Ledger operating
    account or an external account. Juniper Ledger would later debit the reserve/account
    for chargebacks, refunds, reversals, and network adjustments. Merchants would
    see an **estimated fee and reserve explanation** before confirming. Participants
    include Juniper Ledger, the merchant, sponsor bank **Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A.**,
    card networks, and the payment processor. Product wants a **pilot with established
    merchants this quarter**.

    You''ve asked for advice on: (1) legal characterization, (2) required merchant
    agreement terms and disclosures, (3) permissible reserve and collection practices,
    (4) sponsor-bank approvals, and (5) the decisions Juniper Ledger must make before
    approving the pilot.

    I''ve recorded the reported facts, flagged the open issues, and set up two high-priority
    research work items (legal characterization, and sponsor-bank approvals). Before
    I can give you a useful first analysis, the single most decision-changing question
    is the **characterization of the reserve/advance**, because it determines the
    entire regulatory exposure (money transmission vs. lending/credit vs. UDAAP).

    ## Questions (in priority order)

    1. **How does Juniper Ledger intend to characterize the reserve/advance?**

    - A hold on unsettled card proceeds (no new credit extended)

    - A loan/advance funded by Juniper Ledger''s own capital

    - Not yet determined — that''s part of what we need advice on *(suggested)*

    2. **Who funds the instant payout before settlement?**

    - Juniper Ledger''s own capital

    - Sponsor bank (Cedar Harbor) funds

    - A third-party funder

    - Not yet determined *(suggested)*

    3. **How long should merchants remain liable after an instant payout** for chargebacks,
    refunds, reversals, and network adjustments?

    - Only until the underlying transactions settle

    - A fixed post-payout period (e.g., 90/120 days)

    - Indefinite until all chargeback windows close

    - Not yet determined *(suggested)*

    4. **How does Juniper Ledger intend to collect negative balances** if the reserve
    or account is insufficient?

    - Debit future payouts/balances

    - Debit the merchant''s linked operating account

    - Invoice the merchant for collection

    - Not yet determined *(suggested)*

    5. **What is the target pilot timing and scope this quarter** (launch date, number
    of merchants, caps on payout amounts)? *(free text)*

    Answer these together or one at a time — I''ll reassess after each.'
