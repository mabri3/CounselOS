# Original Request

The payments team wants to let online merchants receive instant payouts from card-acceptance proceeds before the underlying transactions fully settle. The proposed flow would maintain a reserve, evaluate merchant history and fraud signals, and make funds available to a Juniper Ledger operating account or an external account. Juniper Ledger would later debit the reserve or account for chargebacks, refunds, reversals, and network adjustments. Merchants would see an estimated fee and an explanation of the reserve before confirming. Juniper Ledger, the merchant, the sponsor bank, card networks, and the payment processor would be involved. Product wants a pilot with established merchants this quarter.

Known facts are that the service would be optional and that Juniper Ledger may suspend payouts when fraud risk increases. Product has not determined whether the reserve is a hold, a loan, or another form of advance; how long merchants may remain liable after payout; or how negative balances will be collected. It also has not defined the disclosure of fees, timing, risk allocation, or payout suspension criteria. Please advise on the legal characterization, required merchant agreement terms and disclosures, permissible reserve and collection practices, sponsor-bank approvals, and the decisions Juniper Ledger must make before approving the pilot.
