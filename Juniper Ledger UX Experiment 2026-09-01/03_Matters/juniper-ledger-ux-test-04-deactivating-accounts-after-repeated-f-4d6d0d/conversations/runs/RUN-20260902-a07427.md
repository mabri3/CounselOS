    ### 1. Lawful grounds and process per action

    | Action | Grounds | Process |

    |---|---|---|

    | **Temporary freeze** (card auth + outbound) | Reasonable suspicion of fraud,
    unauthorized activity, account takeover, synthetic identity, or suspicious transfers
    | Consumer (Reg E): permissible pending error resolution; investigate promptly,
    complete within 10 business days (extendable to 45 with provisional credit). Business

    honor valid, properly payable items subject to funds availability. |

    | **Limited inbound funds** | Preserve funds for return while blocking outbound
    | Allow payroll/refunds in; block outbound transfers and card auth. |

    | **Investigation** | — | Fraud investigator reviews within SLA; document basis.
    |

    | **Restore** | Suspicion cleared | Full access restored. |

    | **Impose limits** | Partial suspicion | Transaction/velocity limits. |

    | **Deactivate** | Sustained/confirmed fraud | Close account; return remaining
    funds. |

    ### 2. Risk-based trigger matrix (recommendation)

    | Alert type | Trigger to freeze |

    |---|---|

    | Unusual card purchases | 2+ high-severity alerts in 7 days, **or** 1 confirmed
    unauthorized |

    | Account takeover | 1 confirmed ATO signal (new device + credential change +
    unusual activity) — immediate freeze |

    | Synthetic identity | 1 confirmed synthetic indicator + failed verification —
    freeze + enhanced KYC |

    | Suspicious transfers | 2+ alerts in 7 days, **or** 1 high-value anomalous transfer
    |

    ### 3. Decision SLAs (recommendation)

    - **Freeze:** immediate on trigger.

    - **Investigation complete:** 10 business days (consumer, Reg E); 5 business days
    (business).

    - **Final restore/limit/deactivate:** 10 business days of freeze (consumer); 5
    business days (business).

    ### 4. Customer notices (investigation-safe)

    - Provide prompt notice of freeze in general terms without compromising the investigation.

    - **Never** disclose SAR filings or tipping-off indicators.

    - Approved language: *"We placed a temporary hold on your account while we review
    recent activity for your protection."*

    ### 5. Pending refunds and payroll deposits

    - **Allow inbound** payroll/refunds during freeze.

    - On deactivation, return remaining funds after satisfying outstanding obligations;
    hold pending refunds for resolution.

    ### 6. Funds return

    - Return remaining balance via ACH/wire to a verified external account after deactivation.

    - Document the return; comply with escheatment if unclaimed.

    ### 7. Negative balances and disputed transactions

    - **Negative balance:** do not return funds; pursue collection per agreement;
    disclose.

    - **Disputed transactions:** hold disputed amount pending resolution; follow Reg
    E error resolution (consumer) and card-network chargeback rules.

    ### 8. Reg E / UCC Art 4A / Reg CC / UDAAP

    - **Consumer:** Reg E error resolution, provisional credit, timely investigation.

    - **Business:** UCC Art 4A (wires), Reg CC (funds availability), agreement terms.

    - **UDAAP:** consistent, documented, non-discriminatory application; avoid unfair
    surprise.

    ### 9. SAR/AML and sponsor-bank escalation

    - File SAR per BSA where required; **do not tip off**.

    - Escalate to Cedar Harbor for sponsor-bank/regulatory decisions and SAR coordination.

    ### 10. Complaint handling

    - Log and track complaints; respond within regulatory timeframes; escalate to
    compliance.

    ### 11. Records and review

    - Retain freeze/deactivate records per retention schedule; periodic review of
    matrix and SLAs.

    ### 12. Juniper Ledger vs. Cedar Harbor decision boundary

    - **Juniper Ledger (first-line):** detect, freeze, investigate, recommend restore/limit/deactivate,
    communicate with customer, return funds.

    - **Cedar Harbor (sponsor bank):** final authority over account closure on the
    bank''s books, SAR filing, regulatory escalation, and any decision the agreement
    reserves to the bank.

    - **⚠ UNVERIFIED — confirm the agreement''s allocation before finalizing.**
