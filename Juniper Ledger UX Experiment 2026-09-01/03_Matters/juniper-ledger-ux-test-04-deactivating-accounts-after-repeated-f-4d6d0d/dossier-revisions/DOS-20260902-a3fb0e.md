## Matter summary

Both consumer and business accounts are in scope, so the process must satisfy Reg E (consumer) and UCC Art 4A / Reg CC (business) frameworks, plus the sponsor-bank agreement. No existing fraud policy exists. Target production Nov 1, 2026.

Target production Nov 1, 2026.
