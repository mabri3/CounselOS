# Draft account-deactivation policy for repeated fraud alerts

Draft a consistent, lawful account-deactivation policy covering: (1) lawful grounds and process for freeze, limit, and deactivate under Reg E (consumer) and UCC Art 4A / Reg CC (business); (2) required notices and timing by account type; (3) funds-return procedures including pending refunds, payroll deposits, and negative balances; (4) escalation obligations to compliance and sponsor bank; (5) complaint handling and disclosure limits that do not compromise an investigation; and (6) the boundary between Juniper Ledger's decision and Cedar Harbor's decision. Target production Nov 1, 2026.
