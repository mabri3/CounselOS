# Draft account-deactivation policy for repeated fraud alerts

Draft a consistent policy covering: (1) trigger threshold for the freeze flow, (2) decision timing after a freeze, (3) handling of pending refunds and payroll deposits, (4) handling of negative balance and disputed transactions, (5) required notices and timing for consumer (Reg E) vs business (UCC Art 4A / Reg CC) accounts, (6) funds-return procedures, (7) escalation obligations, and (8) complaint handling and disclosure limits that do not compromise an investigation. Coordinate with Compliance and Fraud Operations.
