# Review Cedar Harbor program-management agreement for freeze/deactivate authority allocation

Determine whether the Cedar Harbor Bank program-management agreement already allocates who decides to freeze or deactivate an account, or whether that boundary is open for Juniper Ledger to define. This is the key open fact that shapes the design of the account-deactivation process and the Juniper Ledger vs. sponsor-bank decision boundary.
