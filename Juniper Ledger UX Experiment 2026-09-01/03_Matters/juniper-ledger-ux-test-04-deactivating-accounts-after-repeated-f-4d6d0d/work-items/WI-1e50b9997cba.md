# Review Cedar Harbor program-management agreement for freeze/deactivate authority allocation

Determine whether the Cedar Harbor Bank program-management and banking-services agreement already allocates who decides to freeze or deactivate an account, or whether that allocation is open for Juniper Ledger to define in the new policy. This is central to defining the boundary between Juniper Ledger's decision and the sponsor bank's decision.
