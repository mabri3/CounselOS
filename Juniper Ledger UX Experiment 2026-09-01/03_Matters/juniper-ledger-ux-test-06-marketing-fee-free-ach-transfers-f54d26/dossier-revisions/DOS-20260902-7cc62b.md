# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Juniper Ledger, a fintech operating through sponsor bank Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A., wants to advertise "fee-free ACH transfers" to consumers and small businesses across paid ads, the app, email, and partner landing pages. The proposed flow would waive Juniper Ledger's fee on a set number of standard ACH transfers, while expedited transfers, returns, and certain external-bank actions may still carry charges, and network or third-party fees may still occur. Counsel is involved because the claim's net impression could be misleading, the fee schedule and eligibility rules are not final, and sponsor bank oversight obligations are unresolved. The campaign is scheduled to launch in eight weeks (target 2026-10-28).

## Decision question

Can Juniper Ledger launch the "fee-free ACH transfers" campaign by 2026-10-28 without Cedar Harbor Bank's written approval, or must the bank approve the marketing and fee structure first under the program-management agreement and bank oversight rules — and if approval is required but not obtained in time, should the campaign be delayed or narrowed to "No Juniper Ledger fee on standard ACH transfers"?

## Assumptions

- The claim is being evaluated under U.S. advertising law (FTC Act §5, state UDLAP) and applicable bank-partner/network rules.
- Juniper Ledger's fee-free claim refers only to its own fees, not network/third-party fees.
- The sponsor bank relationship may impose approval obligations for marketing of deposit/payment services.
- Sponsor bank relationship likely triggers approval obligations for marketing deposit/payment services, to be confirmed
- Juniper Ledger operates deposit/payment services through sponsor bank Cedar Harbor, so marketing those services likely triggers bank-partner approval obligations under the program-management agreement.
- The claim will require qualifiers (e.g., no Juniper Ledger fee on standard ACH; network/third-party fees and expedited/return charges may apply) regardless of the finalized fee schedule.
- Campaign will not launch until the fee schedule and qualifying conditions are finalized.
