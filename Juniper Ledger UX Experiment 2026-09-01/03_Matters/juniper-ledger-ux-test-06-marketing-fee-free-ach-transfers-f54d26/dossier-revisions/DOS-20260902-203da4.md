# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Marketing review of 'fee-free ACH transfers' claim; focus on claim supportability, required qualifiers, fee/timing presentation, consumer and business advertising rules, and sponsor bank approval.

## Decision question

Determine whether advertising 'fee-free ACH transfers' to consumers and small businesses is supportable, what qualifiers must appear with the claim, how fees and timing must be presented across paid ads/app/email/partner landing pages, which consumer and business advertising rules apply, and whether the sponsor bank must approve the campaign. Campaign target launch 2026-10-28.

## Material facts

- Juniper Ledger proposes to advertise 'fee-free ACH transfers' to consumers and small businesses.
- The proposed flow would permit a set number of inbound and outbound ACH transfers without a Juniper Ledger fee.
- Expedited transfers, returns, and certain external-bank actions might carry charges.
- Marketing wants to use the claim in paid ads, the app, email, and partner landing pages.
- Network fees and third-party fees may still occur even where Juniper Ledger charges no fee.
- Transfer availability depends on risk and settlement timing.
- The campaign is scheduled to begin in eight weeks (target launch 2026-10-28).
- Product has not finalized the fee schedule, qualifying conditions, geographic scope, disclosure placement, or treatment of returned and unauthorized transactions.
- Juniper Ledger has not tested whether a typical customer would understand 'fee-free' to exclude expedited or third-party charges.
- Which ACH transfer types will actually carry a fee, and what is the final fee schedule? This determines whether 'fee-free' can be supported and what qualifiers are needed. — Fee schedule not yet finalized
- The fee schedule is not yet finalized, which determines whether 'fee-free' can be supported and what qualifiers are needed.
- What conditions qualify a customer for the fee-free tier? — Qualifying conditions not yet finalized
- Qualifying conditions for the fee-free tier are not yet finalized.
- What is the geographic scope of the offer and the claim? — Geographic scope not yet finalized
- The ACH fee schedule is not yet finalized.
- Geographic scope of the offer and the claim is not yet finalized.

## Assumptions

- The claim is being evaluated under U.S. advertising law (FTC Act §5, state UDLAP) and applicable bank-partner/network rules.
- Juniper Ledger's fee-free claim refers only to its own fees, not network/third-party fees.
- The sponsor bank relationship may impose approval obligations for marketing of deposit/payment services.
- Sponsor bank relationship likely triggers approval obligations for marketing deposit/payment services, to be confirmed
