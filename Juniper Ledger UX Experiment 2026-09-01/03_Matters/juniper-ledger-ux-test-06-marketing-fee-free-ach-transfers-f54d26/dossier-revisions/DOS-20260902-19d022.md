# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Marketing review of a "fee-free ACH transfers" claim. Key open items are the fee schedule, qualifying conditions, geographic scope, sponsor bank approval, and consumer research. Initial workstreams: claim supportability, required qualifiers/disclosures, channel-specific presentation, applicable consumer/business advertising rules, and sponsor bank approval.

## Decision question

Determine whether advertising "fee-free ACH transfers" to consumers and small businesses is supportable, what qualifiers must appear with the claim, how fees and timing must be presented across paid ads/app/email/partner landing pages, which consumer and business advertising rules apply, and whether the sponsor bank must approve the campaign. Campaign launches 2026-10-28.
