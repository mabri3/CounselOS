# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Juniper Ledger, a fintech operating through sponsor bank Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A., wants to advertise “fee-free ACH transfers” to consumers and small businesses across paid ads, the app, email, and partner landing pages. The proposed flow would waive Juniper Ledger’s fee on a set number of standard inbound and outbound ACH transfers, while expedited transfers, returns, and certain external-bank actions may still carry charges, and network or third-party fees may still occur. Counsel is involved because the fee schedule, qualifying conditions, geographic scope, disclosure placement, and treatment of returned and unauthorized transactions are not final, and no consumer testing has been done on how “fee-free” is understood. The campaign is scheduled to launch in eight weeks (target 2026-10-28), and sponsor bank approval status is unknown.

## Decision question

Can Juniper Ledger launch the “fee-free ACH transfers” claim by 2026-10-28 across paid ads, the app, email, and partner landing pages, given that expedited transfers, returns, external-bank actions, network fees, and third-party fees may still apply, and if so, what qualifiers, disclosures, and sponsor bank approvals are required to avoid a deceptive net impression under FTC and state UDAP standards?
