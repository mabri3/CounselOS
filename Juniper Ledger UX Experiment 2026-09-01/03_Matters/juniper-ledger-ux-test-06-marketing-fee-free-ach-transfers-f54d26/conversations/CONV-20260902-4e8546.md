    ## Summary of the request

    partner landing pages. The proposed flow would make a set number of inbound/outbound
    ACH transfers free of Juniper Ledger fees, while expedited transfers, returns,
    and certain external-bank actions might still carry charges. The campaign launches
    in ~8 weeks (target 2026-10-28). You''ve asked us to determine whether the claim
    is supportable, what qualifiers must appear, how fees and timing must be presented,
    which consumer and business rules apply, and whether the sponsor bank must approve.

    I''ve recorded the known facts and flagged the open items. The core tension is
    that "fee-free" is only supportable if the claim is accurate and not misleading
    — and right now the fee schedule, qualifying conditions, and disclosure plan aren''t
    finalized, and no consumer research has tested how customers read "fee-free."

    ## What I need to move this forward

    The most decision-changing question is the **fee schedule itself** — whether "fee-free"
    can be supported at all depends on which transfer types actually carry a charge.
    I''ve prioritized the questions below in that order.

    what is the final fee schedule?

    - All ACH transfers are free of Juniper Ledger fees

    - Fee schedule not yet finalized *(suggested)*

    **2. Qualifying conditions** — What conditions qualify a customer for the fee-free
    tier?

    - Qualifying conditions not yet finalized *(suggested)*

    **3. Geographic scope** — What is the geographic scope of the offer and the claim?

    **5. Consumer research** — Has any consumer research been done on how customers
