# Original Request

Juniper Ledger wants to advertise “fee-free ACH transfers” to consumers and small businesses. The proposed flow would permit a set number of inbound and outbound ACH transfers without a Juniper Ledger fee, while expedited transfers, returns, and certain external-bank actions might carry charges. Marketing wants to use the claim in paid ads, the app, email, and partner landing pages. Product, marketing, support, Juniper Ledger, the sponsor bank, and ACH service providers would be involved. The campaign is scheduled to begin in eight weeks.

Known facts are that network fees and third-party fees may still occur and that transfer availability depends on risk and settlement timing. Product has not finalized the fee schedule, qualifying conditions, geographic scope, disclosure placement, or treatment of returned and unauthorized transactions. It also has not tested whether a typical customer would understand “fee-free” to exclude expedited or third-party charges. Please determine whether the claim is supportable, what qualifiers must appear with it, how fees and timing must be presented, which consumer and business rules apply, and whether the sponsor bank must approve the campaign.
