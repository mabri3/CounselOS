# Do not launch "fee-free ACH transfers" as currently conceived; use narrower claim with pre-launch conditions

## Chosen path

Do not launch the "fee-free ACH transfers" claim as currently conceived. Prefer the narrower claim "No Juniper Ledger fee on standard ACH transfers." Require, before any launch: (1) a final fee schedule identifying which transfer types carry charges, (2) finalized qualifying conditions, (3) finalized geographic scope, (4) finalized treatment of returned and unauthorized transactions, (5) clear channel-specific disclosures, (6) consumer testing of how customers understand the claim, and (7) written approval from Cedar Harbor Bank. This is a fictional local test decision, not external communication.

## Rationale

The "fee-free ACH transfers" phrase risks a misleading net impression because a typical customer may read it to mean no fee is paid by the customer, while expedited transfers, returns, external-bank actions, network fees, and third-party fees may still apply. The fee schedule, qualifying conditions, geographic scope, disclosure placement, and returned/unauthorized-transaction treatment are not finalized, no consumer research has tested customer understanding, and sponsor bank approval status is unknown. A narrower claim with complete disclosures and pre-launch conditions is the lower-risk path.

## Recommendation disposition

Followed

## Conditions

- Final fee schedule identifying which transfer types carry charges
- Finalized qualifying conditions for the fee-free tier
- Finalized geographic scope of the offer and claim
- Finalized treatment of returned and unauthorized transactions
- Clear channel-specific disclosures across paid ads, app, email, and partner landing pages
- Consumer testing of how customers understand the claim
- Written approval from Cedar Harbor Bank before launch
