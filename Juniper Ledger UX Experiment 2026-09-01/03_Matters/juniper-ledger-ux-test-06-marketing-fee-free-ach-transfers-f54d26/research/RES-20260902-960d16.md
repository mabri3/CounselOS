      Based on model-generated analysis, the controlling framework is FTC Act section
      5 and state unfair or deceptive acts and practices (UDAP) statutes. The FTC’s
      “free” guidance (16 C.F.R. part 251) requires that when an offer is conditioned
      on a purchase or other limitation, all material conditions must be disclosed
      at the outset and in close conjunction with the “free” claim. The disclosure
      must be clear and conspicuous; it cannot contradict the headline, appear only
      in fine print, or be buried behind a link or tooltip.

      The core issue is net impression. A typical consumer or small-business customer
      may read “fee-free ACH transfers” to mean that no fee will be paid by the customer
      for the transfer. Because expedited transfers, returned items, unauthorized-transaction
      handling, external-bank actions, network fees, and third-party fees may still
      apply, the unqualified claim risks being deceptive. The FTC and state attorneys
      general evaluate the overall impression created by the ad, not just the literal
      truth of the statement.

      For small businesses, the FTC Act can still apply, and state UDAP coverage varies.
      Some states extend UDAP protections to business transactions; others do not.
      Counsel should not assume that business recipients understand that “fee-free”
      excludes fees charged by another bank or network.

      Sponsor bank oversight is a separate issue. Even if the program-management agreement
      does not explicitly require marketing approval, Cedar Harbor may have prudential
      or contractual oversight duties for third-party marketing of deposit and payment
      services. Launching without written bank approval creates contractual and regulatory
      risk.

      “No Juniper Ledger fee on standard ACH transfers.” This claim is literally true

      (caps, eligibility, expedited/return/third-party fees, timing), but it avoids
      the strong consumer expectation that “fee-free” means zero cost of any kind.
