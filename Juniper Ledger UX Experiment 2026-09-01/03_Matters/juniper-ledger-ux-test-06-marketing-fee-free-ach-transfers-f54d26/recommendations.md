# Working recommendation

## Bottom line

Do not launch the phrase “fee-free ACH transfers” as currently designed. The fee schedule, qualifying conditions, geographic scope, disclosure placement, and returned/unauthorized-transaction treatment are not final. The phrase risks a misleading net impression because a typical customer may read it to mean that no fee will be paid by the customer, while expedited transfers, returns, external-bank actions, network fees, and third-party fees may still apply.

The lower-risk path is to use a narrower claim such as “No Juniper Ledger fee on standard ACH transfers,” subject to the same material limits. If Marketing keeps “fee-free,” launch only after the fee schedule and eligibility rules are fixed, the sponsor bank gives written approval, and consumer testing shows that the full presentation is understood.

## Required qualifiers and presentation

Every channel must state the scope next to the claim, in clear and conspicuous text. State the number of free inbound and outbound transfers, the measurement period, eligibility, and any plan, balance, geographic, or account limits. State that the offer covers standard ACH only if that is the actual product rule. State that expedited transfers, returned items, unauthorized-transaction handling, and specified external-bank actions may carry charges. State that network or third-party bank fees may still apply. State that availability and posting timing depend on risk review, settlement, cutoff times, and holds.

Do not put a material limit only behind a link, tooltip, or checkout screen. Paid ads and partner landing pages should place a short qualifier beside each prominent claim and link to full terms. In the app, show the fee and timing disclosure before the user selects a transfer type and again before confirmation. In email, put the main qualifier in the body near the headline, not only in a footer. Partner pages must use the same approved language and version control. Disclosures must not contradict the headline or use unreadable type.

## Rules and audiences

For consumers, evaluate the overall net impression under FTC Act section 5 and the FTC guidance on “free” claims, plus the state unfair/deceptive acts and practices laws in the final geographic scope. Material conditions must be disclosed at the outset and in close conjunction with the claim. For small businesses, the FTC Act can still apply, and state coverage varies. Do not assume business recipients understand that “fee-free” excludes fees charged by another bank. Use one substantiation and disclosure standard unless a documented channel or audience analysis supports a difference.

The final review must verify current primary sources, including 16 C.F.R. part 251, relevant FTC guidance or enforcement, and the applicable state rules. The current research packet is not verified authority.

## Sponsor bank approval

Obtain Cedar Harbor Bank’s written approval before any paid, in-app, email, or partner publication. The exact contractual requirement must be confirmed from the program-management and banking-services agreement. Even if the contract is silent, the bank may have oversight duties for third-party marketing of deposit and payment services. No campaign should go live while approval status is unknown.

## Conditions before launch

Product must provide the signed fee schedule, free-transfer caps and period, eligibility and geographic scope, fee treatment for returns and unauthorized transactions, external-bank fee treatment, and timing/availability rules. Marketing must provide final creative for all four channels. Counsel must verify the authorities and test the claim with typical consumers. If any material fact remains open by the launch gate, delay or use the narrower “No Juniper Ledger fee on standard ACH transfers” claim with complete disclosures.

## Assumptions and unverified leads

This assumes U.S. advertising law applies and that Juniper Ledger operates payment services through Cedar Harbor. The fee schedule, contract language, state scope, and consumer research are unverified. The current packet contains model-generated analysis and no retrieved external authority.
