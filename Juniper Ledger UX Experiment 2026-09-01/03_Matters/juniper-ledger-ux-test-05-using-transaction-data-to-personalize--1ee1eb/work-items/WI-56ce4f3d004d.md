# Resolve five open design decisions for personalized offers

Product must decide: (1) whether sensitive transaction categories (health, religion, political activity) are used for targeting or excluded; (2) whether the flow uses raw transaction descriptions or only derived categories/segments; (3) whether segments or other data are shared with third-party offer providers and at what detail; (4) how long personalization profiles remain active; (5) whether business-account users are treated differently from consumer users. These decisions drive the lawful-basis, sensitive-data, sharing, retention, and product-controls analysis.
