Preliminary legal analysis drafted covering lawful basis/notice, sensitive-data restrictions, sharing/retention, default settings, and product controls. Five design decisions remain open and will refine the recommendation.

## Decision question

Advise on lawful basis and notice, sensitive-data restrictions, sharing and retention limits, default settings, and exact product controls (consent, privacy notice, opt-out, deletion) for using Juniper Ledger transaction history to show personalized offers. First release planned next quarter; legal answer needed before launch.
