# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Privacy review of using Juniper Ledger transaction data to personalize offers. All five core design decisions (sensitive categories, raw vs. derived data, third-party sharing, retention, business-user treatment) remain undecided. The most decision-changing question is whether sensitive transaction categories will be used for targeting or excluded, as it determines whether opt-in consent and sensitive-data controls are required.

## Decision question

Marketing wants to use Juniper Ledger transaction history to show personalized offers for card-linked rewards, financial tools, and merchant discounts. The proposed flow analyzes purchase categories, recurring payments, account balances, and payment behavior, then displays offers in-app and by email. Customers could turn off personalized offers in settings, but the team prefers personalization on by default. Juniper Ledger would own the product and marketing experience; sponsor bank Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A. would provide oversight. First release planned for next quarter; legal answer needed before launch. Advise on lawful basis and notice requirements, sensitive-data restrictions, sharing and retention limits, default settings, and required product controls before launch.
