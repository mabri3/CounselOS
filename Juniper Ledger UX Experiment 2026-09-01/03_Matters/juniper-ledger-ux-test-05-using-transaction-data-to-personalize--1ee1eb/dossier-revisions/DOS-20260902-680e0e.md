# Matter dossier

## Matter summary

Advise on the lawful basis, notice requirements, sensitive-data restrictions, sharing and retention limits, default settings, and the exact product controls required before launching personalized offers (card-linked rewards, financial tools, merchant discounts) built on Juniper Ledger transaction history, with a first release planned for next quarter.

## Decision question

Advise on the lawful basis, notice requirements, sensitive-data restrictions, sharing and retention limits, default settings, and the exact product controls required before launching personalized offers (card-linked rewards, financial tools, merchant discounts) built on Juniper Ledger transaction history, with a first release planned for next quarter.

## Material facts

- Marketing wants to use Juniper Ledger transaction history to show personalized offers for card-linked rewards, financial tools, and merchant discounts.
- The proposed flow analyzes purchase categories, recurring payments, account balances, and payment behavior, then displays offers in-app and by email.
- Customers could turn off personalized offers in settings, but the team prefers personalization to be on by default.
- Juniper Ledger would own the product and marketing experience; the sponsor bank (Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A.) would provide oversight.
- The first release is planned for next quarter.
- Data comes from Juniper Ledger accounts.
- Some transactions may reveal health, religion, political activity, or other sensitive information.
- Product has not decided whether to use raw transaction descriptions, whether to share segments with offer providers, how long profiles remain active, or whether business-account users should be treated differently.
- The team lacks final language for consent, the privacy notice, opt-out controls, and deletion requests.
- Will any sensitive transaction categories (health, religion, political activity, etc.) be used for targeting, or will they be excluded entirely from personalization? — Not decided yet

## Assumptions

- Juniper Ledger is a U.S. fintech without a banking charter, relying on sponsor bank Cedar Harbor Bank, N.A. for deposit and payment services.
- Personalized offers involve processing of personal data subject to U.S. privacy law (e.g., state comprehensive privacy laws such as CCPA/CPRA) and potentially card-network rules.
- The matter is a privacy review; no specific state or jurisdiction scope has been set.

## Issues and workstreams

- Lawful basis and notice requirements for using transaction data for marketing/personalization
- Sensitive-data restrictions where transactions may reveal health, religion, political activity
- Sharing limits with third-party offer providers
- Retention limits for personalization profiles
- Default settings (opt-in vs. opt-out) and required product controls
- Consent, privacy notice, opt-out, and deletion language

## Open questions

- Whether raw transaction descriptions will be used or only derived categories/segments
- Whether segments or other data will be shared with third-party offer providers
- How long personalization profiles remain active (retention period)
- Whether business-account users are treated differently from consumer users
- Final consent, privacy notice, opt-out, and deletion language
- Whether the sponsor bank's oversight imposes additional requirements
- Which jurisdictions/customers are in scope (e.g., California CCPA/CPRA, other states)?
