# Original Request

Marketing wants to use Juniper Ledger transaction history to show personalized offers for card-linked rewards, financial tools, and merchant discounts. The proposed flow would analyze purchase categories, recurring payments, account balances, and payment behavior, then display offers in the app and by email. A customer could turn off personalized offers in settings, but the team prefers personalization to be on by default. Juniper Ledger would own the product and marketing experience; the sponsor bank would provide oversight. The first release is planned for next quarter.

Known facts are that the data comes from Juniper Ledger accounts and that some transactions may reveal health, religion, political activity, or other sensitive information. Product has not decided whether to use raw transaction descriptions, whether to share segments with offer providers, how long profiles remain active, or whether business-account users should be treated differently. The team also lacks final language for consent, the privacy notice, opt-out controls, and deletion requests. Please advise on the lawful basis and notice requirements, sensitive-data restrictions, sharing and retention limits, default settings, and the exact product controls required before launch.
